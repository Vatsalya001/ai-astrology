Gajakesari yoga is formed when Jupiter occupies a kendra — the first, fourth,
seventh or tenth house — counted from the Moon.

## The condition

The yoga is strictly geometric: Jupiter in the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from the Moon's
position, not from the ascendant. This is a common confusion and worth checking
before a reading claims the yoga is present at all.

Classical sources add strength conditions that are often dropped in modern
retellings: Jupiter should not be combust, retrograde in a hostile sign, or
debilitated, and the Moon should not be severely afflicted. A technically formed
yoga with an afflicted Jupiter is read as a weak one.

## What it is read as

The name means "elephant and lion" — the two animals classical texts use for
dignified strength. The yoga is traditionally associated with intelligence,
generosity, a good reputation, and support from people in a position to give it.

## The honest caveat

Gajakesari is extremely common. Jupiter falls in a kendra from the Moon in roughly a
third of charts, and that frequency alone should temper how much weight a reading
puts on it. It is best read as a supporting indication rather than a defining one —
and never as a guarantee of any outcome.

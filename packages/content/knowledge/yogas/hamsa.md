Hamsa yoga is the Jupiter member of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas, and is
generally rated the most favourable of them.

## The condition

**Jupiter** in Sagittarius or Pisces (its own signs) or Cancer (its exaltation),
**and** in a kendra — the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from the ascendant.

Both conditions. Jupiter exalted in the 9th is a strong placement and is not this yoga.

## What it is read as

The name means "swan". Classical texts read it as a principled, well-regarded native
with a natural teaching or advisory function — someone consulted rather than obeyed.
Associations include law, philosophy, scholarship, and a reputation for fairness.

Several sources describe physical markers, which modern readings should drop: they are
period detail, not interpretation.

## Why it is rated highest of the five

Jupiter is the natural benefic, so the yoga puts the most generous planet in its own
dignity in a house of action. The other four put a planet with mixed significations
into prominence; this one amplifies something the system already reads as supportive.

## The honest caveat

Jupiter spends a year in each sign, so Hamsa yoga is present in a predictable fraction
of charts born in particular years — it is a cohort effect as much as an individual
one. And a yoga that makes a native consultable does not make them consulted. The
classical descriptions overstate consistently; what survives scrutiny is the
disposition, not the outcome.

Amala yoga is formed when a natural benefic occupies the 10th house from the ascendant
or from the Moon.

## The condition

Jupiter, Venus, or an unafflicted Mercury or waxing Moon in the 10th from either the
ascendant or the Moon. Sources vary on whether Mercury and the Moon qualify
unconditionally.

## What it is read as

The name means "spotless". Classical texts read lasting reputation and work that is
well regarded — specifically, a native whose standing does not have to be defended.
Associations include charitable activity and a public role conducted cleanly.

The 10th house is visible standing, and a benefic there is read as that visibility
being favourable rather than merely present.

## The honest caveat

Counting from both the ascendant and the Moon roughly doubles the frequency, and with
four possible qualifying planets this configuration is common. The classical
description — unblemished reputation, lasting fame — is far stronger than the condition
warrants.

## How to use it

As a supporting indication in any question about career standing or reputation, read
alongside the 10th lord's own condition, which carries more weight. The defensible
statement is that the native's public conduct tends to be read generously, which is a
real and modest advantage.

The term "raja yoga" covers a family of configurations rather than one, and the core
definition is narrower than its popular use.

## The condition

A connection between the lord of a kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, 10th) and the lord of a
trine (1st, 5th, 9th). The connection may be:

- Conjunction
- Mutual aspect
- Mutual exchange — a Parivartana between the two houses
- One lord in the other's house

The 1st house is both a kendra and a trine, which is why the ascendant lord features
in so many of these.

## What it is read as

Kendras are houses of action and trines are houses of merit. A connection between
their lords is read as merit finding a vehicle — capacity and opportunity linked rather
than separate. Classical texts read achievement, position and recognition.

## The honest caveat

**These are common.** With four kendras and three trines, and four kinds of qualifying
connection, most charts contain at least one. The classical literature was written in
large part for royal courts, and the language of "raja yoga" reflects its audience
rather than a claim about every native who has one.

The strength of a raja yoga depends almost entirely on the dignity of the two lords
involved, on whether either is also a dusthana lord, and on how many such connections
exist. A reading that announces "you have a raja yoga" without any of that has said
nothing.

## How to use it

Identify the specific lords, their dignities, and the nature of the connection. The
useful statement names which area of merit is linked to which area of action.

Three related configurations, counted from the Moon, which together form the complement
of Kemadruma yoga.

## The conditions

Counted from the Moon, excluding the Sun:

- **Sunapha** — a planet in the 2nd from the Moon
- **Anapha** — a planet in the 12th from the Moon
- **Durudhura** — planets in both the 2nd and the 12th from the Moon

## What they are read as

All three are read as emotional and material support — the mind is flanked rather than
exposed. Durudhura, having both, is rated highest.

Classical texts associate Sunapha with accumulated wealth and self-earned standing,
Anapha with physical wellbeing and a generous disposition, and Durudhura with both
plus the capacity to spend without depletion.

## How they are actually useful

Which planets flank the Moon matters far more than the fact that some do. A Moon
flanked by Jupiter and Venus and a Moon flanked by Mars and Saturn both form
Durudhura, and the two charts do not read alike. The yoga name identifies a structural
feature; the planets supply the content.

## The honest caveat

Between these three and Kemadruma, nearly every chart carries one of the four — they
are exhaustive by construction. That makes them a classification rather than a
distinction, and a reading that announces Durudhura yoga as a special finding has
misunderstood what the set is for.

Use them as a quick read on whether the Moon is supported, then look at by what.

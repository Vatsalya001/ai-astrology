Sasa yoga is the Saturn member of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas, and the one whose
classical description is least flattering.

## The condition

**Saturn** in Capricorn or Aquarius (its own signs) or Libra (its exaltation), **and**
in a kendra — the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from the ascendant.

## What it is read as

Authority acquired through endurance. Classical texts read a native who attains
position by outlasting rather than by brilliance, with discipline, considerable
organisational capacity, and influence over subordinates or institutions.

Several sources add unflattering material — harshness, exploitation of others'
labour — which is unusual among the mahapurusha yogas and worth reporting rather than
omitting. The honest reading is that the yoga describes power held structurally, and
that the texts are warning about what that enables rather than predicting it.

## The honest caveat

Saturn spends about two and a half years in each sign, so Sasa yoga is strongly
cohort-linked: everyone born in a particular two-year window with the right ascendant
has it.

The negative classical material should be handled carefully in a reading. It describes
a risk inherent in durable authority, not a character verdict on the native, and
stating it as the latter is both unkind and unsupported.

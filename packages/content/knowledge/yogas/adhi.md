Adhi yoga is formed when benefics occupy the 6th, 7th and 8th houses from the Moon.

## The condition

Natural benefics — Jupiter, Venus, and unafflicted Mercury — in the 6th, 7th and 8th
counted from the Moon's position. Sources differ on whether all three houses must be
occupied or whether some subset suffices, which is a significant difference in
frequency.

## What it is read as

Classical texts rate this highly: leadership, good health, longevity, and defeat of
opposition. The reasoning is that the houses of difficulty counted from the mind are
occupied by planets that cannot be hostile to it, so the native's problems arrive
already softened.

## The honest caveat

The strict version — all three houses occupied by benefics — is genuinely rare, which
is what justifies the classical enthusiasm. The loose version, satisfied by one benefic
in one of the three houses, is common and does not support the same claims.

**Which version is being used should always be stated.** This is the single most
important thing to be clear about with Adhi yoga, because the two readings differ by
an order of magnitude in both frequency and significance.

## How to use it

Check whether all three houses qualify. If they do, note it as a genuine structural
advantage in handling opposition. If only one or two, treat it as a mild supporting
indication and say so.

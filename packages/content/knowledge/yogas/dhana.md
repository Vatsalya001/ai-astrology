Dhana yoga is a family of configurations read as supporting accumulation. The useful
ones share a structure: they link the houses of resource.

## The houses involved

- **2nd** — wealth retained
- **11th** — income received
- **5th and 9th** — trines, read as merit that makes resources available
- **1st** — the native's own capacity

## The common configurations

- The 2nd and 11th lords connected by conjunction, aspect or exchange
- The 2nd or 11th lord in a trine, or a trine lord in the 2nd or 11th
- The 1st lord connected to the 2nd or 11th lord
- Jupiter or Venus — natural significators of wealth — well placed in the 2nd or 11th
- The 9th lord in the 2nd, read as fortune becoming substance

## How to read them properly

The second-eleventh distinction does most of the work. Income without retention and
retention without income are different problems and the yoga family does not
distinguish them on its own. Check both lords separately before reading any
combination between them.

## The honest caveat

Dhana yogas are common, their classical descriptions are extravagant, and financial
outcome depends on circumstance, economy and choice to a degree no chart addresses.

A reading must not promise wealth, name an amount, or indicate timing for a financial
gain. What is defensible is structural: whether the chart suggests resources
consolidate easily or with difficulty, and where the native's earning capacity is
indicated. That is useful and it is the limit.

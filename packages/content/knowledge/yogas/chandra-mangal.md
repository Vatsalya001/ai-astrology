Chandra-Mangal yoga is formed when the Moon and Mars occupy the same house.

## The condition

Moon conjunct Mars. Some sources extend it to mutual aspect, which widens it
considerably and is worth stating if that version is being used.

## What it is read as

Emotional energy converted into action. The classical association is with earning
capacity specifically — several sources place it among the dhana (wealth) yogas — on
the reasoning that the Moon supplies motive and Mars supplies execution.

Texts also read it as a native who does not sit with a feeling for long before acting
on it, which is the same mechanism described from the inside.

## The honest caveat

The traditional literature is divided on this one, and the division is worth knowing.
Favourable sources read drive and earning; unfavourable sources read emotional
volatility, a harsh manner, and friction with the mother. Both readings follow from
the same combination — feeling given force — and the rest of the chart decides which
is more descriptive.

A reading that cites only the wealth interpretation is quoting selectively.

## How to use it

Check the dignity of both planets and the house. In an upachaya house — 3rd, 6th, 10th
or 11th — the energetic reading is stronger. In the 4th, the house of peace, the
friction reading usually is.

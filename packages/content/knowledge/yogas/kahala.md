Kahala yoga is formed when the lords of the 4th and 9th houses are in kendras from
each other, with the ascendant lord strong.

## The condition

The 4th lord and the 9th lord in mutual kendra relationship — 1/4/7/10 from each other
— **and** a strong ascendant lord. Some sources instead define it as the 4th and 9th
lords in mutual aspect with the lagna lord dignified.

## What it is read as

The name means "drum" or "trumpet". Classical texts read determination and the capacity
to be heard — a native who announces an intention and carries it out, with associated
material about land, vehicles and inherited advantage from the 4th-9th involvement.

The combination links foundation (4th) to fortune (9th), which is why it is read as
advantage that is both inherited and built upon.

## The honest caveat

The definitional variation matters here more than usual: the kendra version and the
aspect version select different charts, and sources do not agree. State which is being
used.

The classical descriptions also include a note that such natives can be aggressive or
overbearing, which is consistent with the drum image and is usually omitted in modern
summaries.

## How to use it

As a supporting indication for persistence and for advantage arising from family or
property. The ascendant-lord condition is the part most often ignored and is what the
yoga depends on — a Kahala configuration with a weak lagna lord is read as
determination without the constitution to sustain it.

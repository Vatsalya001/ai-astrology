Guru-Mangal yoga is formed when Jupiter and Mars are conjunct, or in mutual aspect in
some sources.

## The condition

Jupiter and Mars in the same house. The wider version admits mutual aspect, which
given Jupiter's 5-7-9 aspect and Mars's 4-7-8 aspect makes it considerably more
common.

## What it is read as

Principle with force behind it. Jupiter supplies the conviction and Mars the
willingness to act on it, and classical texts read a native who does not merely hold
positions but advances them — associated with law, with military or administrative
leadership, and with teaching that is practised rather than described.

Mars and Jupiter are natural friends, which is why this combination reads more cleanly
than most benefic-malefic conjunctions.

## The honest caveat

The traditional sources also note dogmatism: conviction plus force is a native
difficult to persuade, and the texts are frank that the combination can produce
righteousness that does not check itself.

Neither reading dominates. The house matters: in the 9th it is read as principled
authority, in the 3rd as effective self-direction, in the 7th as friction in
partnership with someone the native is trying to improve.

## How to use it

As an indication of how the native pursues what they believe, read with the house and
with both planets' dignity. It is one of the more genuinely informative conjunctions
and one of the less over-claimed.

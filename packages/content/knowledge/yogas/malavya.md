Malavya yoga is the Venus member of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas.

## The condition

**Venus** in Taurus or Libra (its own signs) or Pisces (its exaltation), **and** in a
kendra — the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from the ascendant.

## What it is read as

Classical texts read it as refinement — an eye for quality, comfortable circumstances,
and a capacity to make things and surroundings pleasant. Associations include the
arts, design, hospitality, and relationships that are satisfying rather than merely
present.

Venus in a kendra in its own dignity is read as appetite that is well-directed: the
native wants things worth wanting, which is the traditional distinction between this
and an afflicted Venus.

## The honest caveat

Venus stays within two signs of the Sun, so its sign is strongly constrained by the
time of year — which, as with Hamsa, makes this partly a cohort property.

The classical descriptions lean heavily on comfort and attractiveness, and a reading
should be careful with that. The durable content is aesthetic judgement and relational
capacity; the rest is period expectation about what a fortunate life looks like.

## How to use it

As support for any question about creative or relational capacity. Check the 7th house
separately rather than letting this yoga answer a marriage question on its own.

Ruchaka yoga is one of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas — the "five great person"
yogas, one for each of the non-luminary classical planets.

## The condition

**Mars** in its own sign (Aries or Scorpio) or its exaltation sign (Capricorn),
**and** in a kendra — the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th house from the ascendant.

Both conditions. A Mars exalted in the 5th house does not form it; neither does a Mars
in the 10th in a sign it does not rule.

## What it is read as

Physical strength, courage, and authority exercised directly. Classical texts describe
a commanding presence, success through personal effort, and an interest in land or in
disciplines requiring force. The name suggests "radiant" or "pleasing", which sits
oddly with Mars and is generally read as the attractiveness of competence.

## The other four

- **Bhadra** — Mercury in Gemini or Virgo, in a kendra
- **Hamsa** — Jupiter in Sagittarius, Pisces or Cancer, in a kendra
- **Malavya** — Venus in Taurus, Libra or Pisces, in a kendra
- **Sasa** — Saturn in Capricorn, Aquarius or Libra, in a kendra

## The honest caveat

These are genuinely uncommon — roughly one chart in ten carries any of the five — which
makes them worth noting when present. But the classical descriptions are extravagant,
written for a context in which charts were read for rulers, and they describe a
capacity rather than a station. A native with Ruchaka yoga is read as having the
quality; whether circumstance gave it anywhere to go is a different matter the chart
also speaks to.

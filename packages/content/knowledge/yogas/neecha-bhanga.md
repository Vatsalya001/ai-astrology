Neecha Bhanga is the cancellation of a debilitation — and under some conditions, the
conversion of it into a strength.

## The conditions

A debilitated planet's weakness is traditionally read as cancelled when any of these
hold:

- The lord of the debilitation sign is in a kendra from the ascendant or the Moon
- The planet that would be exalted in that sign is in a kendra
- The debilitated planet is conjunct or aspected by its dispositor
- The debilitated planet is in a kendra and its dispositor is strong
- The debilitated planet is exalted in the navamsa

Sources differ on the list and on how many conditions are needed. This is normal for
the material and should be stated rather than smoothed over.

## What it is read as

The "raja yoga" part of the name is the strong claim: that a cancelled debilitation
does not merely become neutral but becomes a source of unusual achievement. The
classical reasoning is that a native who had to overcome the weakness has developed
something a natively strong planet never needed to.

## The honest caveat

This is one of the most over-applied rules in modern practice. Debilitation is common,
the cancellation conditions are numerous and loosely specified, and between them
almost any debilitation can be argued into a Neecha Bhanga.

The responsible reading is narrow: check which specific condition applies, say which
one, and read the result as a weakness that is workable rather than as a promise of
eminence. A native told their debilitated Moon is secretly a royal yoga has been given
something that will not survive contact with their life.

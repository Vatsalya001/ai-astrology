Bhadra yoga is the Mercury member of the five Pancha Mahapurusha yogas.

## The condition

**Mercury** in Gemini or Virgo, **and** in a kendra — the 1st, 4th, 7th or 10th from
the ascendant.

Mercury rules Gemini and exalts in Virgo, so unlike the other four this yoga has only
two qualifying signs rather than three.

## What it is read as

Intelligence that is both quick and accurate. Classical texts read a native with strong
analytical and verbal faculties, good memory, and capability in commerce, scholarship
or any discipline requiring fine distinctions. The name means "auspicious" or
"gentle".

## The honest caveat

Mercury is never far from the Sun, which means this yoga requires the Sun to be in or
adjacent to Gemini or Virgo — so it clusters around particular birth months, and
combines with the frequency considerations in Budha-Aditya yoga.

Mercury also takes the character of its companions more than any other planet. A
Bhadra yoga where Mercury sits with Rahu reads differently from one where it sits with
Jupiter, and the yoga alone does not capture that. Check the conjunctions before
weighting it.

## How to use it

As support for intellectual capacity in any question about study, writing, trade or
analysis. It is a disposition rather than an achievement.

Saraswati yoga is formed when Jupiter, Venus and Mercury are placed in kendras, trines
or the 2nd house, with Jupiter in a dignified sign.

## The condition

All three of Jupiter, Venus and Mercury in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 5th, 7th, 9th or 10th
houses, **and** Jupiter in its own sign, exaltation, or a friendly sign.

Both parts. The Jupiter dignity requirement is what distinguishes this from a general
observation that the three benefics are reasonably placed.

## What it is read as

Learning, eloquence and artistic capacity — named for the goddess of knowledge and the
arts. Classical texts read a native accomplished in letters, music or scholarship,
with a reputation among people who know the field.

The combination is specific: Jupiter supplies breadth, Mercury precision, Venus
aesthetic judgement. All three well placed is read as the full equipment for
intellectual and creative work.

## The honest caveat

Requiring three planets in a restricted set of houses with a dignity condition makes
this genuinely uncommon — which is why it is worth noting when present.

But it describes equipment rather than output. A native with Saraswati yoga has the
faculties; whether they were trained, and whether circumstance offered anywhere to use
them, is not in the yoga. Classical descriptions of fame should be read as describing
what the capacity can reach, not what it will.

## How to use it

Strong supporting indication for any question about study, writing, teaching or
artistic work.

Shakata yoga is formed when Jupiter and the Moon are in the 6th, 8th or 12th from each
other.

## The condition

Jupiter and the Moon in mutual 6/8 or 2/12 relationship — sources differ on which
combinations count, with the 6/8 axis being the most widely cited.

## What it is read as

Classical texts read fluctuating fortunes: a native whose circumstances rise and fall
rather than accumulate. The name means "cart", and the usual gloss is a wheel turning —
what is up comes down.

The mechanism given is that Jupiter's expansion and the Moon's receptivity are placed
where they cannot support each other, so growth does not consolidate emotionally and
emotional stability does not attract growth.

## The honest caveat

Several things limit this yoga, and all of them should be said:

- It is cancelled in most sources when the Moon is in a kendra from the ascendant, or
  when either planet is strong by sign
- The qualifying relationships vary between sources, so two readers can disagree about
  whether it is present
- The classical description is bleak in a way that is not actionable

## How to use it

As a note on volatility rather than a finding about fortune. The defensible version is
that the native may find gains harder to consolidate than to achieve, which is worth
discussing because it is addressable. The fatalistic version should not be offered.

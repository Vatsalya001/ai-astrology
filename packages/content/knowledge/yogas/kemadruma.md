Kemadruma yoga is formed when no planet occupies the houses on either side of the Moon.

## The condition

The 2nd and 12th houses counted from the Moon are both empty of planets. The Sun is
excluded from consideration in most sources; Rahu and Ketu are counted by some and not
by others, which materially changes how often it occurs.

## What it is read as

Classical texts read isolation of the mind — a native whose emotional life has no
support around it, with associated material about poverty and difficulty that is
strongly worded.

## Why the strong version should not be repeated

Several considerations undercut it:

- The cancellations are extensive. Any planet in a kendra from the Moon or from the
  ascendant, a strong Moon in its own or exaltation sign, or the Moon in a kendra, are
  each held to cancel it. In practice most instances are cancelled.
- The counting rules vary. Whether the nodes are included changes the frequency
  substantially, and no source settles it.
- The classical descriptions were written about material circumstances in a context
  where they were far more determined than they are now.

## How to use it

As an indication worth checking rather than a finding worth reporting. If it is
present and uncancelled, the defensible statement is that the native's emotional
support is not structurally provided and has to be built — which is useful, actionable
and true of many people.

A reading must not tell a native their chart indicates poverty or isolation. That is
not a prediction the material supports and not a thing to say regardless.

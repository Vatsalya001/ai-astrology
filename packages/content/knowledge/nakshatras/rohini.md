Rohini spans 10°00' to 23°20' of Taurus. Its ruling planet is the Moon, its deity
Brahma, and its symbol a cart or chariot — the vehicle in which something is
carried to where it will grow.

## Qualities

Rohini is traditionally read as the nakshatra of cultivation: fertility, growth,
beauty, material increase, and the patience that each requires. The Moon is exalted
in Taurus and Rohini is the heart of that exaltation, which is why classical texts
treat it as among the most favourable of the twenty-seven for beginnings that are
meant to last.

Its shakti is described as the power to make things grow. The traditional caution is
attachment to what has grown.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya (human)
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: earth
- Direction: east

## In a reading

A Moon in Rohini is read as a steady emotional nature with a strong attachment to
comfort and to the tangible. Mercury or Venus here is traditionally associated with
work involving beauty, cultivation or material craft. Saturn here is read as
patience that becomes rigidity if unexamined.

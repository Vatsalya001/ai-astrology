Revati spans 16°40' to 30°00' of Pisces — the last nakshatra of the zodiac. Ruled by
Mercury, with Pushan — the shepherd of the paths — as its deity, and a fish or a drum
as its symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power to nourish, and Pushan's specific function is safe passage:
he guides travellers and brings herds home. Being last, Revati is read as completion
and as the point at which a journey is handed over.

The classical texts treat it as gentle and auspicious, particularly for journeys and
for endings that need to go well.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Sattva
- Element: ether
- Direction: east

## In a reading

A Moon in Revati is read as a kind, somewhat unworldly emotional nature with a strong
protective instinct toward anything vulnerable. Mercury here is associated with
teaching the young, with caretaking work, and with transport and travel in the
traditional sources.

The traditional caution is a difficulty with self-interest — the shepherd attends to
the herd — and a vulnerability that comes from assuming good faith. Revati's final
degrees are a gandanta junction, at the boundary between the last sign and the first,
and classical sources treat a birth there as a sensitive point rather than an
unfortunate one.

Pushya spans 3°20' to 16°40' of Cancer. Ruled by Saturn, with Brihaspati — the
teacher of the gods — as its deity, and a cow's udder as its symbol.

## Qualities

Pushya means "to nourish". Its shakti is the power to create spiritual energy, and the
classical texts rate it among the most auspicious of all twenty-seven nakshatras —
several sources call it the best for beginnings of a durable kind.

The combination is unusual and productive: Saturn's discipline, Brihaspati's wisdom,
and Cancer's nurturing. It is read as care that is reliable because it is dutiful
rather than merely felt.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: water
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Pushya is read as a steady, supportive emotional nature with a strong sense
of obligation to those it cares for. Jupiter or Saturn here is associated with
teaching, counselling, or institutional care.

The traditional caution is rigidity: nourishment given on schedule, in the form the
native thinks is correct, whether or not it is what was wanted. Pushya is also
traditionally considered inauspicious for marriage specifically, which is an exception
to its general favourability and worth checking against before relying on its
reputation.

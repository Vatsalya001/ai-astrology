Ashlesha spans 16°40' to 30°00' of Cancer. Ruled by Mercury, with the Nagas —
serpent deities — as its presiding power, and a coiled serpent as its symbol.

## Qualities

The name means "the embrace", and the classical image is deliberately ambiguous: a
coil can hold or constrict. Its shakti is described as the power to inflict poison,
which the texts also read as the power to penetrate — the same capacity directed
outward or inward.

Ashlesha is among the more difficult nakshatras in the traditional literature, and its
difficulty is specific: insight into what people conceal, held by a native who also
conceals.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Sattva
- Element: water
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Ashlesha is read as a perceptive emotional nature that does not disclose —
it sees motive and keeps the observation. Mercury here is associated with psychology,
investigation, and any discipline requiring understanding of what is not said.

The traditional caution is the use of that perception. The nakshatra is read as
capable of considerable kindness and considerable manipulation, and the classical
sources attach the distinction to the rest of the chart rather than to the nakshatra
itself.

Purva Phalguni spans 13°20' to 26°40' of Leo. Ruled by Venus, with Bhaga — the god
of fortune and enjoyment — as its deity, and the front legs of a bed as its symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power of procreation, and the classical reading is of creative
energy at rest — the bed is for recuperation as much as for anything else. Bhaga
presides over the share of good fortune a native is allotted.

The nakshatra is read as genuinely pleasant, which is less common in this system than
popular accounts suggest. Most nakshatras have a sharp edge; this one is mostly warm.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: water
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Purva Phalguni is read as a sociable, affectionate emotional nature that
needs comfort and gives it. Venus here is associated with the arts, with hospitality,
and with work that makes other people's lives more pleasant.

The traditional caution is indolence — the front legs of the bed are where one lies
down, and the texts note that a native with the capacity to rest may do so when the
situation calls for effort. It is also traditionally favourable for marriage, which is
worth knowing as a counterweight to the harsher nakshatras in Leo.

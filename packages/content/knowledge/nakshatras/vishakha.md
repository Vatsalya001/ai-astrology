Vishakha spans 20°00' of Libra to 3°20' of Scorpio. Ruled by Jupiter, with
Indra-Agni as its joint deity, and a triumphal arch as its symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power to achieve many fruits, and the classical reading is of
focused ambition that arrives. The arch is a gateway built to mark a victory — the
nakshatra is concerned with the achievement being recognised, not merely reached.

A joint deity is unusual, and the pairing of Indra (power) with Agni (transformation)
gives the nakshatra its drive.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: fire
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Vishakha is read as a determined emotional nature that fixes on an objective
and does not release it. Jupiter here is associated with achievement in teaching, law
or any field with formal recognition.

The traditional caution is single-mindedness that costs the native relationships and
perspective — the arch is built, and what it cost to build is not inscribed on it. Its
span across Libra and Scorpio shifts the ambition from the social to the intense,
which is one of the sharper transitions in the nakshatra sequence.

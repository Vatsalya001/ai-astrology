Jyeshtha spans 16°40' to 30°00' of Scorpio. Ruled by Mercury, with Indra as its
deity, and an earring or an umbrella as its symbol.

## Qualities

Jyeshtha means "the eldest". Its shakti is the power to rise and conquer, and the
classical reading is of seniority — authority held by precedence, with the burden that
goes with it. Indra is the king of the gods and also, in the Vedic literature, the one
who is frequently challenged.

The umbrella is a protective symbol and the earring a mark of rank; both point at the
same thing, which is position carrying responsibility.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Sattva
- Element: air
- Direction: west

## In a reading

A Moon in Jyeshtha is read as an emotional nature that takes charge and carries weight
it was not asked to carry. Mercury here is associated with administrative authority and
with protecting others through competence.

The traditional caution is isolation and resentment: the eldest is responsible and is
not thanked, and classical texts are specific that the nakshatra carries a sense of
burden. Jyeshtha is also one of the gandanta nakshatras at the end of a water sign,
traditionally considered a sensitive junction — material worth noting but not worth
alarming a native about.

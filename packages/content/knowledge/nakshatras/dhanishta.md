Dhanishta spans 23°20' of Capricorn to 6°40' of Aquarius. Ruled by Mars, with the
Vasus — deities of abundance — as its presiding power, and a drum as its symbol.

## Qualities

The name is connected to wealth, and its shakti is the power to bring together — to
accumulate. The drum is the more informative symbol: Dhanishta is read as rhythm,
timing, and the capacity to make others move together.

Mars ruling a nakshatra of abundance gives it a quality of effort directed at
accumulation rather than at conflict.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: ether
- Direction: east

## In a reading

A Moon in Dhanishta is read as an emotional nature with a strong sense of timing and a
need for things to be in order. Mars or Venus here is associated with music, with
performance, and with work involving coordination of people.

The traditional caution is materialism and a tendency to measure by accumulation. Its
span across Capricorn and Aquarius moves it from material wealth to social wealth,
which is one of the more useful distinctions in the later nakshatras — the same drum,
beaten for a different audience.

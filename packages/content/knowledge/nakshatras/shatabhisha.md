Shatabhisha spans 6°40' to 20°00' of Aquarius. Ruled by Rahu, with Varuna — the god
of cosmic waters and of oaths — as its deity, and an empty circle as its symbol.

## Qualities

The name means "a hundred healers" or "a hundred physicians". Its shakti is the power
to heal, and the classical reading pairs that with the empty circle: the boundary that
contains, which is what makes healing possible and isolation inevitable.

Varuna presides over oaths as well as waters, which gives the nakshatra a traditional
concern with what is binding.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: ether
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Shatabhisha is read as an emotional nature that keeps its own counsel and is
genuinely comfortable alone. Rahu's rulership adds an appetite for the unconventional,
and the nakshatra is traditionally associated with medicine, research and any
discipline practised somewhat apart.

The traditional caution is isolation that hardens into separation, and a difficulty
accepting help — a hundred healers, and the native is one of them rather than a
patient. Classical sources also connect it to astrology itself, which is worth noting
without making much of.

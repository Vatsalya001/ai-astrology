Uttara Phalguni spans 26°40' of Leo to 10°00' of Virgo. Ruled by the Sun, with
Aryaman — the god of patronage and hospitality — as its deity, and the back legs of a
bed as its symbol.

## Qualities

Where Purva Phalguni is rest, Uttara Phalguni is the support that makes rest possible.
Its shakti is the power to accumulate through union, and the classical association is
with partnership that is formalised — contracts, marriage, patronage.

Aryaman is specifically the deity of friendship as an obligation rather than an
affection, which gives the nakshatra its character: generosity that is dependable
because it is structural.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: fire
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Uttara Phalguni is read as an emotional nature that is generous within
commitments it has made. The Sun here is associated with positions of patronage —
roles in which the native's function is to support others' work.

It is traditionally considered among the more favourable nakshatras for marriage and
for formal agreements generally. The traditional caution is a kind of
over-responsibility: a native who honours the obligation past the point where it is
reciprocated. Its span crosses into Virgo, where the generosity becomes more practical
and more exacting.

Chitra spans 23°20' of Virgo to 6°40' of Libra. Ruled by Mars, with Tvashtar — the
celestial architect — as its deity, and a bright jewel as its symbol.

## Qualities

Chitra means "brilliant" or "variegated". Its shakti is the power to accumulate merit
through good works, and the classical association is with design: the making of
something whose form is the point. Tvashtar is the craftsman of the gods, and the
nakshatra carries his concern with how a thing looks as well as whether it works.

The jewel is also a warning in the texts — brilliance can be the thing or can be the
appearance of the thing, and Chitra is read as capable of both.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: fire
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Chitra is read as an emotional nature with a strong aesthetic sense and a
need to be impressive. Mars here is associated with design, architecture, surgery, and
any craft requiring both force and precision.

The traditional caution is the gap between appearance and substance — not as deceit,
but as a native who may invest in how the work presents and under-invest in what it
does. Its span across Virgo and Libra moves it from technical craft to aesthetic
judgement.

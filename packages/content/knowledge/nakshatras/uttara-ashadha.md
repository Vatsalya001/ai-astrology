Uttara Ashadha spans 26°40' of Sagittarius to 10°00' of Capricorn. Ruled by the Sun,
with the Vishwadevas — the collective gods — as its deity, and an elephant tusk as its
symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power of unchallengeable victory, and the classical texts rate it
among the most auspicious nakshatras for undertaking something that must last. Where
Purva Ashadha declares, Uttara Ashadha prevails.

The elephant tusk is the image: strength that does not need to be demonstrated. The
Vishwadevas being a collective rather than a single deity gives the nakshatra a
quality of acting with and for a group.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Sattva
- Element: air
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Uttara Ashadha is read as a steady emotional nature that carries
responsibility well and does not seek credit for it. The Sun here is associated with
leadership that is earned and durable — the classical reading is of authority that
outlasts its holder's enthusiasm for it.

The traditional caution is inflexibility and a slowness to begin: a nakshatra of
victory is a nakshatra that does not act until it expects to win. Its span crosses
into Capricorn, where the endurance becomes institutional.

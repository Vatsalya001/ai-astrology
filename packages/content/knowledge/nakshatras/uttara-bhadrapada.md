Uttara Bhadrapada spans 3°20' to 16°40' of Pisces. Ruled by Saturn, with Ahir
Budhnya — the serpent of the deep — as its deity, and the back legs of a funeral cot as
its symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power to bring rain, in the sense of bringing what is needed from
somewhere unseen. Where Purva Bhadrapada is the intensity, Uttara Bhadrapada is the
settling after it — the same structure as the two Phalgunis and the two Ashadhas.

Ahir Budhnya is a deep-water serpent, and the classical reading is of wisdom held
below the surface and brought up slowly.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: ether
- Direction: east

## In a reading

A Moon in Uttara Bhadrapada is read as a deep, patient emotional nature that is
difficult to disturb — considered one of the more stable Moon placements in Pisces,
which is otherwise a diffuse sign. Saturn here is associated with sustained
contemplative or scholarly work.

The traditional caution is withdrawal: a native who processes privately and at length,
and who may be assumed not to care because they have not said anything. Classical
sources rate the nakshatra favourably, and it is one of the better placements for
patience in a long undertaking.

Purva Ashadha spans 13°20' to 26°40' of Sagittarius. Ruled by Venus, with Apas —
the waters — as its deity, and a fan or a winnowing basket as its symbol.

## Qualities

Ashadha means "unconquered", and its shakti is the power of invigoration. The
classical reading is of conviction that does not yield — argument held with confidence
rather than with force.

The winnowing basket is the image that explains its method: separating what is useful
from what is not, by movement rather than by cutting. It is the gentler counterpart to
Krittika's razor.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: air
- Direction: east

## In a reading

A Moon in Purva Ashadha is read as an emotional nature with strong convictions and
considerable persuasive warmth. Venus or Jupiter here is associated with teaching,
advocacy, and public speaking.

The traditional caution is the "unconquered" quality turned inward: a native who
cannot be argued out of a position, including a mistaken one. Apas also gives the
nakshatra a traditional association with water — purification, bodies of water, and
in some sources maritime work.

Mrigashira spans 23°20' of Taurus to 6°40' of Gemini. Ruled by Mars, with Soma as
its deity and a deer's head as its symbol.

## Qualities

The name means "deer's head", and the classical reading follows the image: alert,
gentle, and permanently looking for something. Its shakti is described as the power to
find fulfilment, and the texts are consistent that the searching is more
characteristic than the finding.

Mars ruling a nakshatra of gentleness is the tension here. It is read as energy
directed at seeking rather than at conflict.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: earth
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Mrigashira is read as a curious and somewhat restless emotional nature —
easily interested, not easily satisfied. Mercury here is associated with research and
with travel undertaken to learn. The traditional caution is dissatisfaction that
attaches to whatever is currently held, which is the shadow of a nakshatra whose nature
is to keep looking.

Its span across Taurus and Gemini matters: in Taurus the search is for something
tangible, in Gemini for something to understand.

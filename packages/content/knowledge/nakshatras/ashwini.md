Ashwini spans 0°00' to 13°20' of Aries — the first nakshatra of the zodiac. Ruled by
Ketu, with the Ashwini Kumaras as its deity and a horse's head as its symbol.

## Qualities

The Ashwini Kumaras are the twin physicians of the Vedas, and the nakshatra carries
both of their attributes: speed and healing. Its shakti is described as the power to
reach things quickly, and classical texts associate it with medicine, transport, and
anything that has to arrive before it is too late.

Being first, it is read as initiation in its purest form — action taken with no
precedent to consult. The traditional caution is the other side of speed: starting
before the situation is understood.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva (divine)
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: earth
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Ashwini is read as a quick emotional nature that recovers fast and does not
dwell. Mercury or Mars here is traditionally associated with medicine, emergency work,
or any discipline where response time is the skill. Ketu's rulership gives the
nakshatra a quality of acting from instinct rather than from deliberation, which is
what makes it effective and what makes its mistakes characteristic.

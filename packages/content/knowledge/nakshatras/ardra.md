Ardra spans 6°40' to 20°00' of Gemini. Ruled by Rahu, with Rudra — the storm — as
its deity, and a teardrop or a diamond as its symbol.

## Qualities

Ardra means "moist" or "fresh", and its two symbols hold its whole meaning: the
teardrop is what the storm leaves, the diamond is what pressure makes. Its shakti is
described as the power of effort, and the classical reading is of clarity arriving
through disturbance rather than despite it.

Rudra is a fierce form, and the texts do not soften the nakshatra. It is read as
genuinely difficult and genuinely productive.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: water
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Ardra is read as an emotional nature that feels intensely and understands
what it feels — an unusual combination, and the source of both the nakshatra's
turbulence and its insight. Rahu's rulership gives it an appetite for the extreme.

Mercury here is associated with analytical work on difficult material. The traditional
caution is that the native may seek the storm, having learned that it produces
clarity — which is a real pattern and worth naming gently.

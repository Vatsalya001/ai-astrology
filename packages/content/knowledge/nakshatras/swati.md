Swati spans 6°40' to 20°00' of Libra. Ruled by Rahu, with Vayu — the wind — as its
deity, and a young shoot bending in the wind as its symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power to scatter like the wind, and the classical reading is of
independence: the shoot bends because it is not rigid, and survives because it bends.
Vayu moves everywhere and belongs nowhere.

The nakshatra is strongly associated with trade in the traditional literature — wind
carries goods — and with self-directed movement generally.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: fire
- Direction: west

## In a reading

A Moon in Swati is read as an emotional nature that requires independence and
negotiates rather than confronts. Mercury or Venus here is associated with commerce,
diplomacy, and brokerage of any kind.

The traditional caution is restlessness and a difficulty with commitment — the wind
does not settle. Rahu's rulership adds an appetite for what is elsewhere. Classical
texts also read Swati as self-made in the specific sense of a native whose position
owes little to inheritance, which pairs instructively against Magha.

Hasta spans 10°00' to 23°20' of Virgo. Ruled by the Moon, with Savitar — a solar
deity associated with vivification — as its presiding power, and an open hand as its
symbol.

## Qualities

Hasta means "hand", and the nakshatra is read almost entirely through that image:
skill, craft, dexterity, and the capacity to grasp. Its shakti is described as the
power to achieve what one sets out to do, and the texts are unusually direct about its
practicality.

The Moon ruling a nakshatra in Virgo gives it a quality the sign otherwise lacks:
warmth applied to detail.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: fire
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Hasta is read as a capable, dextrous emotional nature — a native who copes by
doing something with their hands. Mercury here is associated with crafts, with
instrumental skill, and with healing work of a hands-on kind.

Hasta is traditionally among the more auspicious nakshatras for beginning something
that requires craft. The traditional caution is acquisitiveness — the hand that grasps
does not always let go — and a tendency toward anxiety when the native has nothing to
work on.

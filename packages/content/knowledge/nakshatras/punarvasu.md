Punarvasu spans 20°00' of Gemini to 3°20' of Cancer. Ruled by Jupiter, with Aditi —
the mother of the gods — as its deity, and a quiver of arrows as its symbol.

## Qualities

The name means "good again" or "returning light". Its shakti is the power to return,
and the classical reading is of recovery: things lost come back, things that failed are
attempted again. Aditi's presence makes it a nakshatra of shelter and of boundless
accommodation.

The quiver is the key image — arrows that can be used again, which is what
distinguishes this nakshatra from those concerned with a single decisive action.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Sattva
- Element: water
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Punarvasu is read as an emotional nature that recovers and forgives, often
repeatedly. Jupiter here is associated with teaching and with philosophical breadth.
The nakshatra is traditionally considered among the more benign of the twenty-seven.

The traditional caution is the other face of recovery: a native who returns to
situations rather than leaving them, because returning is what this nakshatra does
well. Its span across Gemini and Cancer shifts it from intellectual renewal to
emotional renewal.

Magha spans 0°00' to 13°20' of Leo. Ruled by Ketu, with the Pitris — the ancestors —
as its deity, and a royal throne as its symbol.

## Qualities

Magha means "the mighty". Its shakti is the power to leave the body, and the classical
association is with inherited authority — position held because of who came before
rather than because of what the native did.

The throne is the image, and the texts are precise about it: Magha is the seat, not
the person sitting in it. The nakshatra is read as the authority that arrives with a
role.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: water
- Direction: west

## In a reading

A Moon in Magha is read as an emotional nature with a strong sense of lineage and of
what is owed to it. The Sun or Ketu here is associated with positions of traditional
authority — institutional, familial, ceremonial.

The traditional caution is entitlement: a native who expects the deference the role
carries without the standing it was meant to recognise. Material connected to the
Pitris also makes this the nakshatra most associated with obligation to family, which
is often the most practically useful thing to say about it.

Shravana spans 10°00' to 23°20' of Capricorn. Ruled by the Moon, with Vishnu as its
deity, and an ear — or three footprints — as its symbol.

## Qualities

Shravana means "hearing". Its shakti is the power to connect, and the classical
reading is of learning by listening: this is the nakshatra of received knowledge, of
the oral tradition, and of the student rather than the innovator.

Vishnu's three footprints are the alternate symbol, and they carry a related meaning —
the tradition traversed and preserved.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: air
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Shravana is read as a receptive emotional nature that absorbs what it is
told, including things nobody meant to tell it. Mercury or Jupiter here is associated
with scholarship, with counselling, and with any work whose material is other people's
accounts.

The traditional caution is credulity and a susceptibility to gossip — the ear does not
filter. Classical sources also associate Shravana with reputation specifically, on the
reasoning that what is heard about a native is itself a Shravana matter.

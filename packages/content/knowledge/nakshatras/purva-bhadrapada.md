Purva Bhadrapada spans 20°00' of Aquarius to 3°20' of Pisces. Ruled by Jupiter, with
Aja Ekapada — the one-footed goat — as its deity, and the front legs of a funeral cot
as its symbol.

## Qualities

Its shakti is the power to raise up. The symbolism is among the starkest in the
sequence, and the classical reading does not soften it: this is a nakshatra of
intensity, of asceticism, and of attention directed at endings.

Jupiter ruling it is what makes it survivable in the traditional reading — expansion
applied to severity produces conviction rather than collapse.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya
- Guna: Sattva
- Element: ether
- Direction: east

## In a reading

A Moon in Purva Bhadrapada is read as an emotional nature with considerable depth and
a tendency toward extremes of commitment. Jupiter here is associated with philosophy
of a severe kind, with asceticism, and with disciplines concerned with mortality at a
remove — medicine, ritual, scholarship.

The traditional caution is intensity that does not modulate. The funeral symbolism
invites statements about death and must not be read that way: the useful signification
is transformation and the willingness to let something end, which is a very different
thing to tell someone.

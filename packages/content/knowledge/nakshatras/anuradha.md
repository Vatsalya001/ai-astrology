Anuradha spans 3°20' to 16°40' of Scorpio. Ruled by Saturn, with Mitra — the god of
friendship and contracts — as its deity, and a lotus as its symbol.

## Qualities

The name means "following Radha", and its shakti is the power of worship. The
classical reading is devotion that is sustained — Saturn's durability applied to
affection, and Mitra's friendship understood as a bond with obligations.

A lotus in Scorpio is the image worth holding: something that grows out of depth
without being discoloured by it.

## Associations

- Gana: Deva
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: fire
- Direction: west

## In a reading

A Moon in Anuradha is read as a loyal emotional nature with considerable capacity for
sustained attachment. Saturn here is associated with organisational work, with
cooperative structures, and with devotional practice.

Anuradha is traditionally among the more favourable nakshatras in Scorpio, which is a
sign whose other nakshatras are demanding. The traditional caution is loyalty extended
past the point where it is returned, and a tendency to pursue what has already
declined — which is the shadow of a nakshatra whose nature is to follow.

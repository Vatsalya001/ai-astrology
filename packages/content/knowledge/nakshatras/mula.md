Mula spans 0°00' to 13°20' of Sagittarius. Ruled by Ketu, with Nirriti — a goddess
of dissolution — as its deity, and a bundle of tied roots as its symbol.

## Qualities

Mula means "root". Its shakti is the power to ruin, which the texts also read as the
power to get to the bottom of something: both meanings are about reaching the root,
and the difference is what happens when you do.

It is among the most demanding nakshatras in the classical literature, and the
demanding part is specific — it is read as a nakshatra of necessary upheaval, where
things are pulled up rather than pruned.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Tamas
- Element: air
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Mula is read as an emotional nature that goes to fundamentals and is
uncomfortable with anything superficial. Jupiter or Ketu here is associated with
philosophy, research into origins, and disciplines that require dismantling a
received account.

Mula is a gandanta nakshatra at the start of a fire sign, traditionally treated as a
sensitive junction, and some classical sources attach remedial practices to a birth in
its early degrees. That material should be mentioned, if at all, as tradition rather
than as a warning — the frequency alone makes alarm unjustifiable.

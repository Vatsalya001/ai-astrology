Bharani spans 13°20' to 26°40' of Aries. Ruled by Venus, with Yama — the god who
presides over the boundary between states — as its deity, and the yoni as its symbol.

## Qualities

Bharani means "the bearer". Its shakti is described as the power to take things away,
and the classical association is with transition: birth, ending, and the discipline
that boundaries impose. Yama's presence makes it the nakshatra of limits that are not
negotiable.

Venus ruling a nakshatra governed by Yama is the tension that defines it — appetite
under constraint — and classical texts read it as capacity for restraint rather than as
its absence.

## Associations

- Gana: Manushya (human)
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: earth
- Direction: south

## In a reading

A Moon in Bharani is read as an emotional nature that endures and carries, often more
than it says. Venus here is associated with creative or reproductive capacity under
discipline. The traditional caution is severity — a nakshatra of limits can produce a
native who applies them to others as readily as to themselves.

Material connected to Yama invites statements about mortality. It should not be read
that way: the useful signification is transition, not its most final form.

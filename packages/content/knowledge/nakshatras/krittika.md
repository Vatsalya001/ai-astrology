Krittika spans 26°40' of Aries to 10°00' of Taurus, crossing the sign boundary.
Ruled by the Sun, with Agni as its deity and a razor or a flame as its symbol.

## Qualities

Krittika's shakti is the power to burn away — to separate what is wanted from what is
not. Agni is the sacrificial fire, and the nakshatra carries its double meaning:
purification and consumption are the same process seen from different ends.

It is the nakshatra most directly associated with criticism in the classical scheme,
and the texts do not treat that as a flaw. Accurate cutting is read as a skill.

## Associations

- Gana: Rakshasa
- Guna: Rajas
- Element: earth
- Direction: north

## In a reading

A Moon in Krittika is read as a sharp emotional nature with a strong sense of what is
beneath standard. The Sun here is associated with leadership that corrects. Mars is
associated with cutting in the literal sense — surgery, metalwork, any craft of
separation.

The traditional caution is that the faculty does not switch off: a native who sees what
is wrong sees it everywhere, including where saying so costs them. The nakshatra's
span across two signs also means its character shifts — in Aries it is ignition, in
Taurus it is refinement.

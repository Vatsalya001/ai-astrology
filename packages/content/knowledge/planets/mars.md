Mars takes about two years to circle the zodiac and is retrograde for roughly two
months of every cycle. It is the planet of applied force — not anger, which is a
modern simplification, but the willingness to meet resistance directly.

## Significations

Energy, courage, conflict, younger siblings, land and property, surgery, machinery,
muscle and blood. It rules Aries and Scorpio, is exalted in Capricorn — the sign of
structured effort — and debilitated in Cancer, where force is read as least
appropriate to the terrain.

## Friendships

- Friends: the Sun, the Moon, Jupiter
- Enemies: Mercury
- Neutral: Venus, Saturn

The enmity with Mercury is the one worth explaining: Mercury signifies calculation and
Mars signifies acting before the calculation finishes. Classical texts read the
combination as cleverness used combatively rather than as a contradiction.

## Mangal Dosha

Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th from the ascendant is the condition
usually called Mangal Dosha or Kuja Dosha, traditionally read as friction in marriage.
Two things are worth stating plainly about it. The condition is **extremely common** —
roughly half of all charts by construction — and the classical texts list many
cancellations, including the same condition in the partner's chart. A reading that
treats it as a verdict is misreading both its frequency and its literature.

## In a reading

Mars's house is read as where the native expends effort and where they are willing to
be disliked. An unafflicted Mars is not read as a quiet person; it is read as someone
whose conflicts are about the thing rather than about themselves.

Rahu is not a body. It is the north point where the Moon's orbit crosses the ecliptic,
and it is exactly 180 degrees from Ketu by construction — a property worth checking in
any chart, because a Rahu-Ketu axis that is not an axis is a computation error.

It moves backwards through the zodiac, taking about 18 months per sign and 18 years
for a full cycle.

## Significations

Ambition without limit, obsession, foreignness, technology, deception, smoke and
poison, and the appetite that cannot be satisfied by getting what it wanted. Rahu owns
no sign and has no exaltation in the classical scheme, though many modern schools
assign it Taurus; it is read primarily through its dispositor and its house.

## How it is traditionally read

Rahu amplifies and distorts. The classical image is a head with no body: it consumes
without being nourished. A strong Rahu is read as worldly success that does not feel
like enough, which is a specific and recognisable condition rather than a moral
judgement.

It is also the significator of what is unfamiliar — foreign places, new disciplines,
people outside the native's inherited circle — and its house often indicates where a
life departs from the family pattern.

## In a reading

Rahu's house is read as where the native is insatiable, and its dispositor's condition
is read as whether that appetite is productive. Rahu's dasha is traditionally the
longest in the Vimshottari sequence at 18 years, which is why its placement deserves
more attention than its lack of rulership suggests.

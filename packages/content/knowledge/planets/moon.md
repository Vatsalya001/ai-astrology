The Moon moves roughly 13 degrees a day and completes the zodiac in about 27.3 days,
which is why it is the fastest-changing thing in a chart and why its nakshatra is the
anchor of the dasha system. Two people born the same day can have the Moon in
different signs.

## Significations

Mind, emotion, memory, the mother, water, the public, and the quality of receptivity
— what the native takes in rather than what they put out. It rules Cancer, is exalted
in Taurus and debilitated in Scorpio.

Its strength in the classical texts is measured by distance from the Sun rather than
by sign alone: a waxing Moon is read as benefic and a waning one as weak, which is a
property no other planet has.

## Why it carries the dasha system

The Vimshottari sequence begins from the Moon's nakshatra at birth, not from the Sun
or the ascendant. The traditional reasoning is that the mind is what experiences a
period, so the timing of experience is counted from the significator of mind. The
practical consequence is that **birth time matters enormously** — the Moon crosses a
nakshatra in about a day, and a few hours of error moves the entire dasha sequence.

## In a reading

The Moon's house is traditionally read as where the native's emotional attention
lives, regardless of where they say it does. A strong Moon badly placed by house is a
common and specific pattern: capable feeling, directed somewhere that does not return
it.

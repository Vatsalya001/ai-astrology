The Sun is the atmakaraka of the visible scheme — the significator of the self as it
is seen. It takes a year to circle the zodiac, spending roughly a month in each sign,
which makes it the planet whose position a reading can assume most people already know.

## Significations

Soul, vitality, father, authority, government, bone and heart, and the capacity to be
the person whose name is on the decision. It rules Leo, is exalted in Aries and
debilitated in Libra — the sign of accommodation, which the classical texts read as
the one place the Sun's nature is least useful to it.

The Sun is a malefic in the classical scheme, and the reason is heat rather than
malice: whatever it sits close to is said to be burned, which is the mechanism behind
combustion and why a planet within a few degrees of it is read as weakened.

## Friendships

- Friends: the Moon, Mars, Jupiter
- Enemies: Venus, Saturn
- Neutral: Mercury

The enmity with Venus and Saturn is informative. Venus signifies pleasure taken
without justification and Saturn signifies constraint that does not care who you are;
both are read as unsympathetic to a planet whose nature is to be central.

## In a reading

The Sun's house placement is traditionally read as where the native needs to be seen,
and its sign as the manner in which they expect that to happen. An afflicted Sun is
not read as a lack of ability — it is read as recognition arriving in a shape the
native did not want, which is a different conversation and a more useful one.

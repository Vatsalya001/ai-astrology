Ketu is the south lunar node, the point opposite Rahu where the Moon's orbit crosses
the ecliptic southward. It is exactly 180 degrees from Rahu — always, by definition —
and like Rahu it moves retrograde, 18 months to a sign.

## Significations

Detachment, liberation, loss, the past, isolation, and expertise arriving without
apparent effort. Ketu owns no sign in the classical scheme; some modern schools assign
it Scorpio. Like Rahu it is read through its house and its dispositor.

## How it is traditionally read

The classical image is a body with no head: it acts without appetite. Where Rahu wants
without being satisfied, Ketu is said to be capable without wanting — which is why its
house is traditionally associated with skill the native undervalues, and with areas of
life they abandon rather than fail at.

Texts associate Ketu with moksha, and the practical reading of that is narrower than
it sounds: the house holding Ketu is where the native is least attached to outcome,
which can be wisdom or can be neglect, and distinguishing the two is the work of the
reading rather than a rule.

## The axis

Rahu and Ketu are always a pair, and interpreting one without the other loses the
point. The axis describes a tension between what the native pursues and what they
already have and discount. A reading that names only one end of it describes half a
pattern.

## In a reading

Ketu's house is read as where effort produces diminishing returns and where competence
is taken for granted. Its dasha is 7 years, and is traditionally described as a period
of reduction — which classical sources treat as clearing rather than as loss, though
that framing deserves care in a reading rather than repetition.

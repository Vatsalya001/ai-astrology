Mercury never travels far from the Sun — at most about 28 degrees — so it is always in
the Sun's sign or an adjacent one, and it is combust more often than any other planet.
It is retrograde three or four times a year, for about three weeks each time.

## Significations

Intellect, speech, commerce, calculation, writing, nervous system, and the faculty of
making distinctions. It rules Gemini and Virgo, is exalted in Virgo — unusually, the
same sign — and debilitated in Pisces, where boundaries dissolve.

Mercury is described as neutral: it takes the character of whatever it sits with. With
Jupiter it is read as wisdom, with Mars as argument, with Saturn as rigour, with Rahu
as persuasion untethered from truth. This is why Mercury's conjunctions matter more
than its own dignity in many readings.

## Combustion

Because Mercury is rarely far from the Sun, combustion is close to its normal state
and classical texts treat it less severely here than for other planets. A Mercury
within a few degrees of the Sun is read as a mind serving the native's identity rather
than examining it — which is a quality, not a flaw, and is the shape of a persuasive
person.

## In a reading

Mercury's house is read as the subject matter the native thinks in, and its sign as
the register they speak in. A well-placed Mercury badly aspected is a specific and
common pattern: accurate perception, expressed in a way that costs the native the
argument.

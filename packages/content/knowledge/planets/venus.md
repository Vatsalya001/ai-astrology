Venus, like Mercury, stays within a bounded distance of the Sun — about 47 degrees —
so it is always within two signs of it. It completes the zodiac in roughly a year and
is retrograde for about six weeks every 18 months.

## Significations

Relationship, art, beauty, comfort, vehicles, jewellery, reproductive system, and the
capacity to find something worth having. It rules Taurus and Libra, is exalted in
Pisces and debilitated in Virgo, where discrimination is read as unsympathetic to
appetite.

Venus is a benefic, and the classical sense of that is the same as Jupiter's: it makes
things more attractive, including things that should not be. Texts are explicit that
Venus in difficult houses produces pleasant-looking versions of those difficulties.

## Friendships

- Friends: Mercury, Saturn
- Enemies: the Sun, the Moon
- Neutral: Mars, Jupiter

The friendship with Saturn is counterintuitive and worth understanding: both are read
as planets of long commitment, one through attachment and one through duty, and
classical texts treat the combination as durable rather than cold.

## In a reading

Venus's house is read as where the native seeks comfort and what they will compromise
for it. Its condition in a marriage question matters as much as the 7th house itself,
and for men the classical texts give it particular weight — though that asymmetry is
a feature of the literature's period rather than a rule worth applying without
comment.

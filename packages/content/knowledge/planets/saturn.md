Saturn is the slowest of the classical seven, taking roughly twenty-nine and a half
years to complete the zodiac. Everything traditionally attributed to it follows from
that: it is the planet of duration, and of whatever can only be earned by outlasting
the difficulty of earning it.

## Significations

Saturn is read as the significator of discipline, structure, labour, delay,
endurance, old age, and the consequences of past action. It rules Capricorn and
Aquarius, is exalted in Libra and debilitated in Aries.

The classical texts treat it as a malefic, which is easy to misread. Saturn is not
described as wishing harm; it is described as declining to grant anything
unearned. Placements involving it are traditionally read as slow rather than
unfortunate.

## Friendships

- Friends: Mercury, Venus
- Enemies: the Sun, the Moon, Mars
- Neutral: Jupiter

The enmity with the luminaries is the one worth understanding. The Sun and Moon
signify visibility and feeling; Saturn signifies the constraint that outlasts both.

## In a reading

Saturn's house placement is traditionally read as where the native is asked to
work without immediate reward, and its dasha as the period in which that work
either compounds or is abandoned. Nothing about this fixes an outcome — it
describes terrain.

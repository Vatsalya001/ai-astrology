Jupiter spends about a year in each sign and twelve in the zodiac, which makes its
transits the slowest that most people notice and its dasha the one that shapes a
decade. It is the great benefic of the classical scheme.

## Significations

Wisdom, teachers, law, religion and philosophy, children, wealth as distinct from
income, liver and fat, and the quality of expansion. It rules Sagittarius and Pisces,
is exalted in Cancer and debilitated in Capricorn — the sign of limit, where expansion
has least room.

## What "benefic" is traditionally said to mean

Not that Jupiter produces good outcomes, but that it produces MORE of whatever it
touches. The classical texts are specific that Jupiter in a house of loss expands the
loss, and that its aspect on an afflicted planet makes the affliction generous rather
than small. Reading Jupiter as a guarantee of good fortune is the commonest error in
modern interpretation of it.

## Friendships

- Friends: the Sun, the Moon, Mars
- Enemies: Mercury, Venus
- Neutral: Saturn

## In a reading

Jupiter's house is read as where the native is given more than they arranged for, and
where they are expected to teach whether or not they want to. Its aspect — Jupiter
aspects the 5th, 7th and 9th from itself — is traditionally the single most protective
influence in a chart, and checking what it falls on is worth doing before any
conclusion about difficulty.

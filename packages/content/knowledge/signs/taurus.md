Taurus spans 30° to 60° of the sidereal zodiac. Fixed (sthira), earthy, and ruled by
Venus.

## Qualities

- Element: earth
- Modality: fixed — sustains things
- Ruler: Venus
- Exaltation: the Moon · Debilitation: none of the classical seven
- Body part: the face and neck

## How it is read

Taurus is read as accumulation and persistence — the capacity to keep something going
long enough for it to become valuable. Its earth is cultivated rather than wild, which
is why Rohini, the nakshatra at its heart, is the nakshatra of growth.

The Moons exaltation here is the most consequential dignity in the sign: emotional
nature is read as strongest where it has something stable to rest on.

## A planet in Taurus

Slows down and becomes attached. A planet in Taurus is traditionally read as reliable
and difficult to redirect, which is a strength in anything requiring duration and a
liability where the situation has changed and the planet has not.

## As an ascendant

A Taurus ascendant makes Venus the chart lord. The classical reading is a solid
constitution, considerable patience, and a strong relationship to the tangible —
possessions, food, comfort, physical craft. The traditional caution is attachment to
what has been built, which is the same caution Rohini carries.

Pisces spans 330° to 360°, completing the zodiac. Dual-bodied (dwisvabhava), watery,
and ruled by Jupiter.

## Qualities

- Element: water
- Modality: dual
- Ruler: Jupiter
- Exaltation: Venus · Debilitation: Mercury
- Body part: the feet

## How it is read

Pisces is read as dissolution — the sign where distinctions stop holding. Its position
as the last sign is part of its meaning in the classical scheme: it is where the cycle
ends and nothing is any longer being built.

Venuss exaltation here is read as appetite without object, which the texts treat as
either devotion or dissipation depending on the rest of the chart. Mercurys
debilitation is the mirror: the faculty of distinction is weakest where boundaries
dissolve, which is why Pisces is read as unsuited to fine discrimination and well
suited to synthesis.

## A planet in Pisces

Becomes diffuse and compassionate. A planet here is traditionally read as acting
without insisting on its own boundaries, which is a strength in anything requiring
empathy or imagination and a liability wherever a clear limit is what the situation
needs.

## As an ascendant

A Pisces ascendant makes Jupiter the chart lord, as with Sagittarius, and classical
texts read it favourably for the same reason. The reading is a receptive constitution,
a strong imaginative faculty, and a temperament for which other peoples states are
difficult to keep separate from its own.

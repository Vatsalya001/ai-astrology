Virgo spans 150° to 180°. Dual-bodied (dwisvabhava), earthy, and ruled by Mercury,
which is also exalted here.

## Qualities

- Element: earth
- Modality: dual
- Ruler: Mercury
- Exaltation: Mercury · Debilitation: Venus
- Body part: the abdomen

## How it is read

Virgo is read as discrimination applied to material things — sorting, measuring,
correcting. Mercury ruling and exalting in the same sign is unusual in the classical
scheme and is read as intellect operating in its natural medium: the particular rather
than the general.

Venuss debilitation here is the instructive dignity. Appetite is read as least at home
where everything is being evaluated, because evaluation and enjoyment interfere with
each other.

## A planet in Virgo

Becomes precise and critical. A planet here is traditionally read as improving whatever
it touches and as hard to satisfy, which is a strength in anything requiring standards
and a liability in anything requiring acceptance.

## As an ascendant

A Virgo ascendant makes Mercury both chart lord and exalted, which classical texts read
favourably. The reading is a capable, service-oriented constitution and a temperament
that notices what is wrong — including about itself, which is the traditional caution
attached to this ascendant.

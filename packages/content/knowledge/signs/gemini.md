Gemini spans 60° to 90°. Dual-bodied (dwisvabhava), airy, and ruled by Mercury.

## Qualities

- Element: air
- Modality: dual — adapts
- Ruler: Mercury
- Exaltation: none · Debilitation: none of the classical seven
- Body part: the shoulders and arms

## How it is read

Gemini is read as the faculty of distinction — holding two things at once and seeing
how they differ. The classical symbol is a pair, and the texts are consistent that its
nature is plurality rather than indecision, which is the modern misreading.

Its dual modality is a real technical property, not a personality note: dual signs are
read as the register in which transitions happen, which is why they matter in timing
questions.

## A planet in Gemini

Becomes articulate and provisional. A planet here is traditionally read as able to
explain itself and reluctant to commit, which is useful in anything requiring
negotiation and costly in anything requiring a stance.

## As an ascendant

A Gemini ascendant makes Mercury the chart lord, and because Mercury takes the
character of its companions, such charts are read more through Mercurys conjunctions
than through its dignity. The classical reading is a quick constitution, a strong
verbal faculty, and a temperament that processes experience by describing it.

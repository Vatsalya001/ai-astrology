Sagittarius spans 240° to 270°. Dual-bodied (dwisvabhava), fiery, and ruled by Jupiter.

## Qualities

- Element: fire
- Modality: dual
- Ruler: Jupiter
- Exaltation: none · Debilitation: none of the classical seven
- Body part: the thighs

## How it is read

Sagittarius is read as fire directed at a principle. Its classical symbol is an archer,
and the texts emphasise the aim rather than the arrow: this is the sign of the
conclusion a native is working towards, which is why Jupiter — wisdom, law, teaching —
rules it.

Its dual modality gives it a quality the other fire signs lack: it is read as able to
change its target without changing its nature.

## A planet in Sagittarius

Becomes principled and expansive. A planet here is traditionally read as serving
something larger than the natives interest, which is a strength in teaching, law and
any long undertaking, and a liability where the particular case does not fit the
principle.

## As an ascendant

A Sagittarius ascendant makes Jupiter the chart lord, which classical texts read as one
of the more fortunate configurations — the chart lord is the natural great benefic. The
reading is an optimistic constitution, a strong relationship to learning, and a
temperament that needs to believe the work is for something.

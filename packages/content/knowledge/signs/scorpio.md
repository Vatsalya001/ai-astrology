Scorpio spans 210° to 240°. Fixed (sthira), watery, and ruled by Mars; some modern
schools assign Ketu co-rulership.

## Qualities

- Element: water
- Modality: fixed
- Ruler: Mars
- Exaltation: none · Debilitation: the Moon
- Body part: the pelvis

## How it is read

Scorpio is read as sustained depth — water that does not move on the surface. The
combination of a fixed modality with Marss rulership is the signs whole character:
force applied continuously rather than in bursts, which is why classical texts
associate it with research, investigation and secrecy.

The Moons debilitation here is the dignity that matters most. Emotional nature is read
as least comfortable in a sign that holds rather than releases, and a debilitated Moon
in Scorpio is one of the more significant placements a chart can carry.

## A planet in Scorpio

Becomes private and persistent. A planet here is traditionally read as operating
without announcing itself, which is a strength in anything requiring patience or
discretion and a liability where the situation needs the native to say what they want.

## As an ascendant

A Scorpio ascendant makes Mars the chart lord. The classical reading is considerable
resilience, a temperament that does not disclose easily, and an interest in what is
beneath the stated version of things.

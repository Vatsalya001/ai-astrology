Aquarius spans 300° to 330°. Fixed (sthira), airy, and ruled by Saturn; some modern
schools assign Rahu co-rulership.

## Qualities

- Element: air
- Modality: fixed
- Ruler: Saturn
- Exaltation: none · Debilitation: none of the classical seven
- Body part: the calves and ankles

## How it is read

Aquarius is read as structure applied to ideas rather than to things — Saturns other
sign, and the one where its discipline operates on abstractions. The classical symbol
is a water-bearer, a vessel that distributes rather than contains, and the texts read
it as concerned with the collective rather than the individual.

Saturn ruling both Capricorn and Aquarius is worth sitting with: the same planet
governs material limit and conceptual system, and the difference between the two signs
is what the discipline is applied to.

## A planet in Aquarius

Becomes systematic and impersonal. A planet here is traditionally read as acting on
principle rather than on preference, which is a strength in anything requiring
consistency across cases and a liability where the particular person in front of the
native is the point.

## As an ascendant

An Aquarius ascendant makes Saturn the chart lord. The classical reading is a
temperament that holds its positions for structural reasons rather than emotional ones,
considerable tolerance for being in a minority, and a relationship to groups that is
engaged without being warm.

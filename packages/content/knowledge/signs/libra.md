Libra spans 180° to 210°, beginning exactly opposite Aries. Movable (chara), airy, and
ruled by Venus.

## Qualities

- Element: air
- Modality: movable
- Ruler: Venus
- Exaltation: Saturn · Debilitation: the Sun
- Body part: the lower abdomen

## How it is read

Libra is read as weighing — the only sign whose symbol is an object rather than a
creature, and the classical texts make something of that. Its concern is proportion
between things rather than the things themselves.

Saturns exaltation here is one of the more illuminating dignities in the system:
constraint is read as strongest where it operates as fairness rather than as force.
The Suns debilitation is the mirror — identity is weakest in the sign that requires
accommodating another.

## A planet in Libra

Becomes relational and deliberate. A planet here is traditionally read as acting with
reference to someone else, which is a strength in anything requiring negotiation or
partnership and a liability where the situation requires a decision nobody will agree
with.

## As an ascendant

A Libra ascendant makes Venus the chart lord and places Saturn — exalted — in a strong
functional position. The classical reading is a temperament that finds imbalance
genuinely uncomfortable, which is both a social capacity and the source of its
difficulty.

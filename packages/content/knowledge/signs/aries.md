Aries is the first sign of the zodiac, 0° to 30° of the sidereal circle. Movable
(chara), fiery, and ruled by Mars.

## Qualities

- Element: fire
- Modality: movable — begins things
- Ruler: Mars
- Exaltation: the Sun · Debilitation: Saturn
- Gender in the classical scheme: masculine
- Body part: the head

## How it is read

Aries is read as initiation without precedent — the willingness to act where there is
no established way to. Its fire is the fire of ignition rather than of sustained heat,
which is why classical texts pair it with beginnings and are reserved about its
follow-through.

The Suns exaltation here is informative: identity is strongest in the sign with no
inheritance, where the native must be the first authority rather than the next one.
Saturns debilitation is the mirror of it — structure has nothing to build on.

## A planet in Aries

Takes on directness, speed, and a reluctance to consult. A planet in Aries is
traditionally read as acting from its own nature rather than from circumstance, which
is a strength in a house that rewards initiative and a liability in one that rewards
patience.

## As an ascendant

An Aries ascendant makes Mars the chart lord, which puts considerable weight on Marss
own condition. The classical reading is a constitution that recovers quickly and spends
freely, and a temperament that meets resistance head-on by default.

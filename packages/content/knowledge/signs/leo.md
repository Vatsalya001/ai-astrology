Leo spans 120° to 150°. Fixed (sthira), fiery, and ruled by the Sun — the only sign the
Sun rules.

## Qualities

- Element: fire
- Modality: fixed
- Ruler: the Sun
- Exaltation: none · Debilitation: none of the classical seven
- Body part: the heart and stomach

## How it is read

Leo is read as sustained authority — the fire of a steady flame rather than of
ignition. Where Aries initiates, Leo maintains, and the classical texts treat it as the
sign most concerned with whether the natives position is acknowledged.

That it has no exaltation or debilitation among the seven is worth noting: Leo is read
almost entirely through the Suns own condition, which makes solar dignity and
combustion matter more in Leo charts than in most.

## A planet in Leo

Becomes visible and proud. A planet here is traditionally read as unwilling to operate
anonymously, which is a strength where recognition is the mechanism — leadership,
performance, teaching — and a liability where the work requires not being credited.

## As an ascendant

A Leo ascendant makes the Sun the chart lord. The classical reading is a strong
constitution, considerable natural authority, and a temperament for which being
overlooked is a specific and real difficulty rather than a vanity.

Cancer spans 90° to 120°. Movable (chara), watery, and ruled by the Moon.

## Qualities

- Element: water
- Modality: movable
- Ruler: the Moon
- Exaltation: Jupiter · Debilitation: Mars
- Body part: the chest

## How it is read

Cancer is read as receptivity with a boundary — the capacity to take something in and
hold it. The classical symbol is a crab, and the texts emphasise the shell as much as
the softness: this is not an undefended sign, it is a defended one.

Jupiters exaltation here is the signs most important dignity. Expansion is read as
strongest where there is something to nourish, which is why Cancer is considered among
the most auspicious signs for a benefic.

Marss debilitation is its mirror: force is read as least appropriate where the terrain
is yielding, because there is nothing for it to push against.

## A planet in Cancer

Becomes protective and indirect. A planet here is traditionally read as acting through
care rather than through assertion, which is a strength in anything relational and a
liability where directness is what the situation requires.

## As an ascendant

A Cancer ascendant makes the Moon the chart lord, which means the Moons phase matters
unusually much — a waning Moon chart lord is a real consideration. The classical reading
is a sensitive constitution and a temperament that remembers.

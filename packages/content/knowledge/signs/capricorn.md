Capricorn spans 270° to 300°. Movable (chara), earthy, and ruled by Saturn.

## Qualities

- Element: earth
- Modality: movable
- Ruler: Saturn
- Exaltation: Mars · Debilitation: Jupiter
- Body part: the knees

## How it is read

Capricorn is read as structure built deliberately. The combination of a movable
modality with Saturns rulership is the interesting part: this is a sign that
initiates, slowly, and the classical texts treat it as the most durable of the movable
signs for exactly that reason.

Marss exaltation here is the systems clearest statement about force: it is strongest
where it is organised. Jupiters debilitation is the mirror — expansion has least room
in the sign of limit.

## A planet in Capricorn

Becomes disciplined and patient. A planet here is traditionally read as deferring its
own satisfaction, which is a strength in anything cumulative and a liability where the
opportunity will not wait.

## As an ascendant

A Capricorn ascendant makes Saturn the chart lord, which puts the slowest planet in
charge of the charts timing. The classical reading is a constitution that strengthens
with age and a temperament that is unimpressed by what has not yet been earned —
including its own achievements, which is the traditional caution.

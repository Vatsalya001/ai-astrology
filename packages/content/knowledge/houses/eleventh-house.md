The eleventh house is labha bhava, the house of gains. It governs income — what comes
in — as distinct from the second house, which governs what is kept. Any financial
reading needs both.

## What it governs

Income, profit, elder siblings, friends and networks, the fulfilment of desires,
large organisations, and the shins.

It is an upachaya house, so malefics here are traditionally read as strengthening:
Saturn in the eleventh is considered one of its better placements, read as income that
accumulates slowly and does not reverse.

## The second-eleventh pair

- Strong eleventh, weak second: money arrives and does not stay
- Strong second, weak eleventh: resources consolidate but do not grow
- Both strong: the classical reading of durable wealth
- Both weak: read as structure rather than as quantity — see the second house

This pairing does more work in a practical financial reading than any single
indication, and checking both is the first step rather than a refinement.

## Desires fulfilled

The elevenths traditional association with the fulfilment of desires is more
double-edged in the classical texts than modern readings suggest. Several sources note
that it grants what is wanted without reference to whether it was worth wanting, and
pair it with Rahu for exactly that reason.

## A caution

The eleventh describes networks as a resource, and a reading should be careful with
that: a strong eleventh is not a statement that the natives friendships are good, only
that they are consequential.

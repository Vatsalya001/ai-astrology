The second house is dhana bhava, the house of accumulated resources. It is about what
the native holds rather than what they earn — the eleventh governs income, and the
distinction matters in any financial reading.

## What it governs

Wealth already acquired, savings, food, speech and the voice, the face, and the
immediate family. The pairing of wealth with speech is not arbitrary in the classical
scheme: both are read as things the native has accumulated and dispenses.

## How it is read

The second lord is read for capacity to retain, and its relationship to the eleventh
lord is read for whether income becomes wealth. A strong eleventh with a weak second
is a recognisable pattern — money arrives and does not stay.

Planets here affect speech directly in the traditional reading. Saturn is associated
with measured, slow speech; Mercury with fluency; Mars with bluntness; Rahu with
persuasion that outruns accuracy.

## Maraka

The second is one of the houses classical texts associate with longevity questions,
through a technical rule about lords of certain houses. That material exists and is
deliberately not developed here: a reading must never produce a statement about death,
and the rules that invite one are the rules most often misapplied.

## A caution

A weak second house is not read as poverty. It is read as resources that do not
consolidate, which has more to do with structure than with quantity.

The sixth house is a dusthana — one of the three difficult houses, with the eighth and
twelfth — and simultaneously an upachaya house where malefics strengthen. That
apparent contradiction is the key to reading it.

## What it governs

Illness, enemies and opposition, debt, litigation, service and employment as distinct
from vocation, daily discipline, and the intestines.

## Why malefics do well in a difficult house

The sixth signifies obstacles, and a strong planet here is read as strength against
them rather than as strength of them. Saturn in the sixth is a classical example: read
as endurance in the face of opposition, and traditionally considered one of the better
places for it.

A benefic here is read less favourably — Jupiter in the sixth expands what the house
signifies, which is not a gift.

## How it is read

- The sixth lords condition for the natives relationship with their own discipline
- Its relationship to the eighth and twelfth, the other dusthanas, for health questions
- Occupants for the arena in which the native meets resistance

## On health

An astrological indication is not a diagnosis and must never be offered as one. The
sixth house describes a pattern of vulnerability in traditional terms; it does not
identify a condition, predict an illness, or substitute for a physician. A reading that
names a disease has left astrology and entered medicine without a licence.

## A caution

A strong sixth with a weak first is a recognisable pattern: a native who manages
opposition well and manages their own constitution poorly.

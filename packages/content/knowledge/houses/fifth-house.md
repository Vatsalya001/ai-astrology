The fifth house is a trine — a dharma house, with the first and ninth — and is
traditionally read as the most auspicious of the twelve after the ninth. It governs
what the native produces.

## What it governs

Children, creativity, intelligence as distinct from intellect, romance, speculation
and games of chance, the stomach, and merit carried from previous action.

The pairing of children with creative work and with speculation is consistent in the
classical scheme: all three are read as things the native brings into being and then
cannot fully control.

## How it is read

- The fifth lord is a strong benefic by function regardless of its natural character,
  because it rules a trine
- Jupiters relationship to this house matters particularly in questions about
  children, as Jupiter is the significator
- Its condition in any question about creative or intellectual output

## On questions about children

This is among the most emotionally loaded things a chart can be asked about, and the
classical literature is more confident on it than is defensible. A reading must not
produce a statement about whether a native will or will not have children: the
question involves medicine, circumstance and choice, and an astrological indication is
not a prognosis.

What can be said is traditional and conditional — that the house is supported or
stressed, and that the natives relationship to the matter may be easy or effortful.

## A caution

The fifths association with speculation is traditionally read as a warning as often
as a promise. A strong fifth is not read as luck in gambling; it is read as a native
willing to stake something, which is a different trait.

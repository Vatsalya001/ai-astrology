The fourth house sits at the lowest point of the chart in the diurnal sense — the
nadir, opposite the tenth. That geometry is its whole meaning: the tenth is what can be
seen from a distance, the fourth is what cannot be seen at all.

## What it governs

Home, land and immovable property, the mother, emotional foundation, vehicles in some
schools, the chest, and the quality of private life. Classical texts call it bandhu
bhava, the house of connection.

## The fourth-tenth axis

These two houses are read together or not usefully at all. A strong tenth with an
afflicted fourth is traditionally read as a public life that costs a private one — the
classical sources are specific about this, and it is a tension to name in a reading
rather than a verdict to deliver.

The reverse pattern, a strong fourth with a weak tenth, is read as a settled life
without external recognition, which many natives experience as entirely satisfactory
and which a reading should not pathologise.

## How it is read

- The fourth lord for the stability of the natives base
- The Moons relationship to this house for emotional security specifically
- Its condition before any conclusion about property or relocation

## A caution

The fourth is where a reading is most likely to touch something the native has not
discussed. Material about the mother and about early home life deserves tentative
framing and room to be wrong.

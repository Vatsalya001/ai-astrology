The tenth house is the house of action in the world. Classically the karma bhava, it
is read as vocation, public standing, authority, and the visible result of effort.

## What it governs

Profession and the shape of a working life; reputation as distinct from character;
relationship to authority, both exercised and submitted to; and the father in some
classical schemes, though the ninth is more usual for that.

It is the most elevated point of the chart in the diurnal sense — the midheaven sits
at or near its cusp — and that geometry is the whole of its symbolism. The tenth is
what can be seen from a distance.

## How it is read

- Its lord's placement and strength matter more than its occupants
- An occupied tenth is traditionally read as a visible career; an empty one as a
  career shaped by the lord's house instead
- Benefics here are read as recognition; malefics as recognition that is slower and
  more durable

## A caution

The tenth describes standing, not satisfaction. The classical texts are specific
that a strong tenth with an afflicted fourth is read as a public life that costs a
private one — which is a tension to name in a reading, not a verdict to deliver.

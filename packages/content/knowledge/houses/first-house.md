The first house is the ascendant — the sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment
of birth. Everything else in the chart is counted from it, which makes it the one
placement that depends on birth time to the minute.

## What it governs

The body, physical constitution, appearance, temperament, and the manner in which the
native meets the world before deciding how to. Classical texts call it tanu bhava, the
house of the body, and treat it as the foundation of a reading rather than one topic
among twelve.

## Why it is read first

The ascendant determines which sign occupies every other house, and therefore which
planets rule them. A chart read without a reliable ascendant is not a weakened reading
— it is a different chart. This is why birth-time accuracy is the first question in any
consultation and why rectification exists as a discipline.

## How it is read

- Its lord — the ascendant lord — is the single most important planet in the chart
- Planets in the first house are traditionally read as visible traits, the ones
  strangers notice
- Benefics here are read as ease of presence; malefics as a presence that costs
  something to maintain

## A caution

The first house describes constitution, not worth. A difficult ascendant is read as a
body and temperament that require management, which is a practical matter and not a
statement about the person.

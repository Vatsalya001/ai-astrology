The seventh house is directly opposite the first. That geometry is the reading: the
first is the self, the seventh is whatever the self meets as an equal and cannot
absorb.

## What it governs

Marriage and the spouse, business partnership, contracts, open opposition in the sense
of a declared adversary, trade, and the lower abdomen.

## How it is read

- The seventh lord for the nature of partnership the native forms
- Venus for men and Jupiter for women in the classical scheme, though that asymmetry
  belongs to the literatures period and deserves comment rather than repetition
- Occupants for what the native brings to a partnership rather than what they receive
- The seventh from the Moon as a secondary reading, for emotional partnership

## Compatibility

Comparing two charts is a legitimate traditional practice and a reading of it must
never produce a verdict. The classical systems — ashtakoota in the north, the
dasakoota variants in the south — score compatibility on dimensions that were designed
for arranged matches in a specific social context, and they are not a judgement on two
people who have chosen each other.

A compatibility reading can name tensions and strengths. It cannot say "do not marry
this person", and a reading that does has exceeded both its evidence and its remit.

## A caution

Afflictions to the seventh are extremely common — Mangal Dosha alone covers roughly
half of all charts — and the classical texts list extensive cancellations for most of
them. Frequency is the first thing to check before weighting any seventh-house
affliction in a reading.

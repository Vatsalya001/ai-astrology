The third house is sahaja bhava — the house of siblings, and more broadly of the
natives own initiative. It is an upachaya house, one of the four where malefics are
traditionally read as strengthening rather than afflicting.

## What it governs

Younger siblings, courage, short journeys, hands and arms, writing and the mechanics
of communication, and the willingness to act without permission.

## Why malefics do well here

The third is one of the houses — with the sixth, tenth and eleventh — where classical
texts read Mars and Saturn as assets. The reasoning is that the third signifies effort
against resistance, and a planet that supplies resistance supplies the conditions for
effort. A Mars in the third is traditionally read as courage; a Mars in the fourth,
the house of peace, as disturbance.

This is the clearest case in the system of a placements meaning depending entirely on
the house rather than on the planet, and it is worth understanding before applying the
word "malefic" to any chart.

## How it is read

- The third lord for the natives relationship with siblings and with their own drive
- Occupants for the register of their communication
- Its condition in any question about self-directed work, as distinct from employment

## A caution

A strong third with a weak tenth is a specific pattern: considerable self-starting
energy, applied without a structure that accumulates it.

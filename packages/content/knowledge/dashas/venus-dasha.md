Twenty years — the longest of the nine periods. Relationship, comfort and the pursuit
of what is worth having become the organising concern.

## The theme

Classical texts read marriage, pleasure, art, vehicles, property and material ease.
Twenty years is long enough to span most of an adult life's middle, and Venus periods
frequently read as the stretch in which the native built the life they wanted rather
than the one they were given.

## What to read

- Venus's house and the houses of Taurus and Libra
- Venus's dignity: exalted in Pisces, debilitated in Virgo
- The 7th house and its lord separately, since relationship questions during a Venus
  period involve both
- Venus's relationship with Saturn, a friendship that classical texts read as durable
  commitment rather than as constraint

## Common patterns

A dignified Venus is read as a period of comfort, relational satisfaction and
acquisition. An afflicted Venus is read as appetite without satisfaction — getting what
was wanted and finding it insufficient — which is a specific and recognisable
condition.

Because Venus is a benefic in the classical sense of amplifying, a Venus in a difficult
house is read as producing pleasant-looking versions of those difficulties. Several
sources note this specifically for the 6th and 12th.

## What not to say

Nothing about whether a marriage will happen or last. A twenty-year period with a
relational theme is not a prediction about a relationship, and a reading that treats it
as one has made a claim about two people from one chart.

As with Mercury, the length means the antardashas do the interpretive work.

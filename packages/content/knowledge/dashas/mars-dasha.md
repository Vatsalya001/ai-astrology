Seven years in which effort, conflict and the application of force become the
organising concern.

## The theme

Classical texts read a period of action — undertakings begun, resistance met, and
energy expended. Associations include property and land, disputes, surgery, siblings,
and work requiring physical or competitive effort.

Mars dashas are frequently remembered as busy rather than as good or bad, which is the
most accurate general description of them.

## What to read

- Mars's house: where the native will be expending effort
- Aries' and Scorpio's houses, both of which become active
- Mars's dignity: exalted in Capricorn, debilitated in Cancer
- Whether Mars sits in an upachaya house — 3rd, 6th, 10th, 11th — where it is read as
  an asset rather than an affliction

## Common patterns

Mars in an upachaya house is read as a productive period of self-directed work. Mars in
the 4th, the house of peace, is read as domestic disturbance. Mars in the 7th is read
as friction in partnership.

A strong Mars period is traditionally associated with acquisition of property and with
advancement through competition. An afflicted one is associated with disputes, and
classical sources mention accidents and surgery — material that should be handled
carefully. A reading may note that the period emphasises physical risk and that
ordinary caution is sensible; it must not predict an injury.

## What not to say

Nothing about accidents as events. The defensible statement is thematic: this is a
period of exertion, and exertion has a physical dimension worth being sensible about.

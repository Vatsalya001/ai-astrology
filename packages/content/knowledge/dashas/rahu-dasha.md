Eighteen years in which ambition, novelty and appetite become the organising concern.

## The theme

Classical texts read worldly engagement at intensity — advancement, foreign
involvement, unconventional directions, and a drive that does not settle on
satisfaction. Rahu is read as amplifying and distorting whatever it contacts.

Rahu owns no sign, which makes its period unusually dependent on other factors: its
house, and the condition of the planet ruling the sign it occupies.

## What to read

- Rahu's house: where the native becomes insatiable
- **Rahu's dispositor** — the lord of its sign — whose condition is read as whether
  the appetite is productive. This matters more for Rahu than the equivalent does for
  any other planet
- Any planet conjunct Rahu, which is read as that planet's significations amplified
  and destabilised
- Whether the period coincides with transits over the natal nodal axis

## Common patterns

Rahu in a kendra or the 10th is read as conspicuous worldly advancement — the classical
sources are notably positive about Rahu in the 10th and 11th. Rahu in a trine is read
as unconventional merit.

The characteristic Rahu-period experience in the traditional literature is success that
does not feel like enough, which is a useful and non-fatalistic thing to be able to
name for someone.

## What not to say

Rahu attracts the most alarming material in popular astrology, much of it
unsupported — see the Kala Sarpa document. An eighteen-year period should not be
presented as a hazard. The honest reading is that it is a period of appetite, and that
whether the appetite builds something depends on where it is directed.

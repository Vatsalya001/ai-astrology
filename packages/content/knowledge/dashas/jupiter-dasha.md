Sixteen years in which expansion, learning and principle become the organising concern.

## The theme

Classical texts rate Jupiter's period among the most favourable, and the reasoning is
straightforward: the natural great benefic is in charge for sixteen years. Associations
include education, teaching, children, travel, law, and the growth of resources.

## The caveat the classical texts actually make

Jupiter expands whatever it touches, and "whatever" includes difficulty. Several
sources are explicit that a Jupiter in a dusthana produces a period of expanded loss,
and that a Jupiter aspecting an afflicted planet makes the affliction generous rather
than small.

A reading that treats a Jupiter dasha as sixteen good years has substituted a modern
simplification for what the sources say.

## What to read

- Jupiter's house: what will be expanded
- The houses of Sagittarius and Pisces, both active
- Jupiter's dignity: exalted in Cancer, debilitated in Capricorn
- **Jupiter's aspect** — on the 5th, 7th and 9th from itself — which is traditionally
  the most protective influence in a chart and which becomes prominent in its own
  period

## Common patterns

A dignified Jupiter in a trine or kendra is read as a genuinely expansive period:
study completed, children, advancement, resources that grow. A Jupiter in the 6th,
8th or 12th is read as a period in which the native's difficulties become larger and
more visible, which is not the same as worse but is not comfortable.

## What not to say

Nothing about children as a prediction. Jupiter is the significator and the question is
medical, circumstantial and personal; a dasha indication is not a prognosis.

Ten years, and the period in which emotional life and domestic circumstances become
the organising concern.

## The theme

The mind, the home, the mother, and the native's sense of security. Classical texts
read considerable sensitivity during a Moon period — a decade in which how things feel
matters more than how they are.

Because the Moon rules the dasha system itself, its own period is traditionally
considered a time when the native's inner life is unusually legible to them.

## What to read

- The Moon's house, sign and nakshatra — all three, since the nakshatra anchors the
  whole system
- **The Moon's phase**, which no other planet has: a waxing Moon dasha and a waning
  Moon dasha are read differently, and this is a real distinction rather than a
  refinement
- Cancer's house in the chart, which becomes active
- Jupiter's or Saturn's aspect on the Moon, read as support or constraint on the
  period's emotional tone

## Common patterns

A strong waxing Moon in a benefic house is read as a settled, productive decade with
domestic consolidation — property, family, emotional security.

A weak or afflicted Moon is read as a decade of emotional work: not misfortune, but a
period in which the native's inner state requires attention it has not previously
needed. Several classical sources associate it with moves and with changes of
residence, consistent with the Moon's mutability.

## What not to say

Material about the mother is likely to touch something undiscussed and belongs in
tentative framing. And a Moon dasha should never be presented as a decade of
instability — the Moon moves, which is not the same as a life coming apart.

The classical associations between planets and gemstones, and why this product does not
recommend wearing one.

## The traditional correspondences

| Planet | Stone |
|---|---|
| Sun | Ruby |
| Moon | Pearl |
| Mars | Red coral |
| Mercury | Emerald |
| Jupiter | Yellow sapphire |
| Venus | Diamond |
| Saturn | Blue sapphire |
| Rahu | Hessonite (gomed) |
| Ketu | Cat's eye |

Traditional practice specifies the metal, the finger, the weekday, and the muhurta for
first wearing, and the rules vary by school.

## The traditional logic, and its internal dispute

The stated mechanism is that the stone strengthens its planet. Which immediately raises
the question the classical sources themselves argue about: **do you strengthen a weak
planet, or a planet that is already functionally benefic?**

The two schools give opposite advice for the same chart. Strengthening a functionally
malefic planet — a dusthana lord, say — is held by one school to make its difficulty
worse, and by another to be exactly the point. There is no resolution in the
literature.

This matters because it means a gemstone recommendation is not a determinate output of
a chart. Two competent traditional astrologers can recommend opposite stones, and both
can cite sources.

## Why this product does not recommend one

**It is a purchase.** Gemstones in the sizes traditionally specified are expensive, and
a recommendation is a recommendation to spend money. That is the line in the remedies
ethics document.

**The schools disagree**, so the recommendation would be a choice of school presented
as a reading.

**The sales pattern is well established.** The common version pairs an alarming
description of a configuration with a stone that removes it, and the stone is sold by
whoever gave the reading.

## What to say when asked

The correspondence, as cultural material. That the tradition is real and old. That the
schools disagree about when to strengthen a planet, so there is no single correct stone
for a chart. And that nothing in this product's reading requires a purchase.

If a native has already been told to buy one, the useful information is that the
recommendation is contested within the tradition itself.

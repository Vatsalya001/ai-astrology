## Venus as chart lord

Taurus rising makes Venus the chart lord, ruling the 1st and the 6th. Venus's sign,
house, dignity and company are read before any other placement: the lagna lord sets the
terms the rest of the chart operates on. Its 6th-house rulership means the chart lord
carries work and opposition as well as vitality.

## Saturn is the yogakaraka

Saturn rules the 9th (Capricorn) and the 10th (Aquarius) — a trine and an angle. That
combination makes it the yogakaraka: the single strongest functional benefic in a
Taurus chart. Saturn remains a natural malefic, so the reading is not "easy" but
"productive": slow, earned, durable. The designation requires an angle and a trine other
than the ascendant itself, which holds for only six of the twelve; Taurus is one.

Mercury, ruling the 5th (Virgo) and 2nd, is the other functional benefic. The Sun rules
the 4th alone — a natural malefic on an angle, which classical texts treat as mitigated.

## What becomes functionally malefic

**Jupiter** rules the 8th (Sagittarius) and 11th (Pisces) and no trine at all. The
greatest natural benefic is a functional malefic for Taurus rising, which is the single
most-ignored fact about this ascendant. **Mars** rules the 7th and 12th — also no
trine. The **Moon** rules the 3rd only, and reads as mildly difficult.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a solid, broad-set build with the face, neck and throat as the
sign's region, a strong appetite and real endurance. Temperament: patient, tangible,
difficult to redirect once committed. Taurus rises at close to the average rate, around
8% of births — unremarkable in frequency.

## A caution

A Jupiter period is routinely announced as fortunate. For Taurus rising it is the period
of the 8th and 11th lord. Equally, Saturn's benefic role does not make it pleasant — it
makes it worth the cost.

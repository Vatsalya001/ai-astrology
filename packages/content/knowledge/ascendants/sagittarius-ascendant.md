## Jupiter as chart lord

Sagittarius rising makes Jupiter the chart lord, ruling the 1st and the 4th. Jupiter's
condition outweighs every other factor, and this is the ascendant where that principle is
most often skipped — a chart ruled by the great benefic gets assumed to be fortunate. A
debilitated or combust Jupiter is still the chart lord, and the reading follows its state
rather than its reputation.

## The Sun and Mars become the benefics

- **Sun** — the 9th (Leo), a trine, and the strongest functional benefic here.
- **Mars** — the 5th (Aries) and the 12th (Scorpio). The trine prevails: a natural
  malefic functioning as a benefic, which is the useful correction for this ascendant.
- **Jupiter** — 1st and 4th, benefic as lagna lord.
- **Saturn** — the 2nd and 3rd: no trine, no dusthana. Neutral to mildly adverse.

## The Moon rules only the eighth

Cancer falls on the 8th for Sagittarius rising, so the Moon's single rulership is a
dusthana — the strict functional-malefic case. This says nothing about anyone's lifespan:
the 8th signifies what is hidden, inherited and transformative, and the Moon's period
activates those.

**Venus** rules the 6th (Taurus) and 11th (Libra): no trine, one dusthana. **Mercury**
rules the 7th and 10th — two angles, no trine, the kendradhipati condition. Both
remaining natural benefics are therefore functionally unhelpful here, and both are
routinely read as the opposite.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a tall, large-framed build that fills out with age; the hips,
thighs and liver are the sign's region. Temperament: declarative, instructive, inclined
to state the general principle before examining the particular case. Sagittarius rises
at close to the average rate, near 9% of births at Indian latitudes.

## A caution

This is the ascendant most often handed an unearned optimism — "lucky", "protected",
"blessed". The classical texts are more restrained: Jupiter signifies expansion, and
expansion applies to difficulties as readily as to opportunities. A reading can honestly
offer the condition of Jupiter, not a promise about outcomes.

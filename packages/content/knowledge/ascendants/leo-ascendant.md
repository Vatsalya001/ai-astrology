## The Sun as chart lord

Leo rising makes the Sun the chart lord. Like the Moon it rules a single sign, so there
is no secondary rulership to complicate it — the chart lord's reading is unusually clean.
What matters is the Sun's house, its dignity, and whether any planet sits close enough to
be combust, since a combust planet in a Leo chart is weakened by the chart lord itself.

## Mars is the yogakaraka

Mars rules the 4th (Scorpio) and the 9th (Aries) — angle and trine — making it the
yogakaraka and the strongest functional benefic here. Jupiter rules the 5th
(Sagittarius) and the 8th (Pisces); the trine prevails, with the 8th noted.

## The Moon as a functional malefic

Cancer falls on the 12th for Leo rising, so the Moon — a natural benefic when waxing —
rules only a dusthana. There are just six such cases in the whole system: the Moon
supplies three (Leo, Sagittarius and Aquarius rising) and the Sun the other three.

**Saturn** rules the 6th and 7th, no trine, and is the other difficult planet.
**Venus** (3rd, 10th) and **Mercury** (2nd, 11th) rule no trine either and read as
neutral to mildly adverse.

## Signs on the twelve houses

1 Leo · 2 Virgo · 3 Libra · 4 Scorpio · 5 Sagittarius · 6 Capricorn · 7 Aquarius ·
8 Pisces · 9 Aries · 10 Taurus · 11 Gemini · 12 Cancer.

Note the 7th: Aquarius, ruled by Saturn. The Sun's classical enemy sits on the house of
partnership, the configuration most worth reading carefully here.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as an upright carriage with broad chest and shoulders; the heart and
spine are the sign's region. Temperament: direct, generous with those it has accepted,
resistant to being managed. Leo rises slowly — around 9% of births at Indian latitudes.

## A caution

The Moon's functional-malefic status is not a statement about the mother or about
emotional life; it describes which house the Moon's period activates. And the "regal"
reading of Leo rising is flattery rather than analysis — the Sun's dignity decides, and
it is debilitated in Libra like any other Sun.

## Venus as chart lord

Libra rising makes Venus the chart lord, ruling the 1st and the 8th. The chart lord
carrying a dusthana is the first thing to register: a Libra chart is not comfortable by
default because a natural benefic rules it. Venus's sign, house and dignity are read
first, with that 8th-house rulership held alongside.

## Saturn: exalted here, and the yogakaraka

Saturn rules the 4th (Capricorn) and the 5th (Aquarius) — an angle and a trine — so it is
the yogakaraka and the strongest functional benefic here. Libra is also Saturn's sign of
exaltation, so a Saturn in the first house is exalted and yogakaraka at once: about the
most favourable thing the classical system says of a natural malefic anywhere.

Mercury rules the 9th (Gemini) and the 12th (Virgo): benefic on the trine, with the 12th
noted. The Moon holds the 10th — an angle, no trine, neutral.

## Jupiter is a functional malefic

Jupiter rules the 3rd (Sagittarius) and the 6th (Pisces) — no trine, one dusthana. For
Libra rising the greatest natural benefic has no auspicious rulership, exactly as for
Taurus. **The Sun** rules the 11th alone and reads as mildly adverse. **Mars** holds the
2nd and 7th: mitigated on the angle, but ruling no trine.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a well-proportioned build with regular features; the lower abdomen
and kidneys are the sign's region. Temperament: comparative — decisions reached by
weighing options against each other rather than from a fixed position — and genuinely
uncomfortable with imbalance. Libra rises slowly and is a little more common than
average at Indian latitudes, around 9% of births.

## A caution

"Indecisive" is the usual verdict and it mistakes method for weakness: weighing takes
time. The real caution is the pairing of Saturn's favour with Jupiter's lack of it.
Libra is the ascendant where reading planets by natural character alone produces the
most wrong answers — a reason to check rulerships, not a prediction about anyone's
life.

## Saturn as chart lord

Aquarius rising makes Saturn the chart lord, ruling the 1st and the 12th. Aquarius and
Capricorn share a chart lord and get read as near-interchangeable, which is the mistake
to avoid: for Capricorn, Saturn rules the 1st and 2nd; here, the 1st and a dusthana. One
sign's difference changes the whole functional map.

## Venus is the yogakaraka

Venus rules the 4th (Taurus) and the 9th (Libra) — angle and trine — so it is the
yogakaraka and the strongest functional benefic here. Mercury rules the 5th (Gemini) and
the 8th (Virgo): benefic on the trine, with the 8th held against it. The three trine
lords are Saturn, Mercury and Venus, mutual friends in the classical scheme — one of six
ascendants where that holds.

## Jupiter rules no trine; the Moon rules only the sixth

**Jupiter** holds the 2nd (Pisces) and the 11th (Sagittarius) — no trine, no dusthana, so
neutral rather than malefic, but not the chart's benefic either: Venus outranks it. **The
Moon** rules the 6th alone, the strict functional-malefic case. **The Sun** holds the 7th
and is a classical enemy of the chart lord — read carefully on partnership questions
rather than concluded from. **Mars** (3rd, 10th) rules no trine but is mitigated on the
angle.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a tall, long-limbed frame with uneven colouring; the ankles, shins
and circulation are the sign's region. Temperament: principled at a distance, unmoved by
appeals to sentiment or status, idiosyncratic in ways that prove consistent. Aquarius
rises quickly — around 6.5% of births at Indian latitudes, one of the less common
ascendants.

## A caution

Aquarius rising is most often described as "detached", and the word does more harm than
work. Classically the sign signifies the capacity to hold a position that is not socially
rewarded — which looks like coldness from outside and consistency from inside. Describe
the trait; do not grade the person for it.

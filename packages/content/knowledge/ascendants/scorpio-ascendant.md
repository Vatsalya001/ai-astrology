## Mars as chart lord

Scorpio rising makes Mars the chart lord, ruling the 1st and the 6th. The chart lord
holding the house of adversaries and illness is the defining feature: Mars's condition
bears on the constitution and on the capacity to withstand opposition at once, and the
two readings cannot be separated. Mars is assessed first.

## The functional benefics are the luminaries and Jupiter

- **Jupiter** rules the 5th (Pisces) and the 2nd — the clearest functional benefic.
- **The Moon** rules the 9th (Cancer), a trine, and is the other strong one. It is also
  debilitated in Scorpio itself, so a Moon in the first house here is the 9th lord in
  its weakest sign — role and dignity pulling opposite ways.
- **The Sun** rules the 10th alone: a natural malefic on an angle, treated as
  serviceable rather than harmful.
- **Saturn** holds the 3rd and 4th, no trine, and reads as mixed.

## Venus and Mercury both become functional malefics

Venus rules the 7th (Taurus) and the 12th (Libra); Mercury the 8th (Gemini) and the 11th
(Virgo). Neither rules a trine and each rules a dusthana. Scorpio is the only ascendant
of the twelve for which that holds of both at once — derivable from the sign order, not a
matter of opinion. So the two natural benefics most often read as pleasant are here the
two to examine most carefully.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a compact, strongly made build with marked features and a steady
gaze; the reproductive and eliminative systems are the sign's region. Temperament:
reserved about its own affairs, investigative about everyone else's, slow to release a
grievance. Scorpio rises slowly and is among the more common ascendants at Indian
latitudes, near 9% of births.

## A caution

Scorpio rising attracts the most lurid writing of the twelve. Its classical
significations are concealment and research — "intense" describes a method of attention,
not a verdict on character. A reading that treats this ascendant as a warning about
someone is misusing it.

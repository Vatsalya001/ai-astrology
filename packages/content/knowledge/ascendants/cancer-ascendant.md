## The Moon as chart lord

Cancer rising makes the Moon the chart lord, and the Moon rules one sign only — so the
lagna lord carries no second house and no dusthana. Its condition is the chart's first
question and the most variable of the seven: the Moon changes sign in a little over two
days, and its phase matters as much as its sign. Two Cancer ascendants born a week apart
can read differently for that reason alone.

## Mars is the yogakaraka

Mars rules the 5th (Scorpio) and the 10th (Aries) — a trine and an angle — making it the
yogakaraka and the strongest functional benefic here. A natural malefic in the benefic
seat is the whole point of functional analysis: Mars is read as the planet that builds.

Jupiter rules the 9th (Pisces) and the 6th (Sagittarius): benefic on the trine, with the
6th noted. The Sun rules the 2nd alone and reads as neutral.

## What becomes functionally malefic

**Saturn** rules the 7th and 8th — an angle and a dusthana, no trine — and is the
hardest planet for this ascendant. **Mercury** rules the 3rd and the 12th: another
natural benefic working as a functional malefic. **Venus** rules the 4th and 11th, no
trine, and reads as neutral at best.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a softer, rounder build with changeable weight; the chest and
stomach are the sign's region. Temperament: responsive, retentive of slights and
kindnesses alike, protective of a small circle. Cancer rises slowly and is among the
more frequent ascendants at Indian latitudes, near 9% of births.

## A caution

Because the chart lord is the fastest-moving body in the chart, Cancer rising is the
ascendant most sensitive to a vague birth time — not for the lagna degree, but because a
few hours can change the Moon's nakshatra and with it the whole dasha sequence.

## Mars as chart lord

Aries rising makes Mars the chart lord, and in this chart Mars rules two houses — the
first and the eighth. Its condition is assessed before any other planet's: the lagna
lord carries the chart's vitality, and a strong one is traditionally read as making a
difficult chart workable where benefics elsewhere do not.

## Which planets become functionally benefic

Functional nature follows from rulership, not from a planet's natural character.

- **Sun** — rules the 5th (Leo). A trine, and the cleanest functional benefic here.
- **Jupiter** — rules the 9th (Sagittarius) and 12th (Pisces). Ninth lordship prevails.
- **Mars** — the 1st and 8th: benefic as lagna lord, with the 8th as a standing caveat.
- **Mercury** — the 3rd and 6th. A natural benefic functioning as a malefic.
- **Saturn** — the 10th and 11th, no trine, and debilitated in the rising sign itself.
- **Venus** — the 2nd and 7th, and the **Moon** the 4th alone. Both neutral.

## Signs on the twelve houses

1 Aries · 2 Taurus · 3 Gemini · 4 Cancer · 5 Leo · 6 Virgo · 7 Libra · 8 Scorpio ·
9 Sagittarius · 10 Capricorn · 11 Aquarius · 12 Pisces.

Partnership therefore falls in Libra: a sign requiring accommodation opposite one that
does not, which is this ascendant's structural tension.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a spare, quickly recovering build, the head as the sign's region,
and a temperament that meets resistance directly and begins more than it completes.
Aries rises quickly at Indian latitudes — roughly 7% of births against an even share of
8.3%.

## A caution

Mars as chart lord is habitually read as anger. The more useful reading is a
requirement to spend energy — an Aries ascendant with nothing demanding is the harder
configuration. Mars's eighth-house rulership is a reason for careful interpretation,
not a statement about health or lifespan.

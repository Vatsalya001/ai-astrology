## Saturn as chart lord

Capricorn rising makes Saturn the chart lord, ruling the 1st and the 2nd. Neither is a
dusthana: the most demanding of the classical seven governs the body and its resources
here without also carrying a difficult house. Saturn's dignity, house and motion are the
first assessment, and a Saturn in its own sign on the lagna is read as strong — not the
same as comfortable.

## Venus is the yogakaraka

Venus rules the 5th (Taurus) and the 10th (Libra) — trine and angle — making it the
yogakaraka and the strongest functional benefic here. Mercury rules the 9th (Virgo) and
the 6th (Gemini): benefic on the trine, qualified by the dusthana. Mars holds the 4th
and 11th — no trine, but mitigated on the angle and read as serviceable.

## Jupiter and the Sun

**Jupiter** rules the 3rd (Pisces) and the 12th (Sagittarius) — no trine, one dusthana.
Capricorn is the third ascendant, with Taurus and Libra, for which Jupiter holds no
auspicious rulership. **The Sun** rules the 8th alone, and is also a classical enemy of
the chart lord — a property of the sign order, not a statement about anyone's father or
about authority turning against them. The **Moon** holds the 7th: an angle, no trine,
neutral.

## Constitution and temperament

Traditionally read as a lean, dry, strongly jointed build that matures late; the knees,
joints, bones and skin are the sign's region. Temperament: deliberate, undemonstrative,
willing to carry something a long time before showing it to anyone. Capricorn rises
quickly at Indian latitudes — nearer 7.5% of births than an even 8.3%.

## A caution

The "slow start, late consolidation" reading is one of the more durable observations in
the literature, and the easiest to turn into a licence for delay. Classically Saturn
declines to grant what is unearned — it is not read as granting the same things later by
default. The honest statement concerns pacing, and stops there.

Mars aspects the 4th, 7th and 8th houses from its own position, all in full strength.

## What it does

Applies force and urgency. The aspected house's significations are read as energised
and as contested — the native pushes on that area of life and meets resistance there.

## Why Mars's aspect set is the harshest

The 4th and the 8th are both sensitive houses. The 4th is domestic peace and emotional
foundation; the 8th is transformation and discontinuity. Mars's aspect falling on both,
plus the universal 7th, means its influence lands on the three areas least suited to
force.

This is the structural reason Mars has the reputation it does, more than its own
nature: it is not that Mars is worse than Saturn, it is that its aspects fall
awkwardly.

## In practice

Note which three houses Mars aspects. Those are the areas where the native is
energetic, impatient, and likely to create friction they did not intend. In an
upachaya house the reading is productive drive; in the 4th it is domestic disturbance;
in the 8th it is appetite for the extreme.

## What it does not mean

Nothing about accidents or injury as events. A Mars aspect is a standing condition, and
reading it as a forecast of physical harm is both unsupported and the kind of statement
that does damage. The defensible content is temperamental: this is where the native
applies force.

Saturn aspects the 3rd, 7th and 10th houses from its own position, all in full
strength.

## What it does

Applies duration and constraint. The aspected house's significations are read as slow,
exacting, and durable once established. Saturn's aspect is not read as damage — it is
read as the requirement that something be earned.

## Why the 3rd and 10th are the interesting ones

Both are upachaya houses, where Saturn is read as an asset. Saturn aspecting the 10th
is traditionally a career indication of considerable strength: standing that accrues
slowly and does not reverse. Saturn aspecting the 3rd is read as sustained
self-directed effort.

Saturn aspecting the 7th is the one that generally needs care — partnership under
constraint, read as commitment that is durable and effortful, or as delay.

## The asymmetry, made concrete

Saturn in the 1st aspects the 3rd, 7th and 10th. Saturn in the 10th aspects the 12th,
4th and 7th — **not** the 1st. These are entirely different charts, and conflating them
is the standard error.

## What it does not mean

Saturn's aspect is permanent and present from birth. It is not an event, not a phase,
and not something that will pass. A native told Saturn's aspect on their 7th house is a
difficult period has been told something structurally wrong: it is a condition of the
chart, and reading it as temporary is a different error from reading it as severe.

Transits are the time-varying thing. See the Sade Sati document, which is about
Saturn's transit rather than its natal aspect, and which is where the confusion
usually originates.

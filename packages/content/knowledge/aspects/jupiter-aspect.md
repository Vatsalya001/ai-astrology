Jupiter aspects the 5th, 7th and 9th houses from its own position, all in full
strength.

## Why this is the most important aspect in the system

Three houses rather than one, and two of them in trine positions from Jupiter. The
classical texts treat Jupiter's aspect as the chart's primary protective influence —
the thing that makes an otherwise difficult configuration workable.

The practical rule follows: **before concluding anything negative about a house or a
planet, check whether Jupiter aspects it.** A great deal of alarming-looking material
is already mitigated and a reading that misses this overstates consistently.

## What it does

Expands and supports. The aspected house's significations are read as larger, more
generous and better supplied. An afflicted planet under Jupiter's aspect is read as
afflicted and supported rather than as fixed.

## The caveat that applies to all of Jupiter

Jupiter expands whatever it touches. Classical sources note that Jupiter's aspect on a
dusthana or on a malefic in a difficult house makes the difficulty larger and more
visible, not smaller. "Benefic" in this system means amplifying, not improving.

The common case is still protective — most houses benefit from expansion — but the
exception is real and should be checked rather than assumed away.

## In practice

Note which three houses Jupiter aspects in the chart. Those are the areas of life the
native has structural support in, and they are usually the areas they describe as
having been easier than expected.

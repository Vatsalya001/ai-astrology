## Own sign, with moolatrikona in the first ten degrees

Sagittarius is Jupiter's own sign, and its first 10° are Jupiter's moolatrikona. Degree
therefore changes the dignity here, which is not true of Pisces: within those ten
degrees Jupiter holds the strongest standing available to it short of exaltation, and
beyond them it holds own-sign strength, which is already high.

If Jupiter here also falls in a kendra it forms Hamsa yoga, which has its own document —
the same applies to Jupiter in Cancer or Pisces in a kendra.

## Dual fire: the aim can be re-pointed

Fire gives Jupiter a direction and the dual modality lets it change targets without
changing character. That is the real distinction between this placement and Jupiter in
Aries or Leo, where the fire is respectively lit and sustained but not re-aimed.

## Significations at full width

Law, philosophy, teaching, ethical and religious structure. The sign's concerns and the
planet's are the same concerns, which is why the texts spend almost no effort
reconciling them — and why this is one of the few placements where reading the sign
document and the planet document separately already gets most of the way.

## The caution

A planet of certainty in its own fire sign is the classical signature of conviction that
does not audit itself: teaching that does not expect correction, and principle applied
to a case it does not fit. Dignity is strength, not accuracy, and the two are routinely
confused here.

Jupiter in the first is the one position where its directional and positional strengths
agree completely. That makes it the simplest placement in the set to read and the
easiest to overstate.

## Directional strength

The first house is where Jupiter holds dig bala — full strength in the East. No other
house gives it that, and the seventh gives it none. Combined with the first being both
a kendra and a trine, this is Jupiter at its most structurally supported before any
question of sign arises.

## What it reaches from here

From the first, Jupiter's 5-7-9 aspect falls on the fifth, seventh and ninth — both
remaining trines and a kendra. No other placement of Jupiter covers that set. Where it
occurs, the two houses of merit and the house of partnership all carry its expansion at
once, which is worth checking before a reading concludes anything about them
separately.

## What the texts emphasise

Temperament rather than fortune: an expansive, advisory manner, read as optimism and as
a readiness to instruct. The first house is the one strangers notice, so the classical
emphasis is on how this register lands before the native has decided to show it.

Several sources add physical description — Jupiter signifies fat and the liver, and the
texts are blunt about a large frame. That is period detail about bodies and should be
dropped rather than repeated.

## The caution specific to this house

The first house is the self, and Jupiter enlarges what it occupies. The traditional
reservation is self-importance: conviction arriving before it is earned, counsel
offered where none was sought, and more taken on than was arranged for.

Nor is this placement a distinction. Jupiter falls in each house in roughly one chart in
twelve, and the classical warmth toward this one borrows from Jupiter's general
reputation as much as from the house.

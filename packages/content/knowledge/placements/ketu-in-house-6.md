If this corpus has to name a best house for Ketu, the sixth is the strongest candidate,
and the reasoning is structural rather than sentimental. The sixth is a dusthana and
simultaneously an upachaya house. A malefic strengthens here, and what it strengthens is
resistance to the house's own significations.

## Detachment as a working method

Shatru bhava governs opposition, debt, litigation and daily discipline. Ketu applied to
an adversary removes the thing an adversary works with — investment in the outcome.
Classical sources read this as opposition that loses interest, or a native who simply
does not pick the quarrel up. Compare Jupiter here, which expands what the house
signifies and is read less favourably: in the sixth, a planet that withdraws appetite is
doing precisely the right job.

## Service, not vocation

The sixth is employment as distinct from calling. Ketu here is read as work performed
competently and without identification — the job done properly by someone who does not
take it personally, which in service roles is closer to an asset than the word
"detachment" suggests.

## Health — the boundary

The sixth describes a pattern of vulnerability in traditional terms and nothing more. An
astrological indication is not a diagnosis: it does not name a condition, does not
predict an illness, and does not substitute for a physician. Classical material linking
Ketu to particular ailments is deliberately not reproduced here, and a reading that
names a disease has stopped doing astrology.

## The asymmetry to check

A strengthened sixth alongside a weak first is a recognisable pattern: opposition
managed well, the native's own constitution managed poorly. Worth checking before the
favourable reading above is delivered without qualification.

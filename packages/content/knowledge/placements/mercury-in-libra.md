## Friendly sign, movable air

Libra is Venus's, and Venus is the one planet Mercury and the classical table agree
about in both directions — so this is a friendly sign with reciprocity, as Taurus is.
The modality is what separates them. Taurus holds Mercury still; Libra keeps it moving
between positions, because movable air is the register of weighing rather than of
settling.

Read as a mind that formulates by comparison: the other side's case stated first and
stated accurately, proportion noticed before content, agreements drafted rather than
declared. Traditionally favourable for mediation, contracts, diplomacy and any work
whose product is a form of words both parties accept.

## Exalted Saturn in the same sign

Libra is Saturn's exaltation, and Saturn counts Mercury a friend. Mercury takes the
character of what it sits with, and with Saturn that character is rigour — so a
Mercury-Saturn conjunction in Libra puts a well-disposed Mercury beside an exalted
Saturn that regards it well. Classical reading: constraint operating as fairness, and
discrimination that is both exacting and willing to be audited. This is among the better
Mercury configurations the zodiac permits and it does not require any cancellation rule
to arrive at.

## A debilitated Sun is the usual companion

Libra is the Sun's debilitation, and Mercury's elongation limit means Mercury here
requires the Sun in Virgo, Libra or Scorpio. Sun in Libra is a frequent outcome, so this
placement often pairs a supported Mercury with a weakened Sun: a capable instrument
serving an identity that accommodates others before asserting itself. Both halves are
part of the reading.

## The caution

Deferred conclusion. The placement is read as reluctant to decide anything that someone
present will dislike, and the tradition treats indefinite weighing as the cost of the
capacity rather than as a separate flaw.

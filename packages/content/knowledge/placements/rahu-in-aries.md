Rahu holds no rulership and no classical dignity in Aries, as in every sign. The scheme
that grants planets own-sign, exaltation and debilitation status covers the seven; the
nodes sit outside it. What can be said about Rahu in a sign is said through the sign's
lord — here Mars.

## Mars as dispositor

Mars and Rahu are traditionally read as enemies, and Mars is the planet classical
sources pair with Ketu rather than with Rahu: *Kujavat Ketu*, Ketu acts like Mars. Rahu
in Aries therefore sits in material governed by a planet unfriendly to it and
temperamentally closer to its opposite number. The condition of Mars carries this
placement almost entirely. Rahu in Aries with Mars in Capricorn and Rahu in Aries with
Mars in Cancer are not the same reading, and nothing said about the sign alone can
distinguish them.

## What the sign does

Aries is movable fire: ignition without precedent. Rahu is appetite that does not reach
a satiety point. The combination is traditionally read as wanting to be first — not
best, not secure, first — and as reluctance to consult anyone about it. The classical
caution attached to it is not aggression, which is the popular reading and an inflated
one, but starting more than can be carried.

## Frequency

Rahu takes roughly eighteen months to cross a sign, so everyone born inside that window
shares this placement. It is a cohort marker, not an individuating one, and a reading
that leans on it heavily is describing a birth-year group rather than a person. Rahu's
house and its dispositor's condition do the actual work.

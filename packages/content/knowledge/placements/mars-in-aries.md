Mars in Aries is the only placement where nothing stands between the planet and its own
nature, and that is both the strength and the whole of the caution.

## Own sign, and the first twelve degrees

Aries is Mars's own sign (swakshetra), and its first 12° are Mars's mulatrikona — the
strongest dignity below exaltation, and the reason degree matters here when it rarely
does elsewhere. Beyond 12° Mars is still own-sign strong; the distinction is one of
degree rather than of kind, and sources that pass over it are coarse rather than wrong.

## Modality and element pull the same way

Movable fire under its own lord leaves Mars with no moderating influence at all: the
planet of initiation, in the sign of initiation, ruling it. Compare Scorpio, Mars's
other sign, where a fixed modality holds the same force in place. Aries releases it.

## What is traditionally read

Directness that does not consult, physical confidence, and effort that is at its best
early. Classical texts are generous about strength here and noticeably reserved about
duration — the fire of ignition is not the fire of a long campaign. Among Mars's
significations this favours the ones that reward a standing start: contest, enterprise
begun from nothing, physical discipline, work where going first is the advantage.

The Sun is exalted in Aries and the Sun is Mars's friend, so the two sharing this sign
is not read as a clash of natures, which is unusual for Mars conjunctions.

## The caution, and how common this is

Mars occupies Aries in roughly one chart in twelve, as it does Scorpio, so Mars in its
own sign is not a rare finding. The traditional caution is self-originated conflict:
friction the native created rather than met. That is a description of temperament, not
a prediction of events.

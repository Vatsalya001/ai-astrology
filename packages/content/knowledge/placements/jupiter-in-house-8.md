Jupiter in the eighth attracts more unsupportable claims than any other placement of
this planet, for one structural reason: the eighth is ayur bhava, and the classical
longevity methods run through it.

## What this document will not do

The corpus declines longevity estimation, and this is the placement where that refusal
does the most work. The classical methods disagree with each other, they were built for
a question asked in a different setting, and a wrong answer stays unfalsifiable until it
is catastrophic. No statement about death, its timing or its manner follows from this
placement.

## Expansion in the house of discontinuity

The eighth governs transformation and the resources of others, and Jupiter enlarges
both. Read traditionally: inheritance, partner and institutional funds, insurance,
dowry in the older texts — value arriving through someone else's arrangement rather than
the native's own transaction. The eighth's natural sign is Scorpio, ruled by Mars, a
friend of Jupiter, which is part of why the placement reads less harshly than the
house's reputation predicts.

## Research and the unseen

The other classical line is investigative and occult capacity: sustained attention to
what is not visible, with Jupiter supplying the framework that makes it a discipline
rather than an interest. Sources associate it with scholarship in difficult subjects and
with advisory work during crisis — the protective planet stationed where things come
apart.

## What it aspects

From the eighth, Jupiter aspects the twelfth, second and fourth. The second and fourth
are the chart's two houses of holding, resources and home, which is the traditional
basis for reading this placement as resilience rather than exposure. The twelfth
receives the same expansion, and that part is cost.

## The caution

Expansion is not improvement. Discontinuity is read as larger and better supplied, not
as absent, and no inheritance, settlement or recovery is promised by it.

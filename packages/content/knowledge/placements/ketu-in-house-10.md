The tenth is the only house that is both a kendra and an upachaya, and that double
membership is the whole of this reading. As an upachaya house it strengthens a malefic;
as the most elevated and most visible point of the chart, it is where strengthening a
planet of withdrawn appetite produces a genuinely odd result.

## Competence without the wanting

Karma bhava is action in the world and the visible result of effort. Ketu here is read as
professional capability operating at full strength while the usual motive for it —
recognition, advancement, standing — is simply not engaged. Classical sources describe
careers performed well and then left without ceremony. It is not read as incapacity,
which is the common misreading, nor as humility, which is a moral gloss the texts do not
supply.

## Visible, and not minding

An occupied tenth is traditionally read as a visible career. Ketu occupies it while
discounting visibility, so the two halves of the reading pull against each other: work
that is seen clearly by others and not by the native. The recognisable form of this is
the person whose reputation runs ahead of their own account of it.

## Rahu in the fourth

The axis runs down the meridian the other way here: appetite in the private base — home,
land, roots — and indifference at the summit. This is the less discussed half of the
fourth-tenth nodal pair, and it is read as a native who works in order to be left alone
rather than in order to arrive anywhere.

## Standing is not satisfaction

The tenth describes standing, not satisfaction, and Ketu here says nothing about income
— that is the eleventh and the second. No reading may state that a career will or will
not succeed.

Rahu holds no dignity in Pisces, and unlike Taurus, Gemini, Scorpio, Sagittarius and
Aquarius, no modern school has made a serious claim about it either. There is nothing
here to dispute, which is itself worth saying: the combination is classically
unremarkable and should be read as such rather than inflated.

## Jupiter as a neutral dispositor

Jupiter is neutral to Rahu, so the sign supplies neither friendship nor enmity. Pisces
is Jupiter's second sign and the one where its expansiveness has no edges. Everything
specific comes from where Jupiter actually sits.

## Appetite meeting dissolution

This is the genuinely distinctive thing about the pairing, and it is structural rather
than dramatic. Rahu is wanting. Pisces is the sign where distinctions stop holding — the
last sign, where nothing further is being built. An appetite placed in a medium that
dissolves its own objects is traditionally read as longing without a settled target:
devotion, imagination, immersion or diffusion, depending entirely on the rest of the
chart.

Venus's exaltation in Pisces is described in almost the same terms — appetite without
object — which is why the two readings rhyme. Mercury's debilitation is the other half:
fine discrimination is not what this sign does, and the node does not supply it.

## Where readings go wrong

The usual overreach is escape — substances, withdrawal, deception — asserted as outcome.
Those are claims about a person's life and health that no sign placement supports. Dual
water is read as receptive and hard to keep separate from other people's states. That is
where the caution ends.

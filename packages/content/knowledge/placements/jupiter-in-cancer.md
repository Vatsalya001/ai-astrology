## Exaltation, deepest at five degrees

Cancer is Jupiter's sign of exaltation, with the point of maximum dignity at 5°. It is
also the Moon's sign, and Jupiter counts the Moon a friend — the only position in the
zodiac where Jupiter holds the highest dignity and a friendly dispositor at once. By the
classical ranking there is nothing above this for Jupiter anywhere.

## What exaltation actually increases

Exaltation measures how much, not what. This is the placement where that distinction
does the most work, because the planet being amplified is already the planet of
amplification. Maximum dignity sets the volume; the house sets the subject. Treating it
as a guarantee of good outcome is the standard error, and it is harder to avoid here
than anywhere else in the set.

## Movable water

Jupiter's exaltation falls in a movable sign rather than a fixed one, which means the
strength is portable. It is traditionally read as protection that applies itself to new
situations rather than fortifying one position — part of why the classical sources treat
an exalted Jupiter as useful in difficulty and not merely pleasant in comfort.

## Frequency

About one chart in twelve. Jupiter takes roughly a year over a sign, so everyone born
inside the same year-long window carries this placement: it is a cohort marker before it
is an individual one. Worth saying before much weight is put on it, because exaltation
is the word in this vocabulary most likely to be over-read.

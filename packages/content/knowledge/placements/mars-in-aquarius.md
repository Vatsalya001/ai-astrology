Mars in Aquarius is a neutral sign and one of the thinner entries in the classical
literature. Most of what can honestly be said here is built from the sign and the planet
rather than drawn from a specific textual tradition.

## Neutral dignity

Saturn rules Aquarius and is neutral to Mars, so the rulership adds nothing and removes
nothing — the same formal position Mars holds in Taurus and Libra. Aquarius carries no
exaltation or debilitation among the classical seven, so there is no second dignity to
weigh. Some modern schools assign Rahu co-rulership, which is where the reform and
disruption readings originate; the classical attribution is Saturn.

## Force without a personal object

Fixed air is the most abstract register Mars occupies. The energy stays constant and
stops being about the native: the reading is of effort expended on a structure, a
category of people, or a position held for reasons that are not emotional. Organising,
agitating, systems-level engineering, and work done for a collective rather than for a
client.

## What is traditionally read

Persistence in a minority view, and a willingness to be at odds with consensus for a
long time without heat. Mars elsewhere fights things; in Aquarius it is read as fighting
arrangements. Because the sign is fixed, the position does not move when it is argued
with.

## The caution

Conflict conducted impersonally. The specific liability is a principle defended at cost
to the particular person in front of the native — the sign's own caution sharpened by
Mars's method. Given how little the classical sources commit to here, a confident and
detailed reading of this placement should be treated as invention rather than
transmission.

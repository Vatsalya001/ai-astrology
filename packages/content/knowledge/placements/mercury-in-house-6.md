## Exaltation inside a dusthana

Virgo is the sixth house's natural sign, and Virgo is the one sign where Mercury both
rules and exalts — the unusual coincidence noted in Mercury's own document. Where an
ascendant genuinely places Virgo on the sixth, Mercury sits at maximum sign dignity
inside a difficult house. Dignity and house classification point in opposite directions
and the classical texts do not reconcile them: they report the placement favourably and
leave the tension standing. That is the most technically loaded configuration Mercury
has, and it belongs to one ascendant in twelve rather than to the house.

## Why the tension resolves better than it reads

The sixth signifies obstacles, and the house rule is that a strong planet here is read as
strength against them rather than strength of them. Virgo is discrimination applied to
material things — sorting, measuring, correcting. That is exactly the faculty a house of
debt, litigation, opposition and daily discipline calls for. The house document also
notes benefics fare worse here because they expand what the house signifies; Mercury's
neutrality is precisely why that objection has less force against it than against
Jupiter.

## What the combination is read for

Service and employment as distinct from vocation. Diagnostic work in the broad sense of
finding what is wrong with a thing. Administration of debt, of records, of contracts
under dispute. Classically this counts among the better Mercury placements and among the
least glamorous, and a reading should say both.

## The health boundary, which this pair invites crossing

The sixth governs illness and Mercury governs the nervous system. That is the single
pairing in the twelve most likely to tempt a reading into naming a condition, and it must
not. An astrological indication is not a diagnosis, identifies nothing, predicts nothing
and replaces no physician. Describe the house's traditional pattern of vulnerability and
stop there.

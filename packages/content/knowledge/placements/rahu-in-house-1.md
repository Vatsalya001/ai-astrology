Rahu in the ascendant puts the appetite in the house of the body and of first
impression. The first house is both a kendra and a trine, so anything here is
structurally amplified before Rahu amplifies anything.

## Why the dignity schemes have nothing to say

Rahu owns no sign and has no classical exaltation, and the nodes have no entry in the
directional-strength table at all — the first house grants dig bala to Jupiter and
Mercury, and nothing to Rahu. There is no dignity verdict available here.

The reading rests instead on the lord of the rising sign, which in this configuration
is also Rahu's dispositor. That coincidence is specific to the first house, and it
makes the ascendant lord's condition unusually decisive.

## What it is traditionally read as

A presence that registers as unfamiliar: distinctive, hard to place, often taken by
others as more emphatic than the native intends. Classical sources describe
self-definition assembled rather than inherited — an identity built from material the
family did not supply. Ketu occupies the seventh by construction, so the pattern pairs
a magnified self-presentation with partnership the native is inclined to discount.

## Frequency

Rahu spends roughly eighteen months in a sign, so everyone born across a wide window
shares its sign. Which house it falls in depends on the ascendant, and therefore on
birth time to the minute. A first-house Rahu is a claim about birth time as much as
about the node, and it is worth confirming before weighting it.

## The caution specific to this house

The first house governs constitution. This placement is read as temperament and
presentation — not as a statement about health, about the body's soundness, or about a
person's worth.

## An enemy sign with the dispositor exalted

Mercury rules Virgo and is also exalted in it, and Jupiter counts Mercury an enemy. So
this is Jupiter's enemy sign with the sign's ruler at maximum strength — the host's
agenda unusually well supported against its guest. It is still not debilitation, and the
classical texts are measured about it rather than severe.

One symmetry is worth a line: both of Jupiter's enemies hold a dignity in Virgo. Mercury
is exalted here and Venus is debilitated here — one rival at its strongest and the other
at its weakest, in the same thirty degrees.

## Breadth asked to work in the particular

Jupiter concludes; Virgo measures. A planet of general principle placed in the sign of
sorting and correcting is traditionally read as wisdom doing small careful work, and as
being disciplined by the sign rather than only hampered by it. The texts grant this
placement competence more readily than they grant it authority.

## Significations, narrowed

Teaching reads as technical rather than doctrinal: editing, diagnosis, procedure,
standards — the apparatus around knowledge rather than its pronouncement. Law reads as
compliance rather than principle. Dual earth keeps it adaptable within a small compass.

## The caution

Virgo is hard to satisfy and Jupiter is the planet of enough. The combination is
traditionally read as generosity that audits itself — help given, then re-examined — and
as reluctance to accept what is already adequate.

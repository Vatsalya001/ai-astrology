The eighth is a dusthana, and it is the house where a node attracts the most alarming
material in popular astrology. Very little of that material survives contact with the
classical sources.

## The disputed debilitation

Scorpio is the natural eighth sign, and the modern schools that grant Rahu an
exaltation in Taurus grant it a debilitation here by opposition. The classical scheme
gives it neither. Reading a node as fallen in the eighth borrows a convention from a
system that does not cover it, and it is not a reason to darken a reading.

## What it is defensibly read as

Sustained attention to what is not visible. The eighth governs research, hidden things
and the resources of others, and Rahu here is traditionally read as appetite for depth
— investigation, forensic and technical disciplines, occult study, subjects the native
was not raised toward. The eighth is also where things change form, so the placement is
read as a life with sharp discontinuities and an unusual tolerance for them.

Inheritance, insurance and a partner's or an institution's money are eighth-house
matters, and the placement is read as involvement with resources that are not the
native's own. It is not a prediction of receiving any.

## The absolute boundary

The eighth is ayur bhava, and a node in it generates more claims about death than any
other configuration in astrology. None belong in a reading — not timing, not manner,
not likelihood. The classical longevity methods disagree with one another and were
never built for the question as it is now asked. The house document's refusal is total
and a node does not qualify it.

## The caution

Depth costs attention. The tradition reads a native drawn to what resists being known,
which is a vocation for some and a preoccupation for others. The chart does not say
which.

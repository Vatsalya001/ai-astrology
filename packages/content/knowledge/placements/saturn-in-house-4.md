A kendra, but not an upachaya house, so none of the strengthening that favours Saturn in
the third, sixth, tenth and eleventh applies. In the natural zodiac the fourth is
Cancer, the Moon's own sign, and the Moon is one of Saturn's enemies. The fourth is also
where the Moon holds directional strength. On every axis, this house belongs to the
planet Saturn is least at ease with.

## Why the texts are unusually consistent here

Classical sources disagree about Saturn in most houses. On the fourth they largely do
not: it is read as austerity in the foundation — early domestic conditions described as
plain, effortful, or marked by responsibility carried too young. The reading is of a
base that was built rather than inherited.

## The fourth-tenth axis, made literal

Saturn in the fourth aspects the sixth, tenth and first. The tenth aspect is the strong
one, so the tension the fourth house describes in general — a public life that costs a
private one — is structurally present in this placement rather than merely possible.
Naming that tension is the useful move. Delivering it as a verdict is not.

## Property and relocation

Land and immovable property are fourth-house matters, and Saturn is traditionally read
as delay followed by durability: acquired late, held long. Vehicles and domestic comfort
are read the same way. None of this supports a prediction about a purchase, a sale or a
move.

## The caution

This is the placement most likely to touch something the native has not raised. Material
about the mother and about early home life is traditional inference, not knowledge, and
it deserves tentative framing and room to be wrong. Saturn in the fourth is common —
roughly one chart in twelve — and a difficult childhood is not what it establishes.

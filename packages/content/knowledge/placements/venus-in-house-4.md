This is the one placement of the twelve where Venus holds directional strength. It is
also one where the natural sign belongs to an enemy. Both are true at once and neither
cancels the other.

## Dig bala in an enemy's sign

Venus is strongest by direction in the fourth, sharing that position with the Moon.
Cancer is the fourth sign of the natural zodiac and the Moon rules it — and the Moon is
one of Venus's two enemies in the classical friendship scheme. A reading that collapses
this into a single verdict has thrown away the information. Dignity and directional
strength are separate axes, as the strength document sets out, and their disagreement
is the characteristic feature of this combination rather than a problem to resolve.

## The closest overlap in the chart

No other pairing has this much agreement of meaning. The fourth is read for comfort,
home, land and the quality of private life; Venus is read for comfort and for the
capacity to find something worth having. Vehicles appear in both lists — the only point
in this corpus where that signification is doubled — which is why the classical vehicle
material concentrates here.

Where the fourth holds Taurus, Libra or Pisces, the Malavya condition is met in the
house of private life rather than of public standing, and the texts read it as
surroundings arranged to a standard.

## The caution

Two cautions, and they are not the same one. The fourth touches the mother and early
home, material a reading is likely to raise before the native has. And the Venus-Moon
enmity is a statement about two planets — never about the native's relationship with a
parent. Reading it as the latter is the most common error made with this placement.

## The dignity: nothing to report

Neutral sign, and this is the most complete "nothing" in Saturn's dignity table. Jupiter
rules Sagittarius and is Saturn's only neutral — neither friend nor enemy. No classical
planet is exalted or debilitated in Sagittarius. Every dignity axis available returns the
middle value.

That deserves stating plainly rather than filled in. A corpus that supplies every
placement with a verdict teaches the reader that every placement has one, which is the
more damaging error.

## What the combination actually does

Dual fire, aimed at a principle. Saturn's contribution is that the principle is held long
enough to be tested, and the usual Jupiter-Saturn tension — one expands, the other
limits — arrives here without either planet being disadvantaged by the other.
Significations of law, doctrine and teaching are read as acquiring method: slow study,
orthodoxy, a preference for what has already been demonstrated.

The traditional caution is modest in scale: conviction that hardens, and principle
applied where the particular case does not fit it.

## What to read instead

When a sign placement is formally unremarkable, the information is somewhere else — the
house Saturn actually occupies, what aspects it, whether it is combust or retrograde, and
whether a Saturn period is running. Those axes are not quiet. A reading that spends its
words on an average sign placement has spent them in the wrong place.

## Frequency

Like all twelve, it belongs to a two-and-a-half-year cohort. Unlike most of them, there
is no temptation to make that cohort sound special.

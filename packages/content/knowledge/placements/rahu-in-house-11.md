Of the twelve, this is the placement the tradition likes best. The eleventh is an
upachaya house, and its own document already pairs it with Rahu — it grants what is
wanted without reference to whether it was worth wanting. A node in it is that
mechanism concentrated.

## What is traditionally read

Income from unconventional sources, gains arriving through networks rather than through
position, and effectiveness inside large organisations. Classical sources describe
desires met and an appetite for scale. Elder siblings and influential acquaintance are
eleventh-house matters, and Rahu is read as a circle assembled deliberately, from
outside the inherited one.

## The check that actually matters

The eleventh governs inflow and says nothing about retention. That is the second house,
and the pairing the eleventh-house document sets out is the first thing to look at
rather than a refinement. A strong eleventh with an unsupported second is the
recognisable pattern of money arriving and not staying; Rahu here raises the volume on
the inflow side only.

## Desires met is not satisfaction

This is the specific thing to say about the combination. The eleventh delivers what was
asked for, and Rahu is the appetite that revises the request the moment it is granted.
The traditional reading is not of disappointment but of a target that moves — a
describable condition, and considerably more useful to name than a gain is to predict.

## The boundary

No statement that a native will become wealthy, will profit from a venture, or should
act on any financial indication. And the house document's note holds without softening:
a strong eleventh means the native's networks are consequential, not that they are
good.

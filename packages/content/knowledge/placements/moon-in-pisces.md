Pisces is the only water sign in which the Moon is neither dignified nor debilitated —
Cancer is its own, Scorpio its fall — which makes it the unmarked case in the element
the Moon belongs to.

## The unmarked water sign

By relationship the Moon counts Jupiter neutral while Jupiter counts the Moon a friend,
the same one-way goodwill as in Sagittarius. By element the match is exact. Classical
sources read the placement warmly and without emphasis, and that is the correct weight
to give it: congenial rather than strong.

## Receptivity without the shell

The instructive contrast is with Cancer. Both signs are watery and both are read
through reception, but Cancer's symbol includes a defence and Pisces is the sign where
distinctions stop holding. The Moon here is traditionally read as the same receptivity
with nothing filtering it — impressions arriving unsorted, other people's among them.

## Why Mercury's debilitation matters to this placement

Mercury is debilitated in Pisces, and Mercury is the faculty of distinction. The
classical inference for a Moon here is that the equipment which would separate one's own
state from an absorbed one is itself weakest in this sign. That mechanism is the real
content behind the usual caution about sensitivity, and it is considerably more useful
than the caution stated on its own.

## What to emphasise, and what to leave alone

Imagination, compassion, and a capacity for devotion are the standard readings;
porousness is the standard difficulty. Neither is a statement about anyone's
circumstances. Common — and the honest emphasis belongs on how the mind takes things
in, not on what is supposed to come of it.

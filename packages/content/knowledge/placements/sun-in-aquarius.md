Saturn rules Aquarius as well, so this is again an enemy sign for the Sun — shatru
kshetra — and Aquarius offers no exaltation or debilitation to any of the classical seven.
Fixed air under Saturn. The classical material on this combination is thin, and the
honest summary is that the texts give it less attention than they give most of the other
eleven.

## The sign standing opposite the Sun's own

Aquarius is the seventh from Leo, which makes it the only sign directly across from the
Sun's rulership. The arrangement is worth naming because it describes the shape of the
placement: a planet of individual identity in the sign that organises the collective,
under a dispositor whose discipline is applied to systems rather than to persons.

The reading that follows is identity defined by membership — a sense of self running
through a group, an institution or an idea held in common, rather than through personal
standing. Fixed modality makes such a commitment hard to shift once made.

## A confusion worth correcting

Being seventh from one's own sign is not a dignity, and the Sun in Aquarius is not
debilitated. The debilitation is Libra, and only Libra. Popular readings sometimes treat
the opposition to Leo as though it carried comparable weight; it does not, and the
classical scheme never says it does.

## What the tradition does claim

Capacity for impersonal fairness, tolerance for being in a minority, and a relationship
to authority that is sceptical of it on principle. The traditional caution inverts the Leo
one: not a native who needs to be seen, but one who declines recognition in situations
where accepting it would have served the work.

Mars in the second is approached through the voice before the money, which is the
reverse of how dhana bhava is usually read.

## Speech as an instrument

The second governs the voice, and Mars there is traditionally read as speech used as
force: argument preferred to negotiation, correction offered before it was asked for,
and an economy of words that lands as curtness on listeners who expected warmth.
Classical sources are blunter still and specify quarrelsome speech within the household,
since the second also governs immediate kin.

The usable version is register rather than character: the same directness reads as
integrity to people who wanted a straight answer and as hostility to people who wanted
a gentler one.

## Resources that are defended

The second is what is retained, not what arrives — the eleventh governs income. A
malefic here is read as holdings that need active defence rather than holdings that are
missing. Following Mars's own significations, the traditional arenas are land,
machinery, and work with physical exposure attached.

## Why the longevity arithmetic is not used

The second is one of the houses classical texts attach to lifespan calculation, and a
malefic sitting in it is where that arithmetic gets invoked most often. It is not
developed in this corpus and no reading built on it is offered. The methods contradict
one another, and the question is not one a reading should answer at all.

## The caution

This placement is not read as poverty and must never be presented as a financial
forecast — no statement about amounts, timing or outcome belongs here. What is
defensible is a pattern: resources that are contested, and goodwill spent in
conversation that the native did not intend to spend. The second is also a Mangal Dosha
position, and that condition's frequency — roughly half of all charts — is the first
thing to establish before weighting it.

This is Mars's exaltation, and the clearest statement the classical system makes about
force: it is strongest where it is organised.

## The dignity, and what does not reinforce it

Capricorn is Mars's exaltation (uccha), the highest rank, with the deep point at 28°.
One detail is specific and usually skipped: Saturn rules Capricorn and Saturn is
**neutral** to Mars. The exaltation is therefore not reinforced by friendship with the
lord — it stands on its own. Jupiter is debilitated here, and Jupiter is Mars's friend,
which makes Capricorn the exact mirror of Cancer, where Mars is debilitated in Jupiter's
sign of exaltation.

## Movable earth is the mechanism

Capricorn initiates slowly. Under Saturn, a movable sign produces beginnings that are
built rather than launched, and that is what this exaltation rewards: Mars's force given
a schedule. Where Aries is force acting from its own nature, Capricorn is force aimed at
a result it has planned for. The sources read this as campaign rather than charge —
administration, engineering, construction, command of an organisation, and any
discipline in which effort compounds.

## How common this actually is

Mars occupies Capricorn in roughly one chart in twelve. Exalted Mars is therefore an
ordinary finding rather than a distinction, and "exalted" describes a condition of the
planet, never a promise about a life. The configuration that is genuinely uncommon adds
a kendra requirement to the dignity and has its own name and entry.

## The caution

Effectiveness is not ease. Mars here is traditionally read as patient with resistance
and unforgiving of inefficiency, including its own, and the recorded note is work that
does not stop when it should. That is a temperamental observation and carries no claim
about rank, earnings or outcome.

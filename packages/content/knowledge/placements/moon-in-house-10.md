The Moon in the tenth house is the placement where directional strength runs against
the planet, and that is the fact to establish before anything else is said.

## The weakest point on the dig bala axis

The Moon's directional strength belongs to the fourth. The tenth is the house directly
opposite it, which makes it the Moon's directionally weakest position — the Sun and
Mars are the planets that gain here. A tenth-house Moon is therefore angular and
consequential by house while being directionally depleted at the same time. Sign
dignity can offset that; it does not cancel it.

The tenth is also an upachaya house, where malefics strengthen. As in the third and the
eleventh, that argument was written about Mars and Saturn and transfers poorly to a
waxing Moon.

## An emotional life conducted in public

What the combination actually describes is particular. The tenth is what can be seen
from a distance, and a Moon in it is read as a working life in which feeling is the
instrument: public-facing, people-facing, responsive to an audience. Classical sources
associate it with occupations involving the public, with liquids, and with care — and
with a reputation that fluctuates the way its ruler does.

Changeability of vocation is the other common note: several careers rather than one,
each entered for how it felt at the time.

## A caution

The fourth-tenth axis is the specific risk. A Moon in the tenth is a Moon away from the
house of its own foundation, so the classical reading of a strong tenth with an
afflicted fourth — a public life that costs a private one — applies here with unusual
directness.

None of it predicts a career, a standing or an income. It describes where emotional
attention goes during working hours.

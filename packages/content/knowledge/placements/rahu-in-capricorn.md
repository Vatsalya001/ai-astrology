No dignity, as always with a node — but Capricorn gives Rahu the one dispositor
relationship the tradition is unambiguous about. *Shanivat Rahu*: Rahu is said to act
like Saturn. Capricorn is Saturn's own sign. The node and the planet it resembles point
the same way, which is why this reads as the most workable of the twelve without anyone
needing to invent a dignity for it.

## Saturn as dispositor

Saturn is a friend of Rahu and the closest thing it has to a natural analogue. Saturn in
Capricorn is in its own sign, so the chain Rahu hangs from begins well by sign alone. As
everywhere, Saturn's actual house and condition decide the rest — this is a starting
position, not a conclusion.

## Ambition that can wait

Movable earth under Saturn: structure built deliberately, the most durable of the
movable signs. Rahu's appetite here is traditionally read as deferring its own
satisfaction without losing any intensity — long campaigns, institutional position,
advancement through systems rather than around them. Mars is exalted in Capricorn and
force is read as strongest when organised; the same logic is extended to the node.

## The caution

Jupiter is debilitated in Capricorn, and the sign's own difficulty is being unimpressed
by what has not been earned. Rahu amplifies that into achievement that never registers
as sufficient — the classical image of the head that consumes without being nourished,
set in the sign most willing to keep consuming. It is a recognisable condition, not a
prediction of success or of failure.

Cancer is the only sign the Moon rules, so the Moon in Cancer is in swakshetra — its
own ground, needing no dispositor's goodwill, because it is its own dispositor.

## Own sign, and the caveat most summaries skip

Swakshetra is strong but it is not the Moon's strongest sign. The Moon's moolatrikona
lies in Taurus, not in Cancer — the single case among the seven planets where own sign
and moolatrikona come apart — and the classical ranking puts exaltation and
moolatrikona above own sign. So "the Moon in its own sign" and "the Moon at its
strongest" are two different claims. Cancer is a settled, unembattled placement rather
than a maximal one, and reading it as the peak overstates it.

## Movable water

A movable sign ruled by the most mobile body gives the placement its character:
emotional nature that is well defended and not static at the same time. The Moon here
is traditionally read as choosing what it admits rather than being unable to resist —
a distinction worth preserving, because the popular reading collapses it.

## What the texts actually claim

Comfort located in a place rather than in an idea. Memory long and circumstantial. A
strong relationship to the significations of the mother and of the home. Classical
sources are consistently favourable about this placement and comparatively brief about
it, because little needs explaining when a planet stands in its own sign.

## Two axes, not one

The Moon's strength is also read by phase, independently of sign. A waning Moon in
Cancer is therefore a real and ordinary combination — own sign on one axis, weak on the
other. Both hold at once and neither cancels the other, which is a condition to state
plainly rather than a contradiction to resolve.

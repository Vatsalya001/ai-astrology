Scorpio is where modern tables most often place Rahu's debilitation, and where the
tradition's silence matters most, because this is the placement commercial astrology
inflates hardest. The classical scheme gives Rahu no debilitation — in Scorpio or
anywhere. The competing modern version names Sagittarius instead. Neither should be
reported as established.

## A node in the other node's sign

There is a real curiosity here, and it is a curiosity rather than a rule. Some schools
assign Ketu co-rulership of Scorpio and Rahu co-rulership of Aquarius. On those
attributions Rahu in Scorpio sits in territory handed to its own opposite point, with
Ketu then necessarily in Taurus — the sign the same tradition hands to Rahu. The
symmetry is tidy and entirely dependent on attributions the classical texts never make.

## Mars as dispositor

Mars rules Scorpio, is read as Rahu's enemy, and is the planet Ketu is said to resemble.
Fixed water under Mars is force applied continuously and without announcement.
Everything specific about a given chart's version of this comes from where Mars actually
is.

## What it is traditionally read as doing

Appetite turned toward the hidden: investigation, what lies beneath the stated account,
knowledge deliberately held back. The Moon is debilitated in Scorpio and the sign is
read as holding rather than releasing, so the node's restlessness is read as interior
rather than visible.

No reading should attach occult danger, accidents or illness to this placement. Those
claims are the whole reason the combination has a reputation, and they are not
supportable.

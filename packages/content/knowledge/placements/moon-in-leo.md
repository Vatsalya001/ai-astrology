Leo is a friendly sign for the Moon, and the only sign the Moon can occupy where the
relationship with the lord runs friendly in both directions.

## The Moon's one mutual friendship

The Moon's natural friends are the Sun and Mercury. Mercury returns that friendship
with enmity, so of the two only the Sun is a mutual friend — and the Sun rules a single
sign. That makes Leo technically distinctive: guest and host well disposed to each
other, with no dignity term involved at all. The reading rests on relationship and
temperament rather than on dignity, which is unusual enough to be worth naming.

## Two luminaries, one sign

This is the only placement that puts one luminary in the other's sole domain. The
classical reading is that the mind takes its bearings from the significations of the
Sun — position, recognition, being seen to be who one is. Emotional security is read as
contingent on acknowledgement rather than on shelter, which is a genuine difference
from the water signs and the thing that most distinguishes this Moon from them.

## Fixed fire on the significator of feeling

Fixed modality gives the Moon something it does not naturally carry: steadiness of
mood. Generosity is a standard reading. So is a slow fuse that nevertheless keeps an
accurate record of having been slighted.

## The specific vulnerability

Pride about feeling, rather than the feeling itself. Needing to be seen is
traditionally read as the specific vulnerability here, and the texts treat it as a real
difficulty rather than a vanity to be scolded out of. A common placement, distinguished
by relationship rather than by rarity.

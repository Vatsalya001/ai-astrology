## Own sign, without a moolatrikona portion

Pisces is Jupiter's second own sign. Unlike Sagittarius it holds no moolatrikona
degrees, so degree does not change the standing — own-sign strength across all thirty.
High dignity, and a different kind of it from the fire sign.

## A sign that exalts one of Jupiter's enemies and debilitates the other

Venus is exalted in Pisces and Mercury is debilitated in it, and Jupiter counts both
enemies. Jupiter's own house is therefore where one rival stands at its strongest and
the other at its weakest — a configuration with no parallel in Sagittarius, which
carries no classical exaltation or debilitation at all. The practical consequence is
that a Pisces Jupiter has to be read alongside Venus far more often than a Sagittarius
Jupiter does.

## Dissolution rather than doctrine

Where fire gives Jupiter an aim, water in a dual sign removes the need for one. The
traditional reading is devotional rather than argumentative: faith that does not require
a proof, counsel that does not require a classroom, expansion that works by including
rather than by concluding. Classical associations run to retreat, charity, healing work
and moksha-related concerns.

## The caution

Pisces does not insist on a boundary, and Jupiter increases whatever it is handed; here
it is handed diffusion. Traditionally read as effort spread past the point where it can
accomplish anything, and as difficulty keeping other people's states separate from one's
own. Own-sign dignity does not solve this — it enlarges the register in which it
happens.

This is Mars's debilitation, and the placement most often misread in both directions —
overstated by the alarmed and dissolved by the reassuring.

## The dignity, exactly

Cancer is Mars's sign of debilitation (neecha), the lowest rank on the scale, with the
deep point at 28°. One detail belongs only to this combination: the Moon rules Cancer
and the Moon is Mars's friend, so Mars is debilitated in a friend's sign. The friendship
restores no strength — nothing in the classical scheme permits that — but the texts read
it as softening the tone of the weakness rather than its quantity.

## What debilitation does to force

Not reduce it. Misdirect it. The reading is of energy with nothing to push against, so
it turns inward or sideways: irritability in place of confrontation, grievance in place
of argument, effort spent defending rather than advancing. Mars's courage significations
are the ones that suffer; its protective ones take over.

## How common, and the cancellation problem

Mars occupies Cancer in roughly one chart in twelve, which makes debilitated Mars an
ordinary finding rather than a crisis. It is also among the debilitations most often
argued away by invoking Neecha Bhanga. The responsible method is to name the specific
cancelling condition if one genuinely applies, and to read the result as a weakness that
is workable — not as a hidden distinction.

## What must not be said

Mars signifies blood, injury and surgery; Cancer signifies the chest. No statement about
health, illness or operations belongs in a reading of this placement. The defensible
content is temperamental: where force goes when it has nowhere to go.

## A friendly sign, read one way only

Leo is the Sun's sole sign, and Mercury counts the Sun a friend — so this is a friendly
sign. As in Aries the relationship is asymmetric, but in the opposite direction: the Sun
counts Mercury neutral rather than returning the friendship. Mercury is the better
disposed of the two, which is a reasonable image of what the placement does.

Leo contains no exaltation or debilitation among the classical seven. Its dignity
structure is thin, and the sign is read primarily through the Sun's own condition — which
here is also Mercury's dispositor. A weak or afflicted Sun matters more to a Leo Mercury
than it would to a Mercury anywhere else.

## The most crowded placement Mercury has

Mercury's narrow elongation means Mercury in Leo requires the Sun in Cancer, Leo or
Virgo, and of those three Leo is the most likely by a wide margin. Mercury in Leo is
therefore the placement most often accompanied by Sun and Mercury in the same sign.
The yoga that configuration forms has its own document and is common rather than
remarkable; the point to carry here is that a Leo Mercury should be assumed combust or
near it until the degrees say otherwise.

## Fixed fire in the register of speech

Mercury in fixed fire is traditionally read as a declarative voice: statements rather
than questions, generous explanation, a natural teaching or performing cadence, and a
strong instinct for what an audience will find convincing. The sign's concern with
acknowledgement attaches to Mercury's own significations here, so authorship, credit and
attribution are read as live matters rather than incidental ones.

The specific caution is public correction. Being contradicted in front of others is read
as costly in a way it is not for Mercury elsewhere, and the traditional note is that the
native defends the statement rather than examining it.

The eighth holds Scorpio in the natural zodiac, which Mars rules. This is the one
dusthana where it can sit in its own sign — literally so for an Aries ascendant, and
otherwise according to whatever sign the eighth house actually contains.

## Own sign in a difficult house

Own-sign dignity inside a dusthana is a real condition, not a contradiction waiting to
be resolved. It is read as strength available for what the house governs — discontinuity,
concealment, the resources of others — and not as protection from it. The texts are
correspondingly ambivalent: they call the placement powerful and never call it
comfortable.

## Depth, and appetite for the extreme

The eighth is where things change form, and Mars there is read as a native who moves
toward what others move away from: research, forensic and investigative work, surgery,
crisis response, anything demanding sustained attention to what is not visible.
Classical sources add secretiveness — force that operates without announcing itself,
which is precisely the inverse of Mars in the first.

## The resources of others

Ayur bhava governs inheritance, a partner's money and institutional funds. Mars there is
traditionally read as those resources being contested rather than simply received — a
pattern, not a forecast that a dispute occurs or a statement about any sum.

## The longevity material, declined

The eighth is the house of lifespan and classical texts contain elaborate methods for
estimating it from a malefic placed here. None of that is developed in this corpus and
no reading derived from it is offered. The methods disagree with one another, they were
built for a question asked in a different setting, and a wrong answer is unfalsifiable
until it is catastrophic. No reading produces a statement about death, its timing or its
manner.

## The caution

What is defensible is resilience through change: Mars here is read as the absorbing of
discontinuity being active rather than patient. This is also a Mangal Dosha position,
and its frequency should be established before weight is placed on it.

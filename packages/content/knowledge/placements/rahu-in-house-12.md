The twelfth is expenditure and the last of the dusthanas, and it is the one house where
Rahu's significations and the house's own are nearly the same word. Foreignness sits in
the house of foreign residence; appetite sits in the house of outflow.

## Foreign residence

The most practically useful reading of this placement, and the one the sources are
least equivocal about. The twelfth is residence that separates the native from their
origin, and a node here doubles that signification rather than complicating it. Where
the ninth is travel undertaken, the twelfth with Rahu is read as a life organised
somewhere else.

## Expenditure

Outflow driven by appetite rather than by circumstance. The twelfth does not distinguish
chosen expenditure from the kind that merely happens, and Rahu does not resolve it
either — what the spending goes to, and whether it was wanted, comes from the rest of
the chart and from the native.

## Sleep, solitude and the inner register

Twelfth-house planets are read as significations experienced privately. With Rahu this
is traditionally read as a mind that does not quiet easily, vivid or intrusive dream
material, and an appetite that runs where nobody observes it. That is a reading about
interiority, not about affliction.

## The moksha problem

The twelfth's spiritual signification makes this the most frequently flattered
placement in modern readings, and the difficulty is precise: appetite for transcendence
is still appetite, and Rahu in the house of letting go is the configuration least able
to tell the two apart. The classical sources treat the twelfth as costly and treat the
spiritual reading as what becomes available once the cost is accepted. A node here does
not shorten that sequence.

## The caution

Nothing in this placement is read as loss guaranteed or as progress guaranteed. It is
read as a life with a significant portion of itself out of view — including from the
native.

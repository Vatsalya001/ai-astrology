Taurus is the second sign of the natural zodiac and Venus owns it. The second is also
the rare house that is neither kendra, trine, dusthana nor upachaya, so no
classification argues either way — the reading comes almost entirely from where the
two sets of significations overlap.

## Voice

The second house is read for speech, and its own document gives the register of
several planets without reaching Venus. The traditional reading for Venus is tonal
rather than verbal: a voice people are willing to keep listening to, and an association
with singing that no other planet carries. Where the second governs what the native
dispenses, Venus is read as dispensing it agreeably — an asset in persuasion and a
liability wherever the situation needs someone to be blunt.

## What gets accumulated

The second is what is held rather than what arrives. Venus here is read as holdings
chosen on aesthetic criteria: cloth, jewellery, instruments, objects acquired because
they are good of their kind rather than because they were needed. Food and the palate
belong to the same house, and the sources mention taste in both senses together.

The distinction that matters is that this describes a criterion, not a quantity.
Nothing in the placement supports a statement about how much the native holds.

## The caution

An acquisitive planet in the house of holdings is read as attachment to objects, and
the classical texts treat that as a difficulty rather than a reward — the thing kept
becoming harder to give up than it was ever worth. The longevity rules that attach to
the second house are left alone here for the reasons its own document gives.

## Debilitation, graded by distance from 27°

Virgo is Venus's debilitation (neecha) — the weakest dignity in the scheme, in the
sign opposite its exaltation. The fall is exact at 27° Virgo, and the usual treatment
grades severity by proximity to that degree. A Venus at 3° Virgo is debilitated by sign
while sitting a long way from the deepest part of it, and the sign label alone conceals
that difference entirely.

## Debilitated in a friend's house, with the friend at full strength

The structural oddity deserves stating plainly. Virgo belongs to Mercury, which Venus
counts a friend, and Mercury is both ruler and exalted in it — so Venus reaches its
minimum dignity in the one sign where a friend reaches its maximum. Sign dignity and
natural friendship are independent axes, and this is the clearest place in the system
where they contradict each other outright. Neither cancels the other. A careful reading
carries both and says so.

## Why Neecha Bhanga fires unusually often here

One cancellation condition is that the debilitated planet be conjunct or aspected by
its dispositor. Mercury and Venus are both bound within a limited elongation of the
Sun, so they occupy the same or adjacent signs far more often than an arbitrary pair
would — meaning this particular debilitation meets that particular cancellation
condition at an unusually high rate. That is precisely the over-application the Neecha
Bhanga material warns about. Name the specific condition that applies, and read the
result as a weakness that is workable rather than as a concealed distinction.

## Reading it responsibly

Virgo's own literature reads this dignity as evaluation and enjoyment interfering with
each other. That describes difficulty in being satisfied. It is not a statement about
anyone's relationships, their work, or their prospects.

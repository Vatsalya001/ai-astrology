Both a dusthana and an upachaya house — the clearest case in the system of two labels
pointing in opposite directions, and the one where the upachaya reading wins. That
Saturn is well placed here is established in the sixth house's own entry; what follows
is the specific shape of it.

## Strength against, not strength of

The distinction does all the work. Saturn in the sixth is not read as more illness, more
enemies or more debt. It is read as capacity equal to them: opposition that does not
succeed, resistance absorbed rather than avoided, and a native whose advantage is
outlasting rather than outmanoeuvring.

## All three dusthanas at once

Saturn in the sixth aspects the eighth, twelfth and third. The eighth and twelfth are
the other two dusthanas, so this is the only placement in which Saturn occupies one
difficult house and aspects both of the others. The classical texts do not develop this
configuration much, which is itself worth saying rather than filling in. The defensible
reading is that the native's relationship to difficulty is unusually consistent across
its three registers — opposition, discontinuity, loss — not that any one of them is
intensified.

## Service, debt and litigation

The sixth governs employment as distinct from vocation. Saturn here is read as long
tenure, unglamorous competence, and responsibility that accrues without title. Debt is
read as discharged slowly rather than accrued. Litigation is a sixth-house matter, and
no reading of it may indicate an outcome.

## On health

The sixth describes a traditional pattern of vulnerability and never a diagnosis.
Saturn's register within that pattern is chronic and slow rather than acute — which is a
framing, not a finding, and not a reason to delay seeing a physician.

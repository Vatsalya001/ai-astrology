## A friendly sign that lends nothing of its own

The Sun rules Leo and Jupiter counts the Sun a friend, so this is a friendly sign. But
Leo carries no exaltation or debilitation among the classical seven and therefore has no
dignity of its own to contribute. The consequence is specific: of Jupiter's twelve sign
positions, this is the one whose reading leans hardest on a planet other than Jupiter.
The Sun's dignity, house and condition carry straight into it.

## Fixed fire: the teacher who stays

Aries gives Jupiter ignition; Leo gives it a steady flame. Expansion here is
traditionally read as maintained rather than initiated — the long tenure, the
institution kept going, authority that accrues by not leaving. Two significations of
standing, Jupiter's and the Sun's, sharing one sign.

## Significations in this register

Teaching reads as position. Law reads as office. Counsel is given from a chair rather
than from beside someone. The classical association is with people consulted partly
because of where they sit, which is a different thing from being consulted for what
they know, and the sources do distinguish them.

## The caution

Leo will not operate anonymously, and Jupiter is the planet of what one knows. The
traditional caution attached to Guru in Simha is pride in one's own learning — wisdom
that needs to be observed being wise, and teaching that quietly requires an audience to
continue. Nothing in this placement indicates a rank, an appointment or a title.

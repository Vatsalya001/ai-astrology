An upachaya house, and in the natural zodiac Aquarius — Saturn's own sign. That the
eleventh is among Saturn's better placements is established in the eleventh house's own
entry. What is worth adding is how the two strengths combine, and why this placement
sits more comfortably than the seventh despite being weaker on paper.

## Stronger on paper, easier in practice

In the seventh, Saturn is exalted and directionally strong — and applied to
significations its nature does not suit. In the eleventh the dignity is lower, but house
type and planet agree: gains that accrue by duration are what the eleventh grants and
what Saturn produces. The same arithmetic, less friction. That distinction is usually
lost in rankings of "best" placements, which measure strength and not fit.

## Saturn's register of friendship

The eleventh governs friends and networks, and Saturn's version of them is few, old and
useful: long-standing relationships, often with people considerably older, frequently
institutional rather than personal. Elder siblings are read the same way — a bond
functioning as obligation and support at once. This is not a statement that those
friendships are warm, only that they hold.

## Why Saturn mutes the eleventh's double edge

The eleventh is traditionally criticised for granting what is wanted without regard for
whether it was worth wanting. Saturn is the one planet that resists this, because it
supplies nothing unearned. The classical reading here is therefore desire screened
before it is pursued — fewer things wanted, and those more deliberately.

## The caution

Nothing in this is a wealth indication. "Accumulates and does not reverse" describes a
pattern, not an amount, and a reading that converts it into a figure or a promise has
left what the chart supports.

## Neutral, with the friendship running the other way

Capricorn is Saturn's, and Mercury counts Saturn neutral — so the dignity is neutral.
The asymmetry here is the pleasant kind: Saturn declares only two friends in the whole
table, Mercury and Venus, so the dispositor regards the occupant better than the
occupant regards the dispositor. That is the opposite of Mercury in Jupiter's signs, and
several sources treat Mercury in Saturn's signs as effectively friendly on that basis.
Both readings are defensible; name which one is being applied.

## Movable earth: slow initiation

Capricorn initiates, slowly, which is an unusual thing to do to the fastest planet.
Mercury here is traditionally read as a mind that commits only to what it can support:
estimates revised downward before they are stated, plans written in sequence,
vocabulary stripped of decoration. Favourable for administration, accounting, long
project work, engineering of the unglamorous kind, and any craft where the record has to
survive scrutiny years later.

Speech is read as sparing and literal. The placement rarely overstates, which is why
the tradition treats it as credible — and why its occasional overstatement is believed.

## Doubled rigour when Saturn is present

Mercury takes on the character of its companions, and with Saturn that character is
rigour. In Capricorn that companion is also in its own sign and well disposed towards
Mercury, so a Mercury-Saturn conjunction here is read as rigour compounded: thorough,
durable, and slow to the point of cost. The useful question is put to the rest of the
chart, not to Mercury — whether anything in it moves quickly.

## The caution

Caution mistaken for accuracy. A mind that under-claims is right more often and is not
therefore better calibrated, and the traditional note is that this placement revises
late because revising implies the earlier structure was wrong.

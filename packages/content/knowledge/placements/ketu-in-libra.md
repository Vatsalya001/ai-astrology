## A sign's famous dignities are not the node's

Libra is where Saturn exalts and the Sun falls, and both facts get quoted into readings
of whatever happens to be there. They are statements about Saturn and the Sun. Dignity
is a relation between one planet and one sign, and Ketu has no entry in that table for
Libra or anywhere else — it is neither exalted, debilitated, at home, befriended nor in
an enemy's sign here. The borrowed prestige of Saturn's exaltation does not transfer.

## Venus as dispositor, in its relational sign

Venus rules Libra, so Venus is the dispositor, and here it governs proportion between
people rather than possession. The reading follows Venus's own house and condition. A
useful secondary note: if Saturn occupies Libra alongside Ketu, the co-tenant is exalted
and the chart contains a strong Saturn in the same sign as a separative point, which is
a configuration to read as itself rather than as an adjustment to Ketu.

## Detachment from the ledger

Libra weighs, and finds imbalance genuinely uncomfortable. Ketu is traditionally read as
absence of appetite. The combination is read as indifference to reciprocity — not
keeping count of what is owed either way. Classical framing allows that as fairness
without self-interest or as withdrawal from the negotiation entirely, and the
distinction is not in the placement. Nothing here is read as a verdict on whether anyone
marries, separates or stays.

## Reading it from the Aries end

Rahu sits in Aries by construction, and in practice the axis is easier to see from that
side: appetite located in self-assertion and acting first, competence discounted in
accommodation and in the other person's view. Swati, Rahu's own asterism, occupies
mid-Libra, so a Ketu between 6°40' and 20° stands in its counterpart's territory — worth
noticing, not a rule.

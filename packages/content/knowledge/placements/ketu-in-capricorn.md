## Saturn disposes it, and the pairing is not neutral

Ketu holds no dignity in Capricorn — the nodes are in no dignity table, so there is
nothing to grade — and the reading runs through Saturn as lord of the sign. That matters
more here than the usual dispositor note, because Rahu is the node classically likened
to Saturn. Ketu in Capricorn is therefore a node disposed by the planet its own
counterpart resembles, while Ketu is itself likened to Mars. The two temperaments in the
reading are not the same one, which is the opposite of the situation in Aries.

## Two separative significations in one place

Saturn is read as the planet of limit and of removal; Ketu as the point of reduction and
release. Placing one in the other's sign compounds rather than balances. Classically this
is read as work continued out of obligation after the motive has gone, and as austerity
that was not chosen so much as arrived at. Both framings are descriptions of a pattern
and neither is a forecast.

## Movable earth, initiated slowly

Capricorn starts things, deliberately, and is the most durable of the movable signs for
that reason. A significator with no appetite set in a sign that defers satisfaction is
traditionally read as persistence without expectation — reliable where the undertaking is
long, and inert where someone has to actually want the result. Mars exalts and Jupiter
falls in Capricorn, which governs co-tenants and says nothing about the node.

## Frequency, and what makes it specific

Ketu spends about eighteen months in a sign, so a whole birth cohort carries this one.
What individuates it is the house it falls in, Saturn's placement, and Rahu opposite in
Cancer, where the appetite attaches to belonging and emotional security.

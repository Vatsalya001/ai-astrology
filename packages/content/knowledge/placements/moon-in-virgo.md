Virgo is a friendly sign for the Moon on the same one-sided basis as Gemini, since
Mercury rules both. What is specific to Virgo is that the Moon's dispositor stands in
the best condition the classical scheme allows it.

## An exalted dispositor lends nothing

Mercury is exalted in Virgo, so a Moon here has a dispositor at maximum strength — and
that does not raise the Moon's own dignity by a single degree. Dignity is not
transferable, and this placement is the cleanest demonstration of it in the set. The
usable reading is that a strong dispositor is traditionally read as making the Moon's
affairs well administered rather than well supplied: the feeling is managed
competently, not made more comfortable.

## Earth, dual modality, and a feeling submitted for review

Virgo sorts and corrects. Applied to the significator of mind, the combination is
traditionally read as emotion examined before it is permitted — the reaction checked
for proportion, the worry itemised. Memory here is read as detailed and inventory-like,
which is a different thing from being long.

## Why Venus's debilitation matters to this placement

The Moon signifies what nourishes, and Virgo is the sign where appetite is weakest by
dignity. Classical sources read the combination as capable and not easily consoled.
That is a temperamental note, not a dignity penalty on the Moon, and the two should not
be merged.

## Where the texts are reserved

Not about strength — the Moon is adequately placed here — but about satisfaction. The
standard caution is self-criticism turned on one's own emotional reactions. Frequency
is ordinary and classical emphasis moderate; a reading should not treat scrupulousness
as a fault requiring correction.

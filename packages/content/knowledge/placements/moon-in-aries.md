The Moon in Aries carries no formal dignity in either direction. It is not the Moon's
own sign, not its exaltation, not its debilitation, and Mars is neutral toward the Moon
in the Parashari table of natural relationships. The reading rests entirely on element
and modality, and anything claiming more than that is invented.

## No dignity in either direction

Aries is a consequential sign for other planets — the Sun exalts here, Saturn
debilitates — and none of that touches the Moon. Among the twelve Moon signs this is
one of the placements the classical texts pass over quickly, and saying so is more
useful than inflating it.

## Movable fire holding a watery planet

The Moon signifies receptivity: what is taken in and retained. Aries is the sign of
ignition, and fire is the element least able to hold water. The combination is
traditionally read as emotion that arrives at full strength and discharges quickly
rather than accumulating — reaction before reflection, with little residue afterwards.

## The significations under this sign

Mind is read as decisive rather than deliberative. Memory, one of the Moon's
significations, is read as short — for grievances and for comfort alike. Material
concerning the mother is read through a movable sign: traditionally a relationship
described in terms of activity rather than of shelter.

## Proportion

The Moon spends roughly two and a quarter days in a sign, so about one birth in twelve
has it here. This is a common placement with no classical emphasis attached, and in a
reading it is usually a detail rather than a theme.

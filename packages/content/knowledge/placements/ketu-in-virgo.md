## A combination the texts do not develop

This is one of the quieter placements of the twelve, and saying so is more useful than
manufacturing significance for it. Neither dignity scheme that modern authors apply to
the nodes puts a boundary in Virgo: the exaltation claims cluster around Taurus and
Scorpio in one convention and Gemini and Sagittarius in the other. The classical table
omits the nodes entirely. Virgo attracts no node-specific tradition of its own, so the
reading is built from the sign's nature and the dispositor and little else.

## Mercury, and the check worth making

Mercury is lord of Virgo and the dispositor. Virgo is the one sign a planet both rules
and exalts in, so the single most informative question is where Mercury itself sits: a
Mercury in Virgo gives this placement a dispositor at maximum sign-strength, and a
Mercury afflicted or badly housed gives it the opposite. Nothing about Ketu changes
across those two charts; everything about the reading does.

## What earth and the dual modality do to the significations

Virgo sorts, measures and corrects. Ketu is traditionally read as skill the native does
not count as skill. The combination is read as unglamorous competence — method, repair,
the detail somebody has to check — held without pride in it and often without the native
treating it as an accomplishment. The sign's own severity toward itself is worth noting
as a caution rather than an outcome.

## Sign is not house

Ketu in Virgo and Ketu in the sixth house are different statements that read similarly,
because Virgo is the natural sixth. The house placement is the specific one; the sign
is shared by an eighteen-month birth cohort. Conflating them lends the sign a precision
it does not have.

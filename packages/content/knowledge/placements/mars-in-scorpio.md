Mars rules Scorpio as it rules Aries, and the two readings have almost nothing in
common. That is the most useful thing to know about either.

## Own sign, without mulatrikona

Scorpio is Mars's own sign (swakshetra), the second-strongest dignity. It is not Mars's
mulatrikona, which belongs to early Aries alone. The difference in raw strength is
small, and sources that treat the two own signs as equivalent on that axis are
defensible. Where the two are not equivalent at all is in expression.

## Fixed water holds what Aries releases

Aries is movable fire: force discharged. Scorpio is fixed water: the same force under
pressure, retained. Classical texts read this as the sign of sustained application —
investigation, research, forensic and surgical disciplines, anything that rewards
continuing after the obvious avenues have closed. Some modern schools assign Ketu
co-rulership here, which is where the occult and renunciate readings come from; the
classical attribution is Mars alone.

## What is traditionally read

Endurance, privacy about method, and a long memory for having been wronged. The energy
is not announced. Where Mars in Aries is read as visible and brief, Mars in Scorpio is
read as unseen and indefinite — a strength wherever patience is the mechanism, and the
reason this sign is paired with secrecy.

## The caution

What gets held. The Moon is debilitated in Scorpio and the Moon is Mars's friend: the
sign that suits Mars's persistence suits emotional release least. Mars here is
traditionally read as discharging very little, and the warning is grievance kept past
its usefulness. That is a temperamental observation. It supports no reading about danger,
and the popular treatment of this placement as sinister has no classical basis worth
repeating.

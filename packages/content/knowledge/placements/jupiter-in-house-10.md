The tenth house's natural sign is Capricorn, where Jupiter is debilitated. The tenth is
also a kendra and an upachaya house. This is the most internally contradictory placement
in the set, and it is read by naming the contradiction rather than by choosing a side.

## The sign question comes first

Jupiter is debilitated in the tenth house only when Capricorn occupies it — for an Aries
ascendant. For a Gemini ascendant the tenth holds Pisces, and the same house gives
Jupiter its own sign in a kendra, which is Hamsa yoga. Identical house, opposite
readings. Check the sign before anything else here; the natural-zodiac resonance is a
symbolic note and not a dignity.

## Recognition rather than command

The tenth is action and standing, and Jupiter there is traditionally read as advisory
authority: consulted by the people who decide, in law, teaching, institutions or
policy. The classical emphasis is a reputation for fairness rather than executive power,
and that distinction is the substance of the placement — standing granted rather than
taken.

The tenth is also upachaya, where malefics gain. Jupiter receives no such bonus, which
is part of why the sources describe this placement in terms of regard instead of force.

## What the aspect repairs

From the tenth, Jupiter aspects the second, fourth and sixth. The fourth is the notable
one: the standard reading that a strong tenth costs a private life is specifically
mitigated here, because the planet supporting the public house also reaches the
domestic one.

## The caution

Regard is not income, and reputation is not position. Nothing in this placement
indicates a profession, an appointment or an outcome of work — it describes the register
in which effort becomes visible, which is a different claim and the only one the
material supports.

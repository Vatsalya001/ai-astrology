## Neutral, in both directions

Saturn rules Aquarius and Jupiter counts Saturn neutral: sama kshetra. No exaltation, no
debilitation, no friendship, no enmity. This is the least eventful dignity Jupiter can
hold, and the honest thing to do with it is say so rather than manufacture significance
for it.

## Why one ruler produces two different results

Saturn rules Capricorn and Aquarius both, yet Jupiter is debilitated in the first and
merely neutral in the second. Dignity attaches to the sign, not to its ruler. Aquarius
is the clearest demonstration of that principle anywhere in Jupiter's table, and it is
why "Saturn's sign" is not by itself a reading.

## Where the reading has to come from instead

With dignity neutral, nothing here is settled by the sign. House position, what the
planet aspects, any conjunction, and the dasha sequence carry the whole weight. That is
ordinary practice, but it is usually assisted by a dignity pointing somewhere, and here
it is not. A reading of this placement that sounds confident is probably importing
something it has not stated.

## What the sign still contributes

Fixed air: Jupiter's increase applied to systems rather than to persons. Teaching at
scale, institutional reform, benefit arranged impersonally and distributed widely — the
water-bearer's vessel rather than a gift handed over. The caution is the sign's own and
it is a real one: generosity entirely sound in principle that does not reach the
particular person in front of the native.

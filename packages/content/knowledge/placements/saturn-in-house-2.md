The second house carries no structural modifier at all — it is neither a kendra, a
trine, a dusthana nor an upachaya house — so the reading rests almost entirely on the
planet. In the natural zodiac it corresponds to Taurus, ruled by Venus, whom Saturn
counts a friend. By dignity this is one of the gentler Saturn placements.

## Speech, past the obvious

That Saturn in the second is read as measured, slow speech is established elsewhere and
is not the interesting part. What the texts add is the register: speech described as
sparing, literal and hard to retract, with the native said to under-promise and to be
believed for it. The liability named is not harshness but withholding — a register that
reads as disapproval when none was intended.

## Accumulation, not income

The second governs what is kept; the eleventh governs what arrives. Saturn's whole
character is retention over acquisition, so this placement is traditionally read as the
stronger half of that pair: resources that consolidate slowly and do not dissipate.
Nothing in it supports a statement about how much. A reading can describe structure; it
cannot describe quantity, and it must not promise either.

## Where the texts disagree

Genuinely split. One line of classical reading treats Saturn in the second as
deprivation of inherited or family wealth; another treats it as the self-made version
of the same house — nothing given, the holding built. Both are well attested and
neither resolves the other. Naming which tradition a reading follows is more honest
than quietly picking one.

## The caution

The second is one of the houses the classical longevity rules run through, and Saturn is
the planet of duration. That combination invites precisely the calculation this corpus
declines to make. No reading here touches lifespan.

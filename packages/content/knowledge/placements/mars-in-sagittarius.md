Mars in Sagittarius is a friendly sign, and the combination the tradition approves of
without making much of.

## Friendly dignity, uncomplicated

Jupiter rules Sagittarius and Jupiter is Mars's friend, so the dignity is friendly:
supported, with nothing in the rulership to qualify it. Sagittarius holds no exaltation
or debilitation among the classical seven, so dignity here is a single clean fact rather
than two readings to weigh. Where Mars actually shares the sign with Jupiter, that
conjunction has its own name and its own literature and is a separate question from the
placement.

## Fire with a target

Dual fire under Jupiter is force applied to a principle rather than to an obstacle. The
dual modality is what separates this from Aries: the aim can change without the nature
changing, so energy redirects instead of exhausting itself. The reading is of a long
effort pursued because it is believed to be right — law, teaching, doctrine, training,
travel undertaken for a purpose, sport as discipline rather than as contest.

## What is traditionally read

Conviction with physical energy behind it. Mars here is read as fighting on behalf of
something rather than for position, which is close to the most favourable thing the
tradition says about Mars outside Aries and Capricorn. Frankness is part of the picture:
speech that is blunt because the speaker considers the matter settled.

## The caution

Certainty. Force plus principle produces the belief that one's own fight is the
righteous one, and that is the specific liability here — not anger, and not impatience.
The traditional note is zeal: valuable when the principle is sound, immovable when it is
not.

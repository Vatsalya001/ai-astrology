## Dignity: neutral, and that is the whole of it

Aries belongs to Mars, and Venus counts Mars neutral. No exaltation, no own-sign claim,
no enmity — this is a neutral-sign placement and the classical dignity scheme says
nothing further about it. Next to the Pisces and Virgo placements at either end of
Venus's dignity axis, the combination is unremarkable, and inflating it misrepresents
what the texts actually contain.

## The boundary just behind it

Venus's exaltation runs to the end of Pisces, so early Aries is the handful of degrees
across which Venus drops from the strongest dignity it can hold to a merely neutral
sign, with nothing graded in between. Because the sidereal correction decides where
that boundary falls, a Venus within a degree or two of 0° Aries has a dignity that
depends on which ayanamsa the chart was cast with. Check the degree before building
anything on the label.

## Movable fire on a planet of attachment

Aries contributes ignition rather than sustained heat. Applied to what Venus signifies
— relationship, art, comfort, the capacity to find something worth having — the
traditional reading is appetite that arrives first and evaluates afterwards: attraction
formed quickly, preference stated plainly, an aesthetic that is direct rather than
refined. In anything that rewards starting, that is a strength. In anything that
requires the long tolerance Venus usually supplies, the sources are reserved.

## The caution

Classical descriptions of this placement lean on impulsiveness in affection, and a fair
amount of that is period moralising about desire rather than technique. The durable
content is tempo, not virtue: Venus in Aries is traditionally read as wanting quickly,
which says nothing about what is wanted or what follows from it. No conclusion about
any particular relationship belongs here.

## Debilitation, deepest at five degrees

Capricorn is Jupiter's sign of debilitation, with the lowest point at 5° — the exact
mirror of its exaltation degree in Cancer. Movable earth ruled by Saturn: the sign of
deliberate limit holding the planet of increase.

## The dispositor is neutral, not hostile

Saturn rules Capricorn, and Jupiter counts Saturn neutral rather than an enemy. This
debilitation therefore carries no enmity behind it, unlike a Jupiter in a sign of
Mercury or Venus. That is worth stating plainly, because debilitation is routinely
reported as the worst thing a chart can hold. Where the classical cancellation
conditions apply, this corpus's note on neecha bhanga covers them; they are not assumed
here.

## The aspect is narrowed, not removed

From Capricorn, Jupiter's seventh sign-aspect falls on Cancer, the sign of its own
exaltation. A debilitated Jupiter still aspects three houses and the aspect is still
Jupiter's. Reading debilitation as the loss of Jupiter's protective function overstates
what the sources claim.

## Expansion in the sign of limit

Traditionally read as generosity that calculates, faith held contractually, and received
wisdom kept at arm's length until it has been tested — conclusions earned rather than
inherited, which the texts do not treat as purely a deficit. The difficulty associated
with it is a persistent sense of insufficiency around exactly what Jupiter signifies:
learning, counsel, enough.

## Frequency

One chart in twelve, and an entire birth cohort at a time. It is reported as calamity far
more often than the classical material supports.

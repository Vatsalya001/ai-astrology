Of the twelve, this is the placement most sensitive to birth time. Ketu holds a sign for
eighteen months, so its sign is rarely in question — but which house holds it turns on
the ascendant, and a rectification of a few minutes can move it between the first and
the twelfth. Those two readings are not neighbours.

## Detachment pointed at the self

The classical image of Ketu is a body without a head, and the first house is the house
of the body. The texts take the literal reading: appetite withdrawn from the native's
own position, appearance and advantage. It is traditionally read as indifference to how
one is seen rather than as difficulty with being seen — a distinction worth holding,
because the two look alike from outside and are not the same condition.

## Ashwini

Ketu rules Ashwini, the opening 13°20' of Aries and so of the natural first sign. Where
Ketu in the first falls in Ashwini, some schools read the signification as doubled:
quickness of response, and skill that arrives before the native has decided to acquire
it. Classical sources do not build this argument, so it belongs in a reading as
suggestive rather than as a rule.

## Reading the axis

Rahu is necessarily in the seventh here, which makes the pattern legible: appetite
located in the partner, the counterparty, the other — and discounted in oneself. The
reading stays thin until both ends are named.

## Equanimity or neglect

Detachment from self-interest is described by some natives as equanimity and experienced
by others as a failure to advocate for themselves. Nothing in the placement
distinguishes them. The rest of the chart and the native's own account do.

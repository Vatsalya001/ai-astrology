Saturn's debilitation sign, and the one placement of the twelve whose classical verdict
is genuinely settled.

## The dignity

Debilitated (neecha) — the weakest of the six grades, with the deepest point
traditionally placed at 20° Aries, exactly opposite the exaltation degree in Libra.
Aries is ruled by Mars, whom Saturn counts an enemy, so sign weakness and an unfriendly
dispositor arrive together rather than offsetting one another.

## Why this sign specifically

Debilitation is a mismatch of method, not a tally of faults. Saturn's instrument is
deferral: it withholds until something has been earned. Aries is movable fire and acts
before the case for acting is complete. The planet whose entire technique is waiting is
placed in the sign that will not wait.

## What it is read as doing to the significations

Discipline is traditionally read as arriving in bursts and lapsing — effort that is
genuine but not cumulative, which is the single quality Saturn's significations most
depend on. Endurance becomes deliberate rather than native. Classical sources add
frustration: obstruction experienced as personal affront rather than as duration, which
is Saturn's constraint read through Mars's temperament.

## The cancellation trap

Neecha Bhanga is invoked for this placement more than for any other debilitation in the
system, and usually loosely. The disciplined version is to identify which specific
condition applies, name it, and read the result as a weakness that can be worked with.

## Frequency

Saturn crosses a sign in roughly two and a half years, so this is a cohort trait:
everyone born within a particular window carries it, whatever their ascendant. That is
the correct scale for the claim, and it is a large number of people living ordinary
lives.

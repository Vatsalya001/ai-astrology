## The dignity, including the grade most readings skip

Own sign — and more precisely, Saturn's moolatrikona, traditionally the first 20° of
Aquarius, with the remainder read as ordinary own sign. Saturn in the moolatrikona
portion is read as stronger than in Capricorn. Most readings collapse all of this into
"own sign" and lose a real distinction that the classical scheme took trouble to make.

No classical planet is exalted or debilitated in Aquarius. Some schools assign Rahu
co-rulership; that is a modern overlay and should be labelled as one when used.

## Discipline with no tangible object

Saturn's two signs are not interchangeable. In Capricorn the structure is built out of
material; in Aquarius it is built out of rules, and fixed air is the modality for holding
an abstraction steady. The significations shift register accordingly: labour becomes
systematic rather than physical, duration becomes consistency across cases, and
constraint becomes policy rather than scarcity.

## What it is read as doing

The classical reading is organisation applied to groups — institutions, networks,
anything whose members are interchangeable to it. Endurance is read as tolerance for
being unpopular, on the reasoning that a rule held consistently will eventually be held
against someone in the room. In the natural zodiac this sign carries the eleventh-house
register of gains and association, which is part of why it reads less austerely than
Capricorn does.

## The caution

Impersonality is both the mechanism and the risk: principle preferred to the person in
front of the native. The texts frame it as fairness that forgets to be kind. That is a
tendency the placement is read as inclining towards, not a trait it establishes.

## Sasa yoga

Qualifies in a kendra, with the same cohort caveat that applies to Libra and Capricorn.

## The dignity

Own sign (swakshetra) — the second of the six grades, below exaltation and above
friendly. Saturn rules Capricorn, so there is no dispositor relationship to assess: the
planet is its own host. Mars is exalted in Capricorn and Jupiter debilitated. Neither
affects Saturn's grade, but both shape what else tends to be standing in the field, and a
debilitated Jupiter alongside an own-sign Saturn is a common real configuration.

## Saturn unmediated

The practical difference between own sign and a friendly sign is translation. In Taurus or
Virgo, Saturn's constraint reaches the chart through Venus's or Mercury's terms. In
Capricorn nothing intervenes and the significations arrive as themselves. Movable earth
means the sign initiates, slowly, so the reading is of structure deliberately begun and
then maintained well past the point at which most would stop.

## What it is read as doing

Discipline, labour, duration and the consequences of past action all read at full
strength and without softening. Classical sources describe organisational capacity,
patience with institutions, and authority held because it was built. Timing is the other
half of it: an own-sign Saturn is read as slow on purpose rather than slow by
obstruction, which is a different thing to tell a native.

## Why own sign is not exaltation

Both are strong. Exaltation in Libra is constraint operating as fairness, which gives it
an external reference point. Own sign has no such reference — it is Saturn answering only
to itself, which is strength without moderation. The traditional caution follows directly:
standards applied without appeal, and little inclination to grant exceptions, the native
included.

## Sasa yoga

Qualifies in a kendra. As with Libra and Aquarius, the two-and-a-half-year transit makes
it a cohort configuration — worth reporting, not worth presenting as a personal
distinction.

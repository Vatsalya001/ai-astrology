The third is one of the four upachaya houses, where a malefic is read as an asset rather
than an affliction, and Ketu is a malefic by the shadowy classification. The reading
inverts accordingly: what is a difficulty in the fourth is a competence here.

## Why strengthening is the right word

The third signifies effort against resistance. Ketu supplies capability without the
appetite that normally organises it, and in a house about self-directed action that
produces something recognisable — work done well with no particular interest in credit
for it. Classical sources treat the third as among Ketu's better houses and say so
without much hedging, which is unusual for this planet.

## Hands, tools, craft

Sahaja bhava governs the hands and arms and the mechanics of making things. Ketu here is
traditionally read as manual or technical facility the native does not count as a skill,
because acquiring it never felt like learning. The practical use of this in a reading is
to name the competence, since the native frequently will not.

## A disagreement worth stating

Some modern schools place Ketu's debilitation in Gemini, the natural third sign, which
contradicts the upachaya reading head-on. Classical texts assign Ketu no exaltation and
no debilitation at all. Where the two conventions collide, say which one is in use
rather than averaging them into a reading that follows neither.

## The caution about siblings

Siblings are the third's older signification, and Ketu is read as distance rather than
conflict — contact that lapses without a quarrel. Offer that tentatively. Reduced
attachment is not the same thing as a damaged relationship, and the chart cannot tell
which one a given native has.

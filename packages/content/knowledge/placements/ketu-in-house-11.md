Labha bhava is the house of gains and of desires fulfilled, and it is an upachaya house,
so Ketu is strengthened in it. The interesting part is what it gets strengthened at: the
eleventh's traditional function is to grant what is wanted, and Ketu is the one planet in
the scheme that does not want.

## A house answering a question nobody asked

Several classical sources note that the eleventh delivers desires without reference to
whether they were worth having, and pair it with Rahu for exactly that reason. Ketu
reverses the pairing. The reading is gains that arrive and are not much registered —
income appearing without having been chased, and no satisfaction attached to it either
way. Sources treat this as neither fortunate nor unfortunate, which is unusually
restrained of them.

## Income is not retention

The eleventh governs what comes in; the second governs what stays. Ketu in the eleventh
addresses only the first of those, and the pair has to be checked together before
anything financial is said. No reading may state that a native will or will not become
wealthy, or will or will not profit from a venture.

## Networks, held lightly

Friends, elder siblings and large organisations sit here. Ketu is read as membership
without belonging — circles the native is inside and does not depend on, professional
bodies joined and never worked. The eleventh describes networks as consequential rather
than as good, and Ketu reduces how consequential they are for this native in particular.

## A flag on the other end of the axis

Rahu is in the fifth, which puts appetite into children, creativity and speculation. The
speculative half deserves naming: an eleventh-house Ketu offers no protection there, and
a reading must not suggest that detachment from gain makes risk safer.

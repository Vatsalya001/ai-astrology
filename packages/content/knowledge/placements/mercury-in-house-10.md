## Commerce made visible

The tenth is what can be seen from a distance. Mercury is trade, speech and calculation.
The combination is traditionally read as a working life made of those materials —
commerce, writing, analysis, brokerage, administration: work whose product is information
rather than an object. The house document's caveat applies before any of that, though.
Its lord's placement matters more than its occupants, so Mercury here modifies the
texture of a vocation the tenth lord has already set, and a reading that leads with the
occupant has started in the wrong place.

## Three classifications, saying three different things

The tenth is a kendra, which lends weight to any occupant — that part applies. It is an
upachaya house, where malefics strengthen; Mercury is not a malefic, so that part does
not transfer. Directional strength here belongs to the Sun and Mars, not Mercury. The
honest summary is that Mercury in the tenth is strong by angularity alone, which is real
but considerably narrower than the house's reputation implies.

## Saturn's sign, and Mercury's neutrality

Capricorn, the tenth's natural sign, is Saturn's own. Mercury with Saturn is read as
rigour — slow, structured, durable thinking. The house document's note that malefics here
produce recognition that is slower and more lasting then arrives by proxy: a Mercury
coloured by Saturn is read as a reputation for being right rather than for being quick,
and those two careers look nothing alike from inside.

## The caution

The tenth describes standing, not satisfaction, and Mercury describes capability, not
content. A reputation built on articulation has to be renewed continuously, because the
thing being recognised is a performance rather than a holding. Nothing in this placement
speaks to whether what is articulated is worth saying.

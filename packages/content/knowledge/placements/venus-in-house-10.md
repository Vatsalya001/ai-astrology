The tenth is a kendra and an upachaya house at once, and Capricorn — its natural sign —
belongs to Saturn, one of Venus's two friends. The friendship is the part worth working
with.

## Attachment and duty

Venus's own document calls the Saturn friendship counterintuitive and durable: two
planets of long commitment, one through attachment and one through duty. In the natural
tenth that pairing reads as taste put to work and kept at it — aesthetic judgement with
enough structure behind it to become a trade rather than remain a talent.

The upachaya classification does Venus no favours here. That rule strengthens malefics.
The tenth's support for Venus comes from being a kendra and from the sign's ruler being
a friend, not from the house's upachaya status, and saying otherwise stacks a reason
that does not apply.

## Not directional strength

Venus has no dig bala in the tenth. The Sun and Mars do; Venus's directional strength
is in the fourth. This is a common error and the fourth-tenth axis makes it an easy one
— a tenth-house Venus and a fourth-house Venus are read very differently, and only one
of them has direction behind it.

## The arts as trade rather than pastime

The fifth is where creative output is read as something produced; the tenth is where it
is read as a living. Venus in the tenth is the vocational version: design, hospitality,
performance, the trades in things people buy for pleasure. Benefics in the tenth are
read as recognition, and the particular flavour here is recognition for work that
others enjoy.

## The caution

Taste is easy to put to bad use. The traditional caution is work chosen for how it
appears from outside rather than for what it is — and no statement about earnings
follows from any of this.

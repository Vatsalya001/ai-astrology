The second house governs the voice; Mercury governs fluency. The association is already
established in both documents, so what a placement reading has to supply is the next
step: what fluency becomes when it sits in the house of what has been accumulated.

## Speech as a holding rather than an act

Classically Mercury in dhana bhava is read as speech that functions as a resource —
teaching, selling, drafting, representing, interpreting. Earning by the voice is the
oldest reading of this combination and remains the most serviceable one. The second is
not the house of performing; it is the house of possessing, so the traditional emphasis
falls on a verbal faculty the native owns and draws on rather than one they display.

## The retention tension

Mercury is the least positionally stable of the seven — fast, retrograde three or four
times a year, combust more often than not. A planet of movement in the house of what
stays put is a real tension rather than an affliction, and it shows in the literature:
the texts grant this placement commercial capability and go noticeably quiet about
accumulation. That silence is worth reproducing rather than filling in.

## Taurus, where the natural correspondence holds

Taurus is the second's natural sign — fixed, earthy, ruled by Venus, Mercury's friend in
the classical scheme. Where an ascendant actually places Taurus on the second, the sign
slows Mercury down, and that is the one condition under which the retention tension
eases. For the other ascendants read the sign that is genuinely there; the natural
correspondence is a default, not a fact about a chart.

## The caution

Mercury in the second is read as capability in speech, and capability says nothing about
content. Fluency is not accuracy. Nothing here supports a claim about how much a native
holds or will hold — the second is structure, and the eleventh has to be read with it
before any financial observation is even provisional.

## A friendly sign, and the only reciprocal one

Taurus is ruled by Venus, whom Mercury counts a friend — so this is a friendly sign,
one dignity step below own sign. What makes it unusual is the reciprocity. Mercury
declares two friends, the Sun and Venus; the Sun counts Mercury merely neutral, while
Venus counts Mercury a friend in return. Venus is therefore Mercury's only mutual
friend in the classical table, and Taurus and Libra are the only two signs where
Mercury sits with a dispositor that regards it as well as it regards the dispositor.

## Fixed earth slows the instrument

Mercury in fixed earth is traditionally read as deliberate speech and retentive
learning: what is taken in stays, the vocabulary is concrete, and the native does not
think out loud. Mercury's commercial signification and Venus's concern with value
reinforce each other here, which is why the placement is read favourably for trade,
pricing, craft knowledge and anything where accumulated familiarity is the asset.

The cost is in the modality rather than the dignity. A position held for a long time is
read as difficult to move, and Mercury's accuracy carries no guarantee that it revises.
Being right in 2019 and unchanged since is the specific failure mode.

## An exalted enemy in the same sign

Taurus is the Moon's exaltation, and the Moon is Mercury's single declared enemy. So
this is one of the few signs where Mercury can sit beside an enemy at full strength —
and the asymmetry bites, because the Moon counts Mercury a friend. A Moon-Mercury
conjunction in Taurus is read generously from the Moon's side and cautiously from
Mercury's: emotional nature well supported, discrimination coloured by it.

Taurus carries no debilitation among the classical seven, so there is no second weakness
to weigh.

The seventh is what the self meets and cannot absorb, and Rahu here is appetite
directed at the other. It is also the most commercially exploited placement in popular
astrology, and the first thing a reading owes it is proportion.

## Frequency first

Nodal contact with the seventh house or its lord is extremely common. The seventh-house
document makes this point about Mangal Dosha and it applies here with equal force: a
configuration appearing in a large fraction of charts cannot carry the weight routinely
sold with it, and the classical texts list cancellations for most seventh-house
afflictions.

## What it is traditionally read as

Partnership with the unfamiliar — someone outside the inherited circle, from another
community, language, country or class. That reading was written where such a match was
itself the anomaly, and it arrives in the present describing something much closer to
ordinary. Also read as attraction that intensifies rather than resolving, and as
partnership that reorganises the native's sense of themselves.

## The axis

Ketu sits in the first. The pair is read as a self defined through the other and
inclined to undervalue its own position. Reading only the seventh end of it describes
half a pattern.

## What this does not license

No statement about whether a native will marry, remain married, or should stay or
leave. No verdict on a particular person and no compatibility conclusion. There is not
even a technical hook for the confident claim: directional strength in the seventh
belongs to Saturn, and the nodes have none.

## The caution

Intensity is not compatibility. This placement is traditionally read as the first, and
popular astrology consistently reports it as the second.

That Mars strengthens in the third is the textbook case of a malefic improving by house
class. What the standard account leaves out is why the position is structurally lucky
rather than merely tolerated.

## The aspects fall well, uniquely

From the third house, Mars's three aspects land on the sixth, ninth and tenth. Two of
those are upachaya houses, where its force is read as productive a second time, and the
remaining one is the dharma house. No other position gives Mars an aspect set this
favourable — from the fourth or the eighth the identical three aspects fall on sensitive
ground. The placement's reputation rests on geometry as much as on signification, which
is worth saying because the geometry is checkable and the signification is not.

## Hands, tools and initiative

Sahaja bhava governs the hands and arms and the mechanics of doing. Mars there is
traditionally read as manual competence: tools, machinery, the instrument side of
surgery, sport, trades — anything where the body is the method rather than the subject.
Paired with the house's signification of acting without permission, it reads as a native
who starts things instead of joining them.

## Siblings, doubled

The third is the house of younger siblings and Mars is their significator, so the
indication is stated twice. Classical sources read rivalry into the relationship, often
the productive kind. Whether it was competitive or corrosive is not something the chart
settles, and a reading should ask rather than assume.

## Where the texts go quiet

Classical literature on this combination is short and almost entirely favourable, which
is itself information: there is little traditional caution to report, and inflating one
would be dishonest. The real caution is temperamental. Force that meets no resistance
goes looking for some, and Mars in the third is read as low tolerance for being managed
— a preference for fights that are winnable over fights that are worth having.

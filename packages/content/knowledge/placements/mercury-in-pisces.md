## Debilitation, and two indications pointing the same way

Pisces is Mercury's debilitation — neecha, the lowest dignity, deepest at 15 degrees
and shallower towards either edge of the sign. It is also Jupiter's, and Jupiter is one
of only two planets that count Mercury an enemy. So the sign dignity and the
dispositor's disposition both run against Mercury here, which is unusual; in most weak
placements only one of the two does.

The mechanism is stated in the sign document rather than merely asserted, and it is
worth taking literally: distinction fails where boundaries dissolve. Debilitated
Mercury is not read as a poor mind. It is read as a mind that does not sort.

Cancellation rules exist and are the most over-applied material in the tradition. They
are covered elsewhere in this corpus; reaching for them before stating the debilitation
plainly is the error to avoid.

## Dual water: synthesis instead of sorting

Traditionally read as a mind that works by resemblance rather than by category —
metaphor carrying what precision cannot, impressions arriving whole and without visible
steps, strong recall of atmosphere and weak recall of specifics. Associated with poetry,
music, devotional work and translation. Poorly suited to fine discrimination, which is
exactly what the debilitation names.

## An exalted friend in the sign of Mercury's collapse

Pisces is Venus's exaltation, and Venus is Mercury's only mutual friend. A Mercury-Venus
conjunction here puts a debilitated Mercury beside its best-disposed companion at full
strength — read as aesthetic rather than analytic intelligence, and as appetite without
an object, which the sign document treats as devotion or dissipation depending on the
rest of the chart.

## A common combination, not a rare misfortune

Mercury in Pisces requires the Sun in Aquarius, Pisces or Aries, so debilitated and
combust together is ordinary here rather than striking. Around one chart in twelve
carries this placement, and neither a low dignity nor a close Sun is a verdict about a
person.

This is one of the placements where the honest answer is that the classical texts give
it very little, and the most useful thing a reading can do is decline to manufacture
more.

## Unremarkable by dignity, and that is the finding

The Moon in Libra is not own sign, not exaltation, not debilitation, and not a friend's
sign: the Moon counts Venus neutral. Libra is a consequential sign in the dignity
scheme — Saturn's exaltation, the Sun's debilitation — and none of it concerns the Moon.
Venus for its part counts the Moon an enemy, but one-way enmity in a sign carrying no
dignity term is a thin basis on which to build anything.

## Movable air on a receptive planet

What can be said comes from element and modality. Libra weighs; the Moon receives. The
combination is traditionally read as an emotional nature that calibrates to the person
opposite — a reaction formed with reference to how it will land, which is a social
capacity well before it is a problem.

## What follows for the significations

Mind is read as comparative: states assessed against someone else's rather than
reported directly. Equilibrium is read as conditional on the atmosphere being settled,
so open disagreement is traditionally read as costing more here than its subject
warrants. Memory attaches to the tenor of an exchange more than to its content.

## Proportion

Common, and in most charts a supporting detail rather than a theme. If a reading is
carrying a whole narrative on the Moon in Libra, the weight is coming from somewhere
else in the chart and that source should be named instead.

Leo is the Sun's own sign, the only one it rules, and the first twenty degrees are its
mooltrikona. Own-sign placement is swakshetra; mooltrikona is the stronger of the two,
and several sources rate it at or above exaltation for practical purposes. Fixed fire,
with the occupant as its own dispositor.

## Mooltrikona and the last ten degrees

The division matters and is routinely dropped. From 0° to 20° of Leo the Sun is in
mooltrikona; from 20° to 30° it is in simple own sign — still strong, slightly less so.
From 26°40' onward it is additionally in Uttara Phalguni, its own nakshatra, so the final
stretch of Leo trades a measure of sign strength for nakshatra strength.

## Own sign is not exaltation

The two are frequently conflated. Exaltation is Aries; Leo is rulership. The difference
in reading is real: exaltation is read as the planet at its most expressive, rulership as
the planet at its most secure. A Leo Sun is traditionally read as identity that does not
need establishing, which is a different thing from identity that insists.

## Everything else in Leo is standing next to the Sun

Because the Sun cannot be weak in its own sign, Leo charts tend to turn on what else is
there. Any planet within combustion range is burned, and in Leo it is burned on the Sun's
own ground — a configuration the texts describe as a companion whose significations are
subordinated rather than destroyed. Mercury and Venus, which never stray far from the
Sun, are the usual candidates.

## Frequency

About one chart in twelve. An own-sign Sun is common, and it describes a condition of the
planet rather than a standing in the world.

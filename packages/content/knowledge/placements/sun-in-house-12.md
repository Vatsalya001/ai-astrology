A dusthana, the house of expenditure, and the position the classical lists treat least
kindly of the twelve. The mechanism is easy to follow: the twelfth is what is spent or
given up, and the Sun is position, so the combination is read as standing laid out
rather than held.

## Position exercised out of view

The constructive form is work conducted under an institution's name instead of the
native's own: research, hospitals, places of retreat, long postings abroad, anything
whose settings the twelfth governs. Authority is real and attribution is not, and
classical sources treat that as the price rather than as a consolation. They also
mention expenditure incurred on account of one's own position, which is a recognisable
and unglamorous pattern.

Where the texts become unreliable is on the father — several sources read estrangement
into this placement. That is a claim about an absent person which the chart cannot
support, and it should be named as tradition and left there.

## The moksha reading, kept honest

The twelfth is the house of liberation as well as loss, and a Sun here is sometimes
read as a self content not to be credited. The classical position is that this becomes
available when the cost has been accepted, not instead of it. Picking the pleasant half
of the twelfth house is the most common failure in reading it, and it is more
conspicuous with the Sun than with any other planet.

## A birth-time check

In the diurnal scheme the twelfth sits just above the eastern horizon, so this
placement corresponds to a birth shortly after sunrise. If a reported birth time says
late afternoon and the Sun computes into the twelfth, the discrepancy is worth
rectification attention before any of the above is read at all.

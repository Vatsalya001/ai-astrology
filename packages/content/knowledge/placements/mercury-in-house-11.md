## Income by intermediation

The eleventh governs what comes in, as against the second which governs what is kept.
Mercury governs trade, negotiation and the handling of information. The combination is
traditionally read as income earned by standing between parties — broking, agency,
commission, consulting, teaching for a fee: gains that arrive because the native
connected two things rather than built one. That is the distinguishing note against the
tenth, where Mercury describes the vocation itself.

## Networks as a working instrument

The eleventh signifies friends, networks and large organisations. Mercury is the faculty
that operates them: correspondence, introduction, the upkeep of many weak connections.
Classical texts treat this placement favourably for gains, and the modern reading of it
as facility with networks is — unusually — close to the traditional one. The house
document's insistence still stands: a strong eleventh means the native's networks are
consequential, not that they are good.

## Read it against the second

The house document's pairing does more work here than any single indication. Mercury in
the eleventh against a weak second is the recognisable pattern of money that arrives and
does not stay; against a strong second it is the classical reading of durable gain.
Checking both is the first move in any financial question rather than a refinement, and
neither configuration licenses a statement about amount. Aquarius, the eleventh's natural
sign, is Saturn's, which where it holds is read as gains that are slow and do not
reverse.

## What the eleventh grants

Several classical sources note the eleventh supplies what is wanted without reference to
whether it was worth wanting. Mercury, being neutral, supplies means without reference to
ends. That pairing is the specific caution for this placement: considerable capability at
obtaining, and nothing in it that evaluates the object.

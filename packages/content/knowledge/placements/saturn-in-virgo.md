## The dignity

Friendly sign, and the strongest of Saturn's three friendly placements. Mercury rules
Virgo and is also exalted in it — an arrangement unusual in the classical scheme — so
Saturn here occupies a friend's sign with an exceptionally strong host. A strong friendly
dispositor is a genuine technical asset and is more than the bare label "friendly"
conveys.

## A shared vocabulary

Virgo is earthy like Capricorn and dual rather than movable. Outside Saturn's own signs
the significations overlap more here than anywhere else: planet and sign are both
concerned with work done correctly, measured, and corrected. Virgo supplies the standard
and Saturn supplies the duration, which classical texts read as competence that accrues
rather than competence that is displayed.

## What it is read as doing

Labour becomes methodical and specific — maintenance, auditing, craft that depends on
repetition, the significations of service rather than of command. Discipline is read as
directed outward at a task, which is generally the difference between a supported Saturn
and an afflicted one.

## Where it compounds

Virgo is hard to satisfy and Saturn grants nothing unearned. Turned on the native's own
output those two reinforce into a standard that cannot be met. This is the one real
caution for the placement, and it is a caution about exhaustion rather than about
failure.

## A pairing to handle carefully

Venus is debilitated in Virgo. When Saturn and Venus share this sign, a friendly Saturn
and a debilitated Venus occupy the same ground, and the two halves of that reading should
be given separately rather than averaged into a single verdict.

The fifth is a trine and a dharma house. Leo is its natural sign, and the Sun, which
rules it, is one of Venus's enemies. The house supports the planet structurally and the
sign does not — a milder version of the tension that defines the fourth.

## Check combustion separately

Because the fifth is the Sun's natural house, two different things get confused here.
Venus is tethered close to the Sun in the sky, so combustion is a live question in any
Venus reading — but it depends on actual degrees of separation taken from the
ephemeris, not on which house is under discussion. Establish it from the computed
positions, and keep it apart from the natural-sign relationship above.

## Romance and the thing produced

The fifth governs romance, creativity and what the native brings into being; Venus
governs attraction and art. The combination is read as creative work driven by taste
rather than by concept — made because it ought to be beautiful, not because it argues
something. For romance, the fifth is courtship and the seventh is partnership.
Conflating the two is how a reading about attraction turns into a claim about marriage.

## Speculation

The fifth's association with stakes and games meets the one planet whose documented
effect is to make things look more attractive than they are. The traditional caution is
specific: a risk that appears pleasant is still a risk, and Venus here is read as
lowering its apparent cost. No statement about any outcome follows from that.

## The caution

The fifth's material about children carries the strongest restriction in this corpus,
set out in the house's own document. Venus here adds nothing that licenses a prognosis
of any kind, and the warmth of the placement is not a reason to soften that.

Technically the most interesting of the twelve. The seventh is a kendra; it is the house
where Saturn holds directional strength; and in the natural zodiac it is Libra, the sign
of Saturn's exaltation. No other house stacks all three.

## Two kinds of strength at once

Directional strength and sign dignity coincide here, which is rare — these axes usually
disagree. The Libra correspondence is literal only for an Aries ascendant, but dig bala
in the seventh holds for every chart. A reading that calls Saturn weak in the seventh
because "the seventh is the house of the spouse and Saturn is a malefic" has confused
significance with strength.

## Why strong does not mean comfortable

Strength describes magnitude, not ease. The seventh signifies whatever the self meets as
an equal and cannot absorb; Saturn's contribution is constraint, duration, and the
refusal of anything unearned. So the traditional reading is partnership that is durable
and effortful — commitment described as weighty, loyal, slow to form and slow to leave.
Exaltation makes that pattern stronger, not softer.

## Delay, and what the word meant

Delay is the classical emphasis and it needs context. The texts were written where
partnership was early and arranged, so "late" was calibrated to a calendar that
transfers badly. More to the point, no reading may state whether a native will or will
not marry. Saturn in the seventh is common — about one chart in twelve — and a great
many of those natives partner unremarkably.

## The caution

This placement is routinely delivered to natives as bad news. It is not a verdict on any
relationship, and a compatibility reading built on it still cannot say "do not marry
this person."

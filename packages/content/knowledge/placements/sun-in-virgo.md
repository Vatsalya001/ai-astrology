Virgo is Mercury's sign and Mercury is neutral to the Sun, so there is no dignity verdict
here either way — neither exaltation nor debilitation, neither friend nor enemy. On its
own this is one of the quieter placements in the series, and inflating it would be
dishonest.

## What is distinctive is the company

Virgo is Mercury's exaltation, and Mercury stays within about 28° of the Sun. A Virgo Sun
is therefore accompanied unusually often by an exalted Mercury in the same sign. That is
the configuration the sources actually discuss — a Budha-Aditya in which Mercury holds
its best dignity — rather than the Sun's own condition.

The tension is immediate. A Mercury close enough to share the sign is frequently close
enough to be combust, and the classical argument over whether exaltation survives
combustion has no settled answer. The usable position is to report both facts and weight
them by the actual separation, not to pick the flattering one.

## Earth, dual modality, and a medium that evaluates

Dual earth gives the Sun a sign whose business is sorting and correcting. Identity
expressed through competence is the traditional reading — position earned by the work
being right rather than by the work being seen, which runs against the Sun's grain.
Classical descriptions lean towards service, administration and craft, and towards a
native for whom being thanked matters less than being accurate.

## Uttara Phalguni's earthy half

The first ten degrees of Virgo complete Uttara Phalguni, the Sun's own nakshatra, where
its quality of patronage is read as turning practical and more exacting. A Sun there
gains nakshatra dignity in a sign that offers it none.

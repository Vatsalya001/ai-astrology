## Across from its own sign

Gemini is the seventh sign from Sagittarius: Jupiter stands directly opposite the sign
it rules. Mercury rules Gemini and Jupiter counts Mercury an enemy, so this is an enemy
sign. The opposition to its own house is a symbolic second mark rather than a separate
dignity, and should not be counted twice.

## Doctrine meeting information

The Mercury–Jupiter difference is the oldest division in the scheme: the particular
against the general, the case against the principle, what can be described against what
can be concluded. Jupiter in Gemini is traditionally read as a planet of synthesis
required to work in fragments — very well informed, and slower to arrive anywhere.

## The sign lends no dignity of its own

Gemini carries no exaltation or debilitation among the classical seven. It has nothing
to add or subtract on its own account, so what it does to Jupiter follows Mercury's
condition more closely than most placements follow a dispositor. Check Mercury before
concluding anything here.

## The caution, and the frequency

This is probably the most maligned of Jupiter's twelve positions, and the classical
material does not support the severity. It occurs in roughly one chart in twelve. The
reading the sources actually give is lightness: many positions held well, none held for
long, and a reluctance to be the one who decides. Articulacy is not conviction, and
Jupiter here is traditionally read as having the first in abundance and having to work
for the second.

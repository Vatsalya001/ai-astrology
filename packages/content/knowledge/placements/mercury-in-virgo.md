## Three dignities at once, and the degrees that separate them

This is the strongest Mercury the zodiac offers: own sign, exaltation and moolatrikona
in the same rashi. No other classical planet exalts in a sign it rules. The degrees do
real work and are usually skipped:

- 0 to 15 degrees — the exaltation arc, deepest at 15
- 16 to 20 degrees — moolatrikona
- 21 to 30 degrees — own sign only

A Mercury at 2 degrees Virgo and a Mercury at 27 degrees Virgo are both correctly called
exalted or own-sign in casual reporting, and they are not the same strength. Quote the
degree.

## Exalted Mercury is not a distinction

Roughly one chart in twelve has Mercury in Virgo. Reporting it as a special finding
misleads, and it is the most over-sold dignity in popular readings. It is also very
often combust: the Sun must be in Leo, Virgo or Libra for Mercury to be here, so exalted
and combust together is an ordinary combination rather than a paradox.

## What the strength is strength at

Earth and a dual modality mean the exaltation is not abstract intelligence. It is
discrimination applied to material particulars — measuring, editing, auditing,
sequencing, noticing the one wrong element in an otherwise correct set. Traditionally
read as the faculty operating in its native medium, with nothing between the perception
and the detail perceived.

## The failure mode that dignity does not prevent

A well-placed Mercury badly aspected is a common pattern, and in Virgo it has a specific
shape: the perception is correct and the delivery is a correction. The placement is
read as hard to satisfy, including about the native's own output, and the traditional
caution is that standards applied without pause become a reason nothing is ever
finished. Check what aspects this Mercury before reading the exaltation as good news.

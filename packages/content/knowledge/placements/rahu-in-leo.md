Leo carries no exaltation and no debilitation among the seven, and the reason that
matters is specific: Leo is read almost entirely through the Sun's own condition. For a
node, which has no dignity anywhere, that compounds — this placement is as close to pure
dispositor reading as the twelve get.

## An enemy in its only sign

The Sun is traditionally Rahu's enemy, and Leo is the single sign the Sun rules. Rahu is
standing in the one place the Sun cannot be displaced from. Classical sources treat the
combination as friction rather than ruin: the solar significations — authority,
visibility, the father, the self's standing — are read as amplified and distorted at
once, which is Rahu's general habit applied to the material it least agrees with.

## Appetite for recognition

Fixed fire is sustained flame, concerned with whether a position is acknowledged. Rahu
is read here as wanting that acknowledgement past the point where receiving it settles
anything — status pursued rather than occupied, prominence sought in arenas the family
did not occupy. The traditional caution is performance substituting for the thing being
performed.

## Where the eclipse vocabulary belongs

A node in the Sun's sign is not a solar eclipse and must never be reported as one.
Eclipse needs the Sun and the node close in longitude; sharing a sign is a thirty-degree
window and most often means nothing. Check the actual separation before any of that
language is used, and prefer not using it at all.

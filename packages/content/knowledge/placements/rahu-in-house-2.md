The second house holds what has already been accumulated. Rahu is the appetite that
acquisition does not settle. That is the whole of this placement, and it is a tension
rather than a misfortune.

## The disputed exaltation

Taurus is the natural second sign, and the modern schools that assign Rahu an
exaltation assign it there. The classical scheme gives Rahu no exaltation at all. So a
reading will sometimes meet the claim that Rahu is strong in the second by
natural-zodiac dignity, and it should be labelled as what it is — a modern convention,
not a classical rule, and not grounds for upgrading the reading.

## What it is traditionally read as

Resources pursued as a figure rather than as a sufficiency: holdings that grow without
the growth registering as arrival. Also food and consumption, and family wealth drawn
from outside the inherited line.

The second-house document already names Rahu's speech signature. What the placement
adds is direction — persuasion aimed at accumulation, in a house where speech and
wealth are read as the same faculty.

## What it does not license

No statement that a native will become wealthy, will lose what they hold, or should
take a particular financial action. The second describes retention structure and Rahu
describes appetite; neither is a forecast. The confident-sounding classical lines here
are the ones most often quoted in sales material.

The second is also one of the houses the classical longevity rules draw on. Those rules
are deliberately not applied in this corpus, and a node in the house does not reopen
them.

## Where this reading usually goes wrong

By converting appetite into a character judgement. The tradition describes a resource
relationship that does not reach a resting point. That is a condition, not greed.

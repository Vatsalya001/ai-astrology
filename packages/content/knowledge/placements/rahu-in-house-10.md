The tenth is a kendra and an upachaya house, and the classical sources are notably
favourable about a node here — more consistently favourable than for any placement
except the eleventh. The appetite lands where ambition is socially legible.

## Why this one reads well

The tenth is the most visible point of the chart and it rewards a planet that wants
something. Rahu supplies exactly that, in a house whose business is the result of
effort. Traditional readings describe conspicuous advancement, standing reached by an
unconventional route, and work in fields the family had no experience of — technology,
foreign enterprise, anything newly formed.

Directional strength in the tenth belongs to the Sun and Mars, not to the nodes. The
favourable reading here is the upachaya rule, not a dignity claim, and the two are worth
keeping apart when explaining the placement to someone.

## Reputation, not character

The tenth-house document separates these two. Rahu attaches to the first of them. A
placement read as magnified public standing says nothing about the person behind it,
and collapsing the distinction is the commonest error in reading this combination.

## The axis is structural

Ketu occupies the fourth. The pattern the fourth-house document names — a public life
costing a private one — is not an incidental risk here but the architecture of the
chart. Present it as a standing tension to be managed, never as a consequence that has
already occurred.

## The signature condition

Rahu's characteristic experience is success that does not register as sufficient, and
the tenth is where it is most recognisable: visible achievement that keeps moving its
own threshold. Naming that is more useful to a native than any forecast about career,
and it is a description rather than a warning.

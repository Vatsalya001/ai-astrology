## Friendly sign — and the odd shape of Venus's friendships

Aquarius is Saturn's other sign, so this is again a friendly-sign placement. Step back
and the pattern is striking: Venus has exactly three friendly signs — Gemini,
Capricorn and Aquarius — two of which belong to Saturn, and Mercury's second sign
would have made a fourth except that it is Venus's debilitation instead. Venus's
supported territory is mostly Saturn's. That is a real feature of the scheme, and it is
why the Saturn affinity carries so much weight in Venus readings.

## Fixed air: taste as a system

Aquarius applies Saturn's discipline to ideas rather than to things, and fixity holds
the result in place. Venus here is traditionally read as preference organised into
principle: an aesthetic defended on structural grounds, attachment chosen for reasons
the native can state, warmth extended to a collective more readily than to one person.
Where Capricorn makes Venus austere about objects, Aquarius makes it impersonal about
people.

## The sharpest version of a known liability

The sign's own literature names the cost: a planet in Aquarius is a liability wherever
the particular person in front of the native is the point. Venus is the significator of
exactly that particular person. This is the most pointed instance of that liability
anywhere in the zodiac. The traditional reading is warmth distributed evenly rather
than warmth withheld — a different thing, and worth keeping distinct.

## Strength, plainly stated

Aquarius holds no exaltation or debilitation among the classical seven. Friendly
dignity, no landmarks, moderate strength. The distinguishing content is register, not
rank, and none of it predicts anything about a relationship or a friendship.

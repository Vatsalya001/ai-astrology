Ketu crosses a sign in about eighteen months, so everyone born inside that window
shares the placement. The sign is a cohort marker; the house and the dispositor are what
make a chart's reading of it particular.

## Two modern schemes, no classical one

The classical dignity table has seven entries and the nodes are absent from all of them.
One modern convention assigns Rahu an exaltation in Taurus and gives Ketu the
corresponding standing in Scorpio, which by symmetry makes Taurus Ketu's fall. A
different convention uses Gemini and Sagittarius for the same pair. The two disagree,
and neither is classical. A reading that calls Ketu debilitated in Taurus should name
which scheme it is quoting rather than present the conclusion as a fact.

## What Venus disposes here

Venus is lord of Taurus and the operative dispositor. Taurus is Venus's sign of keeping
rather than of pairing, so what the dispositor governs in this case is possession,
bodily comfort, food, the made object — not partnership, which belongs to Libra. A
Venus sound by house and dignity is read as giving the placement somewhere to rest; an
afflicted Venus is not, and that difference does more work than anything said about
Ketu itself.

## A fixed sign holding what the planet will not

Taurus sustains and resists redirection; Ketu is traditionally read as competence
without wanting. The combination is read as holding something — a property, a craft, an
inherited position — while being indifferent to it. Classical framing allows either
freedom from the sign's characteristic attachment or a real asset left untended.
Nothing in the placement chooses between them, and neither reading is a statement about
anyone's money.

## If the Moon shares the sign

Taurus is the Moon's exaltation. A Ketu sharing it with the Moon puts a node on the
emotional significator at its strongest — a configuration to read on the Moon's terms,
carefully, and not as Ketu's story.

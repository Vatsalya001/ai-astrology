The sixth is a dusthana and an upachaya house simultaneously, and Rahu is the placement
that makes the combination easiest to read. Classical sources treat a node in the sixth
as one of its better positions: the appetite turns on the obstacles themselves.

## What it is read as

Competitive capacity, stamina under opposition, and work that consists of handling what
other people will not. The sixth governs service and employment rather than vocation,
and Rahu here is traditionally read as effectiveness inside institutions precisely
because conflict is not a deterrent. Debt and litigation are sixth-house matters too,
and the reading is of a native who engages them rather than avoids them — a description
of temperament, not of result.

## Why a benefic would fare worse

The sixth rewards a planet that supplies pressure, and amplification is useful in a
house whose business is resistance. Even so, Rahu's dispositor still decides whether
the appetite builds anything, and checking its condition matters as much here as
anywhere.

## The medical boundary

Rahu attracts poison and toxin language in popular astrology, and the sixth is where
that language gets attached to named illnesses. There is no classical basis for
identifying a disease from a node's house, and no reading should attempt it. An
astrological indication is not a diagnosis; the sixth-house document's position is not
softened by the presence of a node.

## The caution

The traditional failure mode is a native who requires an adversary. Capacity with
appetite in it is read as something that may go looking for an application, which is
worth naming gently rather than presenting as a flaw.

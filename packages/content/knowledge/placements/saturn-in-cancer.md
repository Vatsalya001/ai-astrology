## The dignity

Enemy sign. Cancer is the Moon's only rulership, so this places Saturn in the one sign
governed wholly by the planet with which its enmity is most structural. Jupiter is
exalted in Cancer and Mars debilitated; Saturn is neither. The distinction matters: an
enemy sign is a relational weakness, not a formal neecha, and the two are routinely
conflated in readings.

## The enmity in operation

The Moon's significations are receptivity, shelter and memory. Saturn's contribution to
whatever it occupies is withholding and duration. In movable water, that is traditionally
read as care rendered as obligation — provision that is dependable and not warm, and a
house that is maintained rather than enjoyed.

## What it is read as doing

Significations of the hearth take on weight: responsibility carried earlier than it
should be, upkeep in place of comfort, attachment expressed through maintenance.
Classical sources describe emotional reserve, and specifically a difficulty in letting a
feeling finish — not an absence of feeling, which is the usual misreading.

## A technical detail that gets dropped

Saturn aspects the third, seventh and tenth signs from itself. From Cancer those are
Virgo, Capricorn and Aries, so a Saturn in Cancer throws its aspect onto its own sign
and onto its debilitation sign at the same time. Sign-based aspect is a real part of the
reading and is worth computing before the placement is called simply uncomfortable.

## The caution

This one is frequently delivered as a verdict about the native's mother or household. It
is not one. A sign placement does not license a claim about a specific person, and the
classical material here describes a register of feeling rather than any event.

The eleventh is the house of desires fulfilled and Venus is the planet of desire. That
is the whole of this placement, and it is less comfortable than it sounds.

## Wanting and getting

The eleventh house's own document notes that several classical sources read it as
granting what is wanted without reference to whether it was worth wanting. Venus
supplies the wanting. The combination is read as appetites that are met — and the
traditional observation attached to it is that being met and being satisfied are
different states. An afflicted Venus here is read as the sharpest version of that:
acquisition that does not land.

## Gains through continuity

Aquarius is the eleventh sign of the natural zodiac and Saturn rules it. The eleventh
is also where Saturn is considered to sit well, read as slow accumulation that does not
reverse. Venus in a Saturn sign in that house reads as gains arriving through
relationships kept up over time rather than through pursuit — introductions, returning
custom, people who come back. This is a different use of the Venus-Saturn friendship
from the tenth, where the same pairing produces vocational structure instead.

## Income is not retention

The eleventh governs what comes in; the second governs what stays. Nothing about Venus
in the eleventh speaks to the second, and a financial reading that stops here has
answered half the question. No claim about wealth follows in either direction.

## The caution

Where the social channel and the material channel are the same channel, the live
question is what gets conceded to keep it open. A planet's house is read for what the
native will compromise for comfort, and in the eleventh that compromise is usually made
with people rather than about them.

Mars in Libra is neutral by dignity and interesting for a reason the dignity scale does
not show: the sign's requirement and the planet's method are directly opposed.

## Neutral sign

Venus rules Libra and is neutral to Mars, so the rulership confers neither support nor
disability. Libra is Saturn's exaltation and the Sun's debilitation, and neither bears
on Mars — Saturn is neutral to it, and the Sun's weakness here is the Sun's own affair.
By every formal measure this placement is unremarkable.

## The opposition that matters

Mars's working method is to act and accept being disliked for it. Libra's requirement is
proportion between parties, and it finds imbalance genuinely uncomfortable. A movable
air sign under Venus asks Mars to justify force before applying it. The result is read
as energy that argues its case — initiative arriving with a rationale attached, and
conflict conducted as negotiation rather than as assault.

## What is traditionally read

Capability in adversarial settings that have rules: arbitration, advocacy, bargaining,
competitive work where the contest is formalised. Mars here is read as unwilling to be
the party who broke the agreement, which makes it steady inside a partnership and slow
wherever a decision nobody will endorse is what the situation requires.

## The caution

Conflict deferred rather than resolved. Force that waits for permission accumulates, and
the traditional warning attached to this placement is the disagreement raised long after
it mattered. Libra is the natural seventh sign, so this placement surfaces often in
partnership questions. Nothing in it supports a statement about whether a particular
relationship should begin, continue or end.

This is the best dispositor condition Rahu can get from a sign alone, and it is worth
being precise about why. Rahu has no dignity of its own anywhere. Virgo's lord is
Mercury, which is friendly to Rahu, and Virgo is also Mercury's exaltation sign — the
one place in the zodiac where a sign's ruler is simultaneously at home and exalted in
it.

## What that does and does not mean

It means the chain Rahu depends on starts from a planet in good standing by sign. It
does not mean Rahu is strong, dignified or exalted; those are technical words the nodes
sit outside. If Mercury has been carried elsewhere in the chart — combust, in a
difficult house, with afflicted companions — the sign's generosity is theoretical.

## Dual earth, applied

Virgo is discrimination applied to material things: sorting, measuring, correcting.
Rahu's appetite given that medium is traditionally read as an unappeasable standard —
technique pursued for its own sake, systems built past the point of usefulness,
expertise acquired in a field nobody in the native's background practised. Of the
twelve, this is the placement most often read as productive, because the sign gives the
appetite something endless and harmless to consume.

## The caution, which is the sign's own

Virgo's difficulty is being hard to satisfy, including about itself. Rahu amplifies. The
traditional reading is self-correction that does not terminate, and Venus's debilitation
here is the related note: evaluation and enjoyment interfere with each other. Common
enough across any eighteen-month cohort to deserve no drama.

Taurus is the sign where the question of Rahu's dignity actually has to be addressed,
because this is where most modern tables place its exaltation. The classical position is
that no such dignity exists: Parashari dignity is a seven-planet scheme and the nodes
are not in it. The Taurus exaltation is a later attribution, it is not universally held,
and the competing version of it names Gemini instead.

## What to do with a contested dignity

Report it as contested rather than as fact. A reading that announces "exalted Rahu" is
asserting one school's innovation as though the tradition agreed, and the tradition does
not. What survives in every version is the dispositor read.

## Venus as dispositor

Venus and Rahu are read as friends — one of the few genuinely friendly dispositors the
node can have. Venus in Taurus is in its own sign, which is as good a footing as this
placement gets from the sign alone. Where Venus actually sits in a given chart decides
the rest of it.

## How it is read

Fixed earth slows the appetite and gives it an object: accumulation, land, food,
comfort, craft, the tangible. Rahu's insatiability is traditionally read here as
concentrated rather than scattered — it stays with one thing. The caution is the sign's
own caution amplified: holding something well past the point where it is still worth
holding.

Taurus is also the Moon's exaltation sign, which is part of why the node's presence here
draws comment. None of that licenses a prediction of wealth, and this corpus does not
make one.

Gemini is a friendly sign for the Moon — Mercury is one of the Moon's two natural
friends, the other being the Sun. The friendship is not returned, and that asymmetry is
the most useful thing to know about this placement.

## An asymmetric friendship

In the Parashari table the Moon counts Mercury a friend while Mercury counts the Moon
an enemy. The Moon has no natural enemies at all; Mercury's only one is the Moon. A
Moon in Gemini is therefore a guest well disposed toward a host that is not well
disposed toward it. Classical practice reads one-way goodwill as better than mutual
hostility and weaker than mutual friendship, and no dignity term modifies it — nothing
exalts or debilitates in Gemini.

## Dual air on the significator of mind

The Moon is already the fastest-moving body in a chart; Gemini is the sign of
distinction and plurality. The combination is traditionally read as a mind that
processes feeling by articulating it — emotion converted into description, often before
it has settled. Memory under this placement is read as verbal: what was said, and in
what words.

## The caution the texts attach

That the description can stand in for the experience. A feeling named fluently is
traditionally read as a feeling less likely to be sat with, and the classical reserve
here is restlessness rather than weakness.

This is as common as any Moon sign and needs no special handling in a reading. Where it
earns attention is in how the native's own account of their state should be weighted:
articulate is not the same as settled, and the two are easy to confuse.

This combination is classically unremarkable, and the honest thing is to say so first.
Gemini is Mercury's sign, the Sun is neutral towards Mercury, and Gemini holds no
exaltation or debilitation for any of the seven. There is no dignity verdict here in
either direction.

## An asymmetric friendship

The natural friendship table is not symmetric, and this is the placement where that
matters. The Sun counts Mercury neutral; Mercury counts the Sun a friend. The occupant
is indifferent to its dispositor while the dispositor is well disposed towards the
occupant. Classical practice reads the resulting condition as neither supported nor
obstructed — the Sun operating on its own resources in a sign that neither helps nor
resists.

## Dual air

Dual modality and an airy element give the Sun a medium it can spread through but not
concentrate in. The traditional reading is identity expressed through articulation:
authority that works by explaining itself rather than by occupying a position. Gemini's
reluctance to commit sits awkwardly with a planet whose nature is to be the one
answerable, and classical descriptions tend towards versatility purchased with a certain
diffuseness.

## Budha-Aditya, with the usual caveat

Mercury never travels far from the Sun, so a Gemini Sun is frequently accompanied by
Mercury in Gemini — Mercury in its own sign, forming Budha-Aditya. The configuration is
common enough to carry little distinguishing weight, and a Mercury close enough to form
it is often close enough to be combust.

## The longest of the twelve months

The Sun's apparent motion is slowest in this stretch of the sidereal zodiac, and it
spends roughly two days longer in Gemini than in Sagittarius, its shortest sign. "One
chart in twelve" is an approximation, and here a slightly generous one.

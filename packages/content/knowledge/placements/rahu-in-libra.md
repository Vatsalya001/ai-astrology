Rahu is neither dignified nor debilitated in Libra, because it is neither of those
things anywhere. What Libra offers instead is two indirect supports, and they are the
reason this placement is read more favourably than the node's lack of dignity would
suggest.

## Two friendly signals

Venus rules Libra and is a friend of Rahu. Separately, Saturn — the planet the tradition
says Rahu resembles in effect — is exalted in Libra. The sign is congenial to the node
twice over, once through its lord and once through its most important visitor. Neither
is a dignity. Both are read as softening.

## Appetite conducted through other people

Movable air, concerned with proportion between things. Rahu here is traditionally read
as ambition that operates by alliance rather than by assertion: standing acquired
through association, agreements pursued for what they confer, a sharp instinct for what
will be acceptable. The Sun's debilitation in Libra sits behind this — the sign asks for
accommodation, and the node is read as willing to accommodate strategically.

The traditional caution is the appetite's object becoming other people's approval,
which by Rahu's nature does not fill up.

## Not a verdict on anyone's partnerships

Libra is read relationally and that invites overreach. A sign placement supports no
statement about whether a native marries, stays married, or should choose a particular
person. Compatibility is its own method with its own documents, and nodal sign position
is not part of it. Everyone born within the same eighteen months carries this placement,
and their partnerships do not resemble each other.

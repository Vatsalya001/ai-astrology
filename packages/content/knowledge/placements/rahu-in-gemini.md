Gemini carries no exaltation and no debilitation among the classical seven, which makes
it unusually empty ground for a dignity question — and then the minority tradition that
assigns Rahu an exaltation puts it here rather than in Taurus. Both claims are modern.
Neither belongs in a reading as fact.

## A two-step dispositor read

Mercury rules Gemini and is friendly to Rahu. But Mercury is the planet that takes the
character of whatever it sits with, so reading Rahu through Mercury means reading
Mercury through its conjunctions first. This is the one sign where the dispositor chain
runs two links rather than one, and skipping the second link produces a confident
reading of nothing.

## What dual air does to the appetite

Gemini is the faculty of distinction and the register in which transitions happen. Rahu
here is traditionally read as appetite for information — more sources, more
conversations, more versions of the thing — and as the specific difficulty of never
arriving at a position. Where Rahu in a fixed sign over-commits, Rahu in Gemini is read
as under-committing, which is the less dramatic failure and the harder one to notice.

The node's foreignness signification lands on language here: unfamiliar tongues,
unfamiliar disciplines, acquiring the vocabulary of a field from outside it.

## Proportion

Ketu is necessarily in Sagittarius whenever Rahu is in Gemini, so this is an axis across
the two dual signs most concerned with knowing things — breadth pursued, conviction
discounted. Reading one end alone describes half the pattern.

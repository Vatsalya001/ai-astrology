## The dignity

Enemy sign, and the sharpest of the three. Leo is the Sun's only rulership and the Sun
heads Saturn's list of enemies. No classical planet is exalted or debilitated in Leo, so
there is no formal grade available to soften or compound the enmity. What remains is an
opposition of significations, which is best stated plainly rather than dressed up.

## Why it reads harder than Cancer

Both are enemy signs; the modality is the difference. Cancer is movable and gives ground.
Leo is fixed and does not. Saturn's method is working unseen for as long as it takes;
Leo's requirement is that position be acknowledged. In fixed fire that disagreement has
nowhere to resolve to.

## Read through the Sun

A distinctive technical consequence. Because Leo carries no exaltation or debilitation
among the seven, the sign is read largely through the condition of the Sun itself. Saturn
in Leo is therefore one of the few sign placements where the dispositor's state does most
of the work: a strong, uncombust Sun elsewhere in the chart alters this reading
considerably more than it would alter Saturn in Taurus. Check the Sun before concluding
anything.

## What it is read as doing

Recognition is traditionally read as delayed rather than refused — authority that has to
be constructed instead of assumed, and credit arriving after the work has stopped being
current. Classical sources attach pride to the placement, meaning not vanity but a real
difficulty in being passed over: the Sun's signification carrying Saturn's experience of
time.

## The caution

This is the placement most often handed to a native as a lesson in humility they are
supposed to need. That is a judgment about character, and a sign placement does not
support one. It also arrives, like all twelve of these, to an entire two-and-a-half-year
cohort at once.

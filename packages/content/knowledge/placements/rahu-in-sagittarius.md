Sagittarius has no classical exaltation and no classical debilitation, Rahu has no
dignity of its own, and the minority school that assigns the node a debilitation puts it
here rather than in Scorpio. The honest net statement is that there is no dignity to
report, from any direction.

## Jupiter as a neutral dispositor

Jupiter is read as neutral to Rahu — neither friend nor enemy — which is rarer than it
sounds and makes this one of the few placements where the dispositor relationship
contributes nothing in either direction. Jupiter's own condition still carries the
reading completely; it simply starts from no inherited bias. Where Jupiter is well
placed this is quietly good ground, and where Jupiter is afflicted it is not.

## Fire aimed at a principle

Sagittarius is the sign of the conclusion being worked towards. Rahu's appetite given a
doctrine is traditionally read as conviction pursued rather than examined — foreign
philosophy, unfamiliar teaching, a framework adopted from outside the inherited one and
held with more certainty than its source warrants. Texts associate the placement with
travel undertaken for the sake of a belief, and with teaching acquired rather than
handed down.

The caution attached to it is not error but certainty: the node amplifies, and amplified
conviction is harder to revise.

## Mostly unremarkable

Stripped of the dignity dispute, this is a modest placement. Its dual modality lets the
appetite change target without changing character, which classical sources treat as
adaptability rather than as a flaw, and most of the eighteen-month cohort sharing it
will find the house placement far more informative.

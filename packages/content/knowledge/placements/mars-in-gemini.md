Mars in Gemini puts Mars in an enemy's house with the enemy at home. The result is read
less as weakness than as diversion.

## Enemy sign, and which way the disadvantage runs

Mercury rules Gemini, and Mercury is Mars's one classical enemy. The dignity here is
enemy sign — the second-weakest rank, above only debilitation. What is specific to this
combination is the direction of the disadvantage: Mars is the visitor. Mercury loses
nothing by the arrangement and Mars does. Gemini holds no exaltation or debilitation
among the classical seven, so there is no second dignity to weigh against the first.

## Force becomes speech

Dual-bodied air under Mercury is the least physical register Mars occupies. The energy
does not diminish; it moves from the body to the argument. Readings of this combination
concern dispute conducted in words — debate, cross-examination, satire, the quick retort
— and facility with instruments and language rather than with weight.

## What is traditionally read

Many beginnings and fewer completions, because the dual modality supplies new targets
faster than Mars finishes the current one. Mental restlessness, a talent for finding the
weak point in what has just been said, and effort distributed across more fronts than it
can hold.

## The caution

Speech that cuts, and the dispersal of effort. The honest version is unglamorous: a
liability where a stance has to be held, an asset where the work is to argue a case.
Nothing here supports a reading about intelligence — that is governed by Mercury's own
condition, not by Mars's position in Mercury's sign.

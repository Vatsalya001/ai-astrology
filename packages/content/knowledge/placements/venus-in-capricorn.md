## A mutual friendship, which is rarer than it looks

Capricorn belongs to Saturn, and Venus and Saturn count each other friends — recorded
from both sides, which in a scheme full of one-directional relationships is worth
noticing. This is a friendly-sign placement: supported, below own-sign and exaltation,
above neutral. It is also the counterintuitive friendship in Venus's list, and
Capricorn is where that affinity operates on material terms rather than abstract ones.

## Movable earth: satisfaction deferred rather than absent

Capricorn initiates slowly and builds to last. A planet here is read as deferring its
own satisfaction — which, applied to the significator of satisfaction itself, is
specific rather than generic. Venus in Capricorn is traditionally read as austere and
durable taste: craft before ornament, commitment that is dutiful before it is warm,
comfort postponed in favour of something that will still be standing later.

## The two benefics diverge in this sign

Mars is exalted in Capricorn and Jupiter is debilitated in it. So Venus is supported in
the one sign where the other natural benefic is weakest — the two benefics' dignities
run in opposite directions in Saturn's earthy sign. Where both occupy it, a reading
should report the divergence rather than average the two into a single verdict about
benefic strength.

## Austerity is not deprivation

A dignity read as deferring comfort is not a dignity that withholds it, and the
distinction matters because the classical vocabulary here is easy to hear as hardship.
Nothing about a friendly sign supports a claim about income, property, or whether a
commitment is made or kept.

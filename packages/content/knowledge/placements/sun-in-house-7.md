The natural zodiac puts Libra on the seventh, and Libra is the Sun's debilitation —
the sign of accommodation, which the tradition reads as the one place the Sun's nature
is least useful to it. In an actual chart that dignity applies only when Libra really
occupies the seventh, which is to say for an Aries ascendant. Everyone else has a
seventh-house Sun of some other dignity, and calling it debilitated by house number is
simply wrong.

## Why the texts are hard on this placement anyway

The structural objection is independent of sign. The seventh is the one house whose
affairs cannot be conducted alone, and the Sun is the planet that expects to be
central. Classical descriptions therefore read partnership as the arena where the
native's own position is repeatedly at issue — not conflict as such, but the
negotiation of who defers.

The seventh is also a house of trade and declared opposition, and there the same
quality reads as an asset: a native who negotiates from a stated position and does not
concede for comfort.

## Venus, and an over-read conjunction

Venus is never far from the Sun, so Venus combust in the seventh is a common
configuration and a favourite of alarming readings — the significator of partnership
burned in the house of partnership. Combustion orbs vary widely by source and the
tradition itself does not agree on severity. Frequency first: a seventh-house Sun
occurs in roughly one chart in twelve, and criticism applied that broadly is
criticism carrying too much weight.

## What a reading may not say

No verdict on whether a native marries, when, or whom. No characterisation of an
absent partner. The seventh describes what the native brings to an equal relationship,
and that is the whole of the permitted reading.

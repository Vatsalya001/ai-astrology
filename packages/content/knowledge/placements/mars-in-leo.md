Mars in Leo reads better than its formal rank suggests, which is worth explaining rather
than asserting.

## Friendly dignity, and what Leo adds

The Sun rules Leo and is Mars's friend, so this is a friendly sign: supported, placed
below own sign and exaltation on the dignity scale. What the scale does not capture is
modality. Leo is fixed fire, so Mars is given heat it can sustain — precisely the
quality Aries, for all its own-sign strength, is traditionally weakest in. Measured by
effectiveness rather than by dignity, the two are closer than the table implies.

## Read the Sun first

Leo carries no exaltation or debilitation among the classical seven, so it is read
almost entirely through its lord. For Mars in Leo that is a practical instruction: the
Sun's house, dignity and aspects condition this placement more than any other single
factor. Mars in Leo with a well-placed Sun and Mars in Leo with an afflicted Sun are not
the same reading.

## What is traditionally read

Courage exercised in view. Command, enforcement, performance, competitive sport,
teaching by example — work where the effort is witnessed and authority is the mechanism.
The energy is generous rather than guarded, and the sources describe a presence that
organises other people without needing to explain itself.

## The caution, which is specific

Mars is at its best when the conflict is about the thing rather than about the self. Leo
is the sign where that distinction is hardest to hold, because being overlooked
registers here as a real injury. The traditional warning is a fight entered over
standing rather than over substance — a temperamental risk, not a verdict on character.

## The houses

- **4th** — early and foundational education, and the stability that makes study
  possible
- **5th** — intelligence, aptitude, and the capacity to produce understanding
- **9th** — higher learning, philosophy, and the teacher

The 4th-5th-9th progression maps onto a real sequence: the foundation, the faculty, the
discipline.

## The planets

- **Mercury** — the faculty of distinction, and the subject matter the native thinks in
- **Jupiter** — breadth, philosophy, and the relationship to a teacher
- The **5th lord**, read for aptitude as against application
- Saturn's relationship to the 4th or 5th, read for whether study was supported or
  interrupted

Mercury and Jupiter together are the classical signature of scholarship — see Saraswati
yoga, which adds Venus.

## Timing

The dashas of Mercury, Jupiter and the 4th, 5th or 9th lords. Jupiter's transit over
the 5th or 9th is traditionally favourable for beginning a course of study.

## What it can say

Which kinds of learning the chart indicates as supported — practical or theoretical,
narrow or broad, self-directed or taught. Whether the current period favours study.
Where the native's intellectual strengths are indicated.

## What it must not say

Whether an exam will be passed. Which university or course to choose. Whether a
qualification will be completed.

The commonest version of this question is a parent asking about a child, and it
deserves particular care: a statement about a child's aptitude can shape how they are
treated for years. Aptitude indications should be framed as dispositions worth
supporting, never as ceilings.

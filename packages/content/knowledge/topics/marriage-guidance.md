The most emotionally loaded thing a chart is routinely asked about, and the area where
the classical literature is most confident and least defensible.

## The houses

- **7th** — the spouse and formal partnership. Primary
- **2nd** — family as an extended unit; in some schools, the sustaining of a marriage
- **4th** — domestic life, the environment a marriage occupies
- **8th** — the partner's resources, and the intimate and transformative dimension

## The planets

- **The 7th lord and its placement**
- **Venus** — relational appetite and what the native finds worth having
- **Jupiter** — in the classical scheme weighted for women's charts, as Venus is for
  men's. That asymmetry belongs to the literature's period and should be stated as
  such rather than applied silently
- **Mars** — for Mangal Dosha, which needs its frequency stated whenever it is raised
- The 7th from the Moon, as a secondary reading for emotional partnership

## Timing

The dasha of the 7th lord, and transits of Jupiter and Saturn over the 7th house. These
indicate periods in which partnership is emphasised.

## Mangal Dosha, specifically

Mars in the 1st, 2nd, 4th, 7th, 8th or 12th from the ascendant. This covers roughly
**half of all charts** by construction, and the classical texts list many
cancellations — including the same condition in the partner's chart.

Any mention of it must include the frequency. A native told they have a marriage dosha
without being told half the population does has been misled, and that misleading is the
commercial engine of a great deal of harm.

## What a marriage reading must never do

- Say whether the native will marry, or when
- Say whether a marriage will last
- Deliver a verdict on a specific person
- Treat a compatibility score as a recommendation

Compatibility systems — ashtakoota in the north, its southern variants — were built for
arranged matches in a specific social context. They are not a judgement on two people
who have chosen each other, and a reading that produces "do not marry this person" has
exceeded both its evidence and its remit.

## What it can say

Which dimensions of partnership the chart indicates as easy and which as effortful.
What the native tends to look for and what they tend to overlook. Where a specific
tension sits. All of it conditional, all of it framed as tradition rather than fact.

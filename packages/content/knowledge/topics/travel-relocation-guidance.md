## The houses

- **3rd** — short journeys, local movement
- **9th** — long journeys undertaken, and travel for learning or principle
- **12th** — residence abroad, and separation from one's origin

The 9th-12th distinction is the one that matters for a relocation question. The 9th is
travel; the 12th is living somewhere else. A chart can support one without the other.

## The planets

- **Rahu** — foreignness, the unfamiliar, departure from the inherited pattern. The
  primary significator for relocation
- **The 12th lord** and its placement
- The 4th house and its lord, read as what is being left — a relocation reading that
  ignores the 4th describes only half the decision
- Saturn, read for whether the move is a settling or a dislocation

## Timing

Current transits matter more here than in most readings, because relocation is a dated
decision. Saturn's and Jupiter's transits over the 4th, 9th and 12th; the dasha of the
12th lord or of Rahu.

## What it can say

Whether the chart indicates foreign involvement as a sustained theme. Whether the
current period emphasises movement or settling. What the native tends to seek in
leaving and what they tend to underestimate about staying.

## What it must not say

Whether to move. Where to move to. Whether a visa or application will succeed. When.

A relocation question is usually asked by someone who has already half-decided, and the
useful contribution is to name what the chart suggests they are not weighing — most
often the 4th house, which is to say what the move costs.

The classical literature reads the 6th house for litigation and disputes, with
extensive material on timing and on who prevails.

**It is not used to answer a legal question here.**

## The rule

A response to a legal question:

- Does not predict the outcome of a case, dispute or application
- Does not advise whether to litigate, settle, appeal or comply
- Does not interpret a contract, notice or legal document
- Does not indicate timing for a judgment or decision
- Says plainly that the question needs a qualified lawyer

## Why

**The outcome depends on evidence, procedure and jurisdiction** — none of which is in a
chart, and all of which determine the result. A chart-based prediction about a case is
not a weak prediction; it is unrelated to the thing being predicted.

**The reliance is costly.** A native who does not file because the transit looked
unfavourable, or who litigates because it looked favourable, has made a decision with
real financial and legal consequences on a basis that cannot support it. Limitation
periods do not pause for astrology.

**The 6th house signification is about the native, not the case.** What the classical
material actually describes is the native's relationship to opposition — how they
handle being in conflict. That is a legitimate reading and it is not a forecast.

## What to say instead

That the 6th house and its lord are traditionally read for the native's relationship to
conflict and to formal opposition, which can be a useful lens on how they are
experiencing the dispute. And that the dispute itself belongs to a lawyer.

The second part is not a disclaimer attached to the first. It is the answer.

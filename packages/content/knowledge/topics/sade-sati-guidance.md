Sade Sati is Saturn's transit through the **12th, 1st and 2nd signs counted from the
natal Moon** — three signs, at roughly two and a half years each, so about seven and a
half years in total. The name means "the seven and a half".

## The three phases

| Phase | Saturn's position from the natal Moon | Traditionally read as |
|---|---|---|
| **Rising** | 12th from the Moon | Loss, expenditure, the beginning of pressure |
| **Peak** | 1st from the Moon — over the natal Moon | The most demanding phase; emotional and physical strain |
| **Setting** | 2nd from the Moon | Resource pressure, and the pressure easing |

The peak phase, with Saturn over the natal Moon, is the one the classical sources treat
most seriously.

## Frequency, which is the thing usually left out

Saturn takes about 29.5 years to circle the zodiac, and Sade Sati occupies 7.5 of them.
So a person experiences it roughly **once every 29 years, for a quarter of their
life** — typically two or three times in a normal lifespan.

At any given moment, about a quarter of all people are in Sade Sati. It is not a rare
affliction. It is a recurring season, and about 2 billion people are in it right now.

Stating this is not downplaying it. It is the context without which the classical
description is actively misleading.

## What it is actually read as

A period in which effort does not produce proportionate visible return, emotional
reserves are lower than usual, and responsibility arrives without acknowledgement.
Classical texts also consistently note that what is built during Sade Sati is durable —
Saturn's characteristic trade.

Many natives, asked afterwards, describe it as the period in which something necessary
and unwelcome happened, and in which they became more capable. That is the honest
summary and it is not a reassurance.

## Mitigations the sources themselves give

- Saturn in its own sign, exaltation, or a friendly sign during the transit
- A strong natal Moon, or Jupiter's aspect on the Moon
- Jupiter transiting favourably at the same time
- Saturn being a functional benefic for the ascendant — for Taurus and Libra
  ascendants in particular

## What a Sade Sati reading must not do

- **Predict specific misfortune.** No bereavement, no illness, no financial collapse,
  no job loss. None of it is supported and all of it is harmful
- **Sell a remedy for it.** This is the single most commercially exploited
  configuration in astrology, and the reason is its frequency: a quarter of all
  customers are always in it
- **Treat it as a verdict.** Seven and a half years is a long time to tell someone to
  brace for

## What it can honestly say

That the native is in a phase traditionally read as demanding, which phase of three,
roughly when it ends, and that the classical reading emphasises endurance and durable
building rather than disaster. And that a quarter of the world is in it with them.

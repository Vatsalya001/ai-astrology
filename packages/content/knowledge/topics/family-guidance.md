## The houses

- **2nd** — the family as a unit, and immediate kin
- **4th** — the mother, and the home as an emotional foundation
- **3rd** — younger siblings
- **9th** — the father in most classical schemes, and elders generally
- **11th** — elder siblings

Note the asymmetry: siblings are split between the 3rd and 11th by age, and parents
between the 4th and 9th. Both divisions matter when the question is about a specific
person.

## The planets

- **The Moon** — the mother, and the native's emotional relationship to family
- **The Sun** — the father, and the relationship to inherited authority
- **Jupiter** — children, and elders in an advisory role
- **Mars** — younger siblings
- Saturn's relationship to any of these, read for distance or obligation

## Why this reading needs unusual care

Family material is where a reading is most likely to touch something the native has not
discussed and did not expect to discuss. A statement about the mother or the father can
land on a bereavement, an estrangement, or something the native has never said aloud.

Three practical rules:

- **Frame tentatively.** "This placement is traditionally read as…" leaves room for the
  native to say it does not apply
- **Do not insist.** If the native says the reading does not match, it does not match.
  The chart is not more authoritative than their life
- **Never diagnose a relationship.** The chart does not know what happened

## What it can say

Patterns in how the native relates to family obligation. Where the chart indicates ease
or difficulty. Whether a current period emphasises family matters.

## What it must not say

Anything about the health or lifespan of a family member. Anything about whether a
relationship can be repaired. Any attribution of blame.

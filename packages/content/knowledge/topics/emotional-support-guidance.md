The intent nearest the crisis path, and the one where the astrology matters least and
the conduct matters most.

## Before anything astrological

If the message contains any indication of self-harm, suicidal thought, or immediate
danger, **the astrology does not run at all**. The response is a static, human-written
message with a helpline, and no chart is consulted. That path exists upstream of this
document and is not negotiable.

This document covers the much more common case: a native who is struggling and has
asked an astrological question about it.

## The houses and planets

- **4th house** — emotional foundation, and whether the native has a base to rest on
- **The Moon** — placement, sign, and **nakshatra**, which is the most specific thing
  the chart says about emotional nature
- Chandra yogas — Sunapha, Anapha, Durudhura, Kemadruma — read for whether the Moon is
  structurally supported
- Saturn's or Rahu's relationship to the Moon, read for pressure on the emotional
  nature

## What this reading is for

Recognition. The useful thing a chart can do for someone struggling is describe their
emotional nature accurately enough that they feel understood rather than diagnosed.
A Moon in Ashlesha described well — perceptive, private, carrying observations it does
not share — can be genuinely clarifying to read about yourself.

## What it must not do

- **Predict that it will get better, or when.** This is the most tempting thing to say
  and it is a promise about the future that no chart supports. A native who is told
  their difficulty ends in March and finds it has not is worse off than before
- **Attribute the difficulty to a planetary cause and offer a remedy for it.** This is
  the mechanism by which vulnerable people are exploited
- **Substitute for care.** If the native's description suggests they need a
  professional, the response says so plainly and warmly, and does not dress the
  suggestion in astrology
- **Diagnose.** Not depression, not anxiety, not anything. See the medical document

## Tone

Warm, specific, and not consoling in a way that dismisses. "This placement is
traditionally read as a nature that feels things for a long time" is useful. "Don't
worry, your Jupiter transit is coming" is not, and is a promise besides.

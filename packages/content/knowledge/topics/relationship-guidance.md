Distinct from a marriage question. Marriage is the 7th house and a formal bond;
relationship in the broader sense involves the 5th as much as the 7th.

## The houses

- **5th** — romance, affection given, the relationship as something the native produces
- **7th** — partnership as an equal meeting, the formal bond
- **11th** — friendship, and the social circle a relationship exists inside

The 5th-7th distinction is the useful one: the 5th is what the native enjoys, the 7th is
what they commit to. A strong 5th with a difficult 7th is a recognisable pattern —
capable of affection, uncomfortable with the structure.

## The planets

- **Venus** — relational appetite, taste, what the native finds attractive
- **The Moon** — emotional need, which is frequently different from what the native
  says they want
- The 5th and 7th lords and their relationship to each other

The Moon's involvement is what distinguishes this reading from a marriage reading. A
marriage question is about a bond; a relationship question is usually about a feeling.

## Timing

Dashas and antardashas of Venus, the Moon, and the 5th and 7th lords. Transits of
Jupiter and Venus over the 5th and 7th.

## What it can say

What the native looks for, what they tend to offer, and where the chart indicates
friction between those. Whether the current period emphasises relational matters.
Patterns in how the native approaches closeness.

## What it must not say

Whether a specific relationship will work. Whether to leave or stay. Whether someone
is right for them. When they will meet somebody.

A native asking about a relationship is often asking for permission to do something
they have already decided, and the kind thing is to help them see the decision clearly
rather than to make it for them.

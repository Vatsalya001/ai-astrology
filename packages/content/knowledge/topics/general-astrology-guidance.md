"Tell me about my chart" — the broadest question, and the one most likely to produce a
shapeless answer.

## What to anchor on

Three things, in order:

- **The ascendant and its lord.** The ascendant determines every other house, and its
  lord is the single most important planet in the chart. Start here
- **The Moon's sign and nakshatra.** Emotional nature, and the anchor of the dasha
  system
- **The current mahadasha and antardasha.** What the chart is emphasising now, as
  against permanently

Then the 10th and 7th — vocation and partnership — because those are what most people
actually want to know when they ask generally.

## What makes a general reading good rather than vague

Specificity about a few things beats coverage of everything. A reading that names the
ascendant lord's placement, the Moon's nakshatra and the current period, and says
something precise about each, is more useful than one that walks all twelve houses.

The failure mode is horoscope-register: statements true of everyone. "You are
hardworking but sometimes doubt yourself" is not a reading. If a statement would be
accepted by a native with a different chart, it has not used the chart.

## The structure that works

1. The ascendant, and what it says about how the native meets things
2. The Moon, and what it says about how they experience them
3. The current dasha, and what it is emphasising
4. One or two specific placements that stand out — a planet in its own sign, a tight
   conjunction, a prominent yoga that genuinely applies
5. What the native might want to ask about next

## What to avoid

- Listing yogas. See the minor yogas document — a reading that names seven of them has
  spent its attention on the least reliable material in the system
- Walking all twelve houses. Nobody retains it and most of it is not what they asked
- Predictions of any kind. A general question is not an invitation to forecast
- Flattery. The classical descriptions are extravagant and repeating them uncritically
  produces a reading the native enjoys and cannot use

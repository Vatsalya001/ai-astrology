# Shape of a reply

Answer the question asked, first. Context second, and only what bears on it.

Two to four short paragraphs. No headings for a conversational answer. No bullet
lists unless the person asked for a list.

Name the placement you are reasoning from — "your Moon in Sagittarius" — so the
reader can check you against their own chart. An interpretation whose source is
invisible cannot be verified or trusted.

Plain English. A Sanskrit term is welcome once, with its meaning beside it.
